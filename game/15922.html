Stickman Ghost Online Online

The title screen tells you most of what you need to know. Big green letters spell out Stickman Ghost Online, a spiky-haired ghost warrior stands ready, and an upgrade button sits right there on the main screen. The game behind it is a fighting adventure through a city of enemy stickmen, and the controls are the classic trio: move, jump, attack. What makes this one stand out on mobile is how completely it fills a portrait screen, with music and sound buttons tucked in the corners and everything reachable by thumb. It feels made for phones rather than squeezed onto them. The spooky city setting gives the fighting a backdrop that the bright title screen only hints at, and the description's promise of combos and a boss to reach gives the action a destination beyond the next enemy. What the title screen already proves is intent: this is a game built for phones first, with its buttons, upgrade option and portrait layout all decided before a single fight starts. That care shows in how little setup the game needs.

How to Play

The PC controls are WASD to move, spacebar to jump and click to attack. On mobile, use the on-screen buttons to move, jump and attack. The title screen also shows an upgrade button, so the character can be improved from the main screen.

Tips

  • Use the on-screen buttons deliberately rather than mashing, since attacks land better with timing.
  • Keep moving between attacks so enemy stickmen cannot surround the ghost warrior.
  • Check the upgrade option on the title screen before starting a run.
  • Play in portrait on your phone, which is how the title screen and controls are laid out.
