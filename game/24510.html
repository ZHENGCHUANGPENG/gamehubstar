Steve and Alex Dragon Egg Online

The title screen is a grey scene with the two heroes front and centre. STEVE AND ALEX runs across the top in big white letters with Dragon Egg in gold below it, and on the left stand the two blocky characters, one orange-haired in teal and one dark-haired in green. On the right sit three glowing teal gems and a purple egg shape, and in the middle a big white button with a black play triangle waits to be pressed. The listing frames the job as the last adventure of the pair: take the dragon egg, kill the dragon and escape, while green exploding zombies crowd the way, and the way forward has to be dug out by breaking walls.

The play is a keyboard platformer for one or two players. WASD or the arrow keys move the heroes and a double jump is available, exactly as the instructions list them, and the listing adds the split that makes the pair work: one hero carries a sword and the other a pickaxe, so digging and fighting fall to different hands. Press the play triangle and the adventure starts from that grey title screen, with the gems and the egg shape already hinting at what the run is about.

What works is the clarity of the setup: two heroes, one egg, one dragon, and a title screen that shows all of it at a glance. The downside is the wait before the menu, since a skippable ad runs first and has to be cleared before the title screen can be used, and the game showed only a black screen on phones, so it is a computer game despite the touch control the instructions mention. The title screen is also plain grey behind the heroes, with no level choice on view.

Blocky adventure fans will like the two-hero setup, and a two-player session works well when one digs and the other fights. On a phone it is the wrong choice, and so is any session that must start in seconds or cannot stand a pre-game ad.

How to Play

On a computer, move with WASD or the arrow keys and double jump, as the instructions list them. The instructions also mention mobile touch control, but the game showed a black screen on phones in testing, so it is listed for computers only.
