Watermelon Run Online

Watermelon Run looks like a summer race drawn in bright, simple shapes. The captured shot, taken during a skippable ad break, still reveals the essentials underneath: a blue sky, white clouds, a small green island floating to one side, and a grey track running down the middle of a tall portrait frame. A runner in white with a yellow and orange top stands on the track, a green cylindrical shape sits ahead, and a row of pink round pieces lines the lower part of the course. The official write-up frames the job in plain terms: carry watermelon bricks, find a shortcut on the water, build a bridge, and surpass your opponents. That is a race with a building twist, not just a sprint: the bricks you carry become the way forward. The portrait layout fits a phone held upright, so the track and the sky share the same tall shape, and the colours stay readable even behind the break overlay.

How to Play

On a computer, play with the mouse, clicking on the track area to interact with the run. On a phone, tap the screen and swipe along the track to guide your runner, since the test run responded to taps and a swipe on the course.

Tips

  • Keep your runner on the track line instead of wandering off it, because the shortcut only helps if you stay on course.
  • Pick up the round pieces as you pass them, since carrying bricks is how the bridge gets built.
  • Watch the runners beside you and aim to pass them on the bridge section, where a clean line matters most.
