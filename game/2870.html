Stickman Zombie 3D Online

Stickman Zombie 3D is a third-person shooter set in a stone arena, and the goal is simple: shoot the zombie stickmen and the enemy survivors before they reach you. The start screen shows a 3D building scene with a mobile-controls-off notice and a Connect button, and the footer names the game Stickman Killing Zombie 3D. Once the arena loads, a black stickman stands in the middle of a stone yard with a red health bar and a pizza icon at the top, two kill counters reading zero on the right end of the top bar, a joystick at the bottom left, and jump, shoot and action buttons at the bottom right. The description promises a post-apocalyptic 3D world, physics-based ragdoll deaths, and lots of shooting, and the arena view backs that up.

The controls are keyboard and mouse only. Arrow keys move the stickman, the mouse rotates the view, and the left mouse button or Z fires the weapon. X turns the stickman around 180 degrees in one move, and C triggers the friendly speech line. The start screen states plainly that mobile controls are off, so this is a computer game. The on-screen joystick and buttons are visible in the arena view, but the instructions list keys and mouse as the way to play.

The arena itself is the best part. The stone yard is open enough to move in, the health bar and kill counters are always visible, and the ragdoll zombies make every hit easy to read. The drawback is the start screen, which shows a Connect button and a mobile-controls notice without explaining what Connect does, so the first minute feels unclear. The arena is also one open yard, so the fighting stays in one place rather than moving through different ground.

Good for players who like zombie shooters and 3D stickman action on a computer. It works well for short arena sessions, for anyone who enjoys shooting games with visible kill counts, and for players who are comfortable with arrow-key movement and mouse aiming.

How to Play
