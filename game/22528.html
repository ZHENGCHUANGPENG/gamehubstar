Mr Noob Fighter Online

A stone dungeon wall fills the screen, and three icons sit in the middle: a sword for ATTACK, an arrow for JUMP and a shield for DEFENSE. That is Mr Noob Fighter, a 2D fighting game where a little noob hero has to fight through a labyrinth of dangers and enemies. The trial began with the TUTORIAL screen, with the KUKOGAMES mark at the top, a MORE GAMES button at the lower left and a play button at the lower right. The listing says the little character has to jump, fight and collect coins, that the goal is to move constantly through the obstacles and enemies, and that sharp blades come up to kill instantly if the hero stops moving. The art is pixel-styled, the stone bricks and wooden doors give the dungeon a clear look, and the three icons make the controls readable before the first fight. Playing it is simply this: move, attack, jump, defend, keep going.

How to Play

On a desktop move with the keys, A to attack, S to move and D to defend, according to the posted instructions. On a phone tap the on-screen buttons to attack, move and defend. The screen shows the TUTORIAL with the sword, arrow and shield icons, a MORE GAMES button and a play button, over a stone dungeon wall.

Tips

  • Keep moving, the listing says blades rise if you stop
  • Attack when the enemy is close, the sword is the first button
  • Jump over the low danger, the arrow is the middle button
  • Defend with the shield when the attack comes at you
