Masquerades vs impostors Online

The tutorial map shows two beige rooms side by side on a dark background, a blue masked character in the left room under the words Move with WASD and a red masked character in the right room under Move with Arrow Keys. Blue and red mask icons sit at the top next to a speaker and a pause button, a coin counter reads zero at the top left, and small yellow coins wait in each room. At the bottom, each side gets its own left and right arrow buttons with a blue jump button in the center. The listing describes masked characters escaping a spaceship where only same-color impostors can kill, plus zombies to watch for, and the two-room map with its color split matches that setup. The wide view keeps both rooms visible at once, and the touch buttons put both characters under the thumbs.

How to Play

With a desktop, move the blue character with WASD and the red one with the arrow keys, as the map itself says. On a phone, use the on-screen arrow buttons at the bottom, with the blue jump button in the center. Double jump is available.
