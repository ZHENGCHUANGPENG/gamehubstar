2 Player Among Soccer Online

2 Player Among Soccer wears its joke on its title screen. Two bean-shaped crewmates float against a starry sky, one black and orange with a glowing yellow visor, one purple with a blue tuft, and between them a soccer ball sits inside the title lettering itself. The words 2 Player run in orange above Among Soccer in blue, which tells you the format before any menu appears: this is a duel, not a solo campaign. The developer description backs that up in one line: get ready for challenging soccer matches with Among Soccer, play against others, or challenge your friends in 2 Player mode. That is the whole pitch, and it is enough, because the crewmate look already makes a simple football duel feel playful. The title screen is clean, bright, and easy to read, with no clutter around the two characters and the ball.

How to Play

On a computer, Player 1 jumps with the Up arrow, moves with the Left and Right arrows, kicks with P or the Down arrow, and triggers a super bonus with K. Player 2 jumps with W; check the in-game prompts for the second player remaining keys before a versus match.

Tips

  • Learn Player 1 controls first in a solo round, since the arrow keys plus P and K take a moment to feel natural.
  • Jump to meet high balls instead of waiting under them, because a crewmate who jumps first usually wins the header.
  • Save the super bonus for a moment when you are close to goal, since a bonus wasted from far away rarely scores.
