Kick The Teddy Bear Online

A patched teddy bear sits in the middle of a worn room, and a row of weapons waits at the top of the screen. That is Kick The Teddy Bear, an anti-stress game where the whole point is to let the bear have it. The room is drawn in warm detail: a wooden floor with broken boards, a staircase on the right, cobwebs in the corners and a cactus picture on the wall. The weapon row holds a pistol, a knife, a shuriken, a chair and a rocket, and the listing says more weapons can be bought to use against the puppet. The coin counter reads zero, the green health bar runs along the bottom and a LEVEL 1 badge sits in the corner, so every hit has a number attached to it. The bear itself looks unimpressed, which is the joke. Pick a weapon, click the bear, and watch the bar move.

How to Play

Tap to play, according to the posted note, and a desktop mouse selects a weapon and clicks the bear. The capture shows the bear in the middle of a worn room, a row of weapons at the top with a pistol, a knife, a shuriken, a chair and a rocket, a coin counter at zero, a green health bar and a LEVEL 1 badge.

Tips

  • Try each weapon, they all hit the bear differently
  • The coin counter starts at zero, every hit earns
  • The health bar shows how much the bear can take
  • Level 1 is the start, the room is the whole arena
