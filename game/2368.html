Jewel Aquarium Online

Jewel Aquarium is a match-3 game set under the sea, and the flow to the first board is all laid out on screen. The title shows the name in big orange bubble letters over blue water, a large yellow play button in the middle, and three bubble buttons for information, sound and rankings below. Tapping play opens a level map where level 1 is the only unlocked circle and the rest stay locked with three-star slots beside them. Level 1 shows a goal screen that reads GET: 6000 POINTS, and the board preview behind the flow shows fish and star tiles arranged in a grid. The description calls it a classic turn-based match-3 arcade with lots of challenging levels and modes.

The play is swap and match. Click a fish tile and swap it with a neighbour to line up three or more fish of the same colour, and the matched fish pop for points. Match four or more and the game rewards you with explosive power-ups, and combining fish crafts exploding ones that help crush through blocks standing in the way. The ocean theme runs through the pieces: rare fish and other sea creatures fill the board instead of plain jewels. This one is listed for computers, since the title renders on a phone but the play button did not respond to taps in testing.

The clearest part is the goal. The level map shows exactly which level is open, and the goal screen states the 6000-point target before the board appears, so there is no guessing about what a level wants. The weak point is the wait before the first move: an interstitial ad can appear between the goal screen and the board, so the flow from title to tiles takes longer than the game itself suggests. The board preview also shows the tiles without explaining the blocks, so the first level teaches its obstacles by showing them rather than naming them.

Good for match-3 players who like an ocean theme and clear level goals on a computer. It suits relaxed puzzle sessions, anyone who enjoys swapping tiles for chain reactions, and players who like unlocking levels one by one with a star slot to fill.
