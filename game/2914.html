Bubble Shooter Easter Online

Bubble Shooter Easter keeps its opening simple. The test run reached the start screen, a white panel carrying a cartoon spotted dog with its tongue out and a dark START button below it, framed by a thin brown border. The feed description defines the game behind that screen: a classical bubble shooter with new items and power-ups, where you aim and release the egg to combine it with other similar eggs, and use power-ups to overcome the challenges. That is the familiar bubble-shooter loop, dressed for Easter: point, match the colour, and watch the group pop. The start screen itself is friendly and uncluttered, with one button and one mascot, which suits a puzzle meant for relaxed sessions. It is a gentle, colourful shooter, built for players who like aiming carefully and clearing a board one good shot at a time rather than rushing.

How to Play

On a computer, aim with the mouse and release the egg, as the instructions say mouse or touchscreen play. On mobile, tap and drag on the screen to aim in the same way, then release to shoot.

Tips

  • Aim at a group of matching eggs before shooting, rather than firing at the first cluster you see.
  • Save power-ups for a board that looks stuck, since a power-up clears more when the board is crowded.
  • Look for a shot that drops a whole hanging group, because one good angle can clear more than several direct hits.
