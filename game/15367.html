Chaotic Spin Online

A pink screen, a maroon ring and a single ball circling it. That is the whole of Chaotic Spin at first glance, and the tap is the only control. The ball moves forward and backward around the ring, the white dots sit on the track waiting to be collected, and the score at the top reads zero at the start. The listing says the ball must avoid flying objects and other things on the track, and that some objects rotate, so the timing of each tap matters more than the speed of them. A hit on an object ends the run, which keeps every lap tense even though the screen looks calm. The presentation is flat and bright, the ring is the only shape on screen, and there is nothing to learn beyond the tap. Tap, collect, dodge, and see how long the ring lets you last.

How to Play

Tap on the screen to play, the posted note says. The screen holds the ball on a maroon ring over a pink background, a score of zero at the top and white dots spaced along the track. The same tap is a mouse click on a desktop.

Tips

  • Tap once and watch the ball before tapping again
  • The dots sit on the ring, plan the path to the next one
  • Some objects on the track rotate, give them room
  • A hit ends the run, so slow is better than sorry
