How to Play

Move with AD or the left and right arrow keys, jump with W or the up arrow, and slide down with S or the down arrow, following the key list in the posted note. Press SPACE to play, as the title screen itself instructs. Pick a menu button at the bottom first, STATS, SETTINGS, CHALLENGES or ABILITIES, then press PLAY when ready.

Tips

  • Jump with W over the low traps, since the note lists it beside the move keys.
  • Slide with S under the high obstacles, rather than jumping into them.
  • Move with AD early, because the note frames the run as a constant dodge.
  • Watch the apes behind you, since the evil monkey monsters keep chasing, the feed says so.
