How to Play

Drive with the arrow keys, use F for nitro, the spacebar for the handbrake and C to change the camera, per the posted instruction. The menu shows a yellow supercar on a red-carpet stage with a Play Game button, and the title text above it. A keyboard is required, and the car answers to the keys alone.

Tips

  • Use nitro on the long straights, not on the jumps
  • Tap the handbrake before a tight turn, not during it
  • Stay on the track, falling off the edge ends the run
  • Save the money from each run toward a new supercar
