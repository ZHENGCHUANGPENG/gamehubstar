Woody Block Puzzles Online

A rabbit and a cat, both built from wooden blocks, tell you everything before a single level loads. Woody Block Puzzles is a picture-assembly game wearing lumberjack clothes: fit the blocks, complete the silhouette. The title screen is the thesis — two animal mosaics in warm wood tones flanking Play and More Games buttons — and the LEVELS map extends it, a scatter of locked blue nodes with only level 1 open. An important correction: the old store copy described a 10x10 line-clearing puzzler, and the screenshots flatly contradict it. There is no grid-clearing here; there are animal pictures made of blocks. What the test couldn't show is the actual fitting mechanic — no level was entered — so the moment-to-moment feel remains unverified. But the presentation is coherent and warm, the level-gated structure is clear, and the concept reads instantly. If the block-fitting plays as good as the mosaics look, this is a calm, satisfying puzzler.

How to Play

Tap Play on the title screen, then pick a level node on the map. A sound toggle sits top-right; More Games links out.

Tips

  • - Only level 1 is unlocked — progression opens the map one node at a time.
  • - The rabbit and cat mosaics use blocks of different sizes; plan around the big chunks first.
  • - Study the silhouette edges before placing — the title art suggests picture-completion, not line-clearing.
  • - The wood-grain UI is consistent throughout, so the title screen shows the game's whole aesthetic.
