Protect My Dog Online

The menu is a bright cartoon scene: a blue sky with white clouds, green hills and grey mountains, and a yellow sun in the top left corner. Across the middle runs the title in bubble letters, PROTECT in green and My Dog in yellow, with a small bee sitting by the title, a pencil on the right, and a smiling dog face tucked inside the letter O. Below it sit two buttons, a green one reading PLAY and a yellow one reading LEVEL, with a gear icon in the top left corner for settings. The listing frames the job as protecting the dog from bee attacks by drawing a defensive line, and the pencil by the title and the dog in the logo match that idea at once.

The play is a single gesture. On a computer the mouse draws the defensive line, and on a phone the same line is drawn with a fingertip, exactly as the instructions describe it. Press PLAY to start, draw the line around the dog before the bees arrive, and the line is the shield. The LEVEL button opens the level choice for players who want to pick a stage instead of playing in order, and the gear icon holds the settings. Reaching the clean menu takes a little patience, since the game shows a chain of skippable ads first and each one needs its skip control, but the menu itself is simple once the ads are cleared.

The big win is how clear it all is: one dog, one pencil, one gesture, and a menu that explains itself without a tutorial. The downside is the ad chain, since several skippable ads stand between opening it and the first level, and the first minute is more tapping skip controls than drawing lines. The game is also a gentle puzzle, so players who want fast action or a challenge with a timer will find it too calm.

A good fit for casual puzzle players and draw-to-save fans, and for a short, calm game that can be played with one finger. Skip it if you dislike pre-game ads, want a fast or competitive game, or need a level to start in seconds.

How to Play

On a computer, create a defensive drawing line with the mouse, as the instructions put it: create a drawing line using your mouse. On a phone, draw the same line with a fingertip, the same single gesture the game is built around.

Tips

  • Press the green PLAY button to start, the yellow LEVEL button opens the level choice
  • Draw the line before the bees reach the dog, not after they arrive
  • A closed shape protects better than an open stroke
  • The gear icon at the top left handles the settings before a run
