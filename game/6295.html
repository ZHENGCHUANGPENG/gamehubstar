How to Play

【电脑专属，需键盘鼠标，手机无法操作】On a computer, Player 1 drives with WASD, boosts with F, looks back with T, respawns with R and pulls the handbrake with SPACE. Player 2 uses the arrow keys, boosts with K, looks back with L, respawns with O and brakes with Right-Shift. Pick Race for a contest, Free Drive to explore, or Garage to change cars.

Tips

  • Learn WASD steering on Free Drive first, since the note lists it as Player 1 core set.
  • Save the F nitro for straight stretches, because a boost mid-corner pushes the car wide.
  • Tap SPACE for the handbrake on tight turns, rather than braking late and sliding off.
  • Finish first to earn the reward, since the note ties prizes to first place, not to finishing.
