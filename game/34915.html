Super Zombie Driving Online

A forklift stands on a city street between skyscrapers, and a LEVEL 1 label floats above it. That is Super Zombie Driving, a driving game set in a city overrun by the undead, where the vehicle is the weapon. The street is wide, the buildings are tall, a crosswalk runs across the road and a CONTINUE button waits in the middle of the screen. The feed note describes the job as surviving behind the wheel: choose a vehicle, start the engine, crush through hordes of zombies and complete missions to clean up the streets and earn rewards, with each level asking for a set number of zombies smashed before time runs out. The forklift is an odd hero for a zombie game, and that is part of the charm, a work vehicle doing the smashing. The scene the trial caught is the level start, and it shows the city, the vehicle and the button in one frame. Start the engine, drive through the horde, and beat the clock.

How to Play

WASD drives, SPACE brakes and R restarts, per the instructions posted with the game, and the mouse rotates the view. What appeared was the forklift from behind on a city street with skyscrapers, a LEVEL 1 label and a CONTINUE button.

Tips

  • Drive through the zombies, the street is the weapon
  • SPACE is the brake when a wreck blocks the road
  • R restarts a run that goes wrong
  • Each level sets a number to smash before time runs out
