How to Play

Desktop players solve with the mouse, dragging pieces into place, as the key guide states.

Tips

  • Start with the edges and the red body panels, since the car colour groups the pieces fastest.
  • Use the gravel background as a neutral zone and sort pieces by shade before placing any.
  • Pick the smallest piece count first to learn the picture, then step up to the bigger sets.
