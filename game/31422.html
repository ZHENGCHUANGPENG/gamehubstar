Taxi Driver Las Vegas Online

The menu is a whole garage, not a flat screen. Yellow brick walls, a wooden roof with skylights, shelves of books, a grey sofa, a red toolbox on wheels, a punching bag and a staircase fill the space behind the car. In the middle sits the taxi itself, a bright orange classic coupe on a service ramp, and above it the logo: TAXI in big yellow letters, LAS VEGAS in red below it, DRIVER on a blue ribbon, with a black city skyline along the top. Three buttons sit on the left, Play Taxi, Controls and More Games, a dollar counter reads zero at the top right, a speaker icon and a red volume slider sit at the left edge, and a small version mark reads v 2.1 at the bottom left. The listing describes a simulator set in an open environment where the driver picks up passengers and drives them to their destination while exploring the streets, and the garage is a fitting front door for that job.

Driving uses WASD, with the mouse moving the camera and C switching the camera view, F interacting, and Esc opening the menu. The instructions also list Spacebar to jump and Ctrl to run, which read like on-foot keys carried over from a shared template, so the driving keys are the ones to trust first and the rest can be learned after the first fare. The first thing to do is press Play Taxi, leave the garage, and follow the directions to the next passenger rather than exploring the map at random.

The garage is the best part: it is detailed, the taxi looks the part, and the menu puts every option in plain sight. The flaw is the key list, since the on-foot keys do not fit a driving game and will confuse a first-time player, and the game is keyboard only, so there is no way to play it properly on a phone. The loading also takes a while, with the KIZ10 screen sitting on the bar until the garage appears.

Worth a drive for simulator fans and players who like taxi games on a computer, and for a slow open-world drive with passengers to collect. Skip it for a phone session, for instant arcade racing, or if you dislike reading a key list before driving.

How to Play

On a computer, drive with WASD, move the camera with the mouse, press C for the camera, press F to interact, press Spacebar to jump, hold Ctrl to run, and press Esc for the menu, as the instructions list them.
