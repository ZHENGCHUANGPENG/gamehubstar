How to Play

Arrow keys or WASD move the active stickman, and tapping jump twice mid-air triggers the double jump for taller ledges. On mobile, each side of the screen carries its own arrow cluster and jump button, so both brothers stay within reach. A round swap button sits dead center - one tap hands control to the other stickman instantly, no menus needed.

Tips

  • Send one brother ahead while the other waits on safe ground. The swap button is free to use, so never risk both stickmen on an untested jump.
  • Pick up the floating key before approaching the door. It stays locked until the key is collected, so rushing ahead gains nothing.
  • Collect the green diamonds only when they sit along your path. Backtracking across tricky ledges for one diamond is rarely worth it.
  • Save the double jump for the tallest steps. Its timing is the hardest part of the controls, so test it on safe ground first.
