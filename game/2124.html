Create Your Robot Online

The title screen does all the explaining this game needs. A yellow sunburst fills the background, the words BUILD YOUR ROBOT are spelled out in small robot figures, and a chunky blue robot with a red gauge on its chest stands in the middle. The listing describes a game where you build a robot from given parts, and the title screen matches that idea exactly: the robot is the star, and the parts are the point. One round blue play button sits at the bottom, and two small icons, a gamepad and a speaker, sit in the top corners. Pressing play moves past the title to a loading screen, so the game is quick to enter.

Controls follow the listing, which says to use the mouse. On a computer that means clicking the play button and then clicking to choose parts. On a phone the same actions become taps, and the portrait layout keeps everything inside an upright screen, with the play button placed where a thumb naturally rests. The speaker icon mutes the game from the title screen, and the gamepad icon sits ready in the corner. Nothing on the title screen asks for a keyboard, so the game is a tap-and-click affair from start to finish.

The charm here is the look: the sunburst yellow, the robot-letter title and the chunky blue robot give the game a cheerful toy-box feel that suits its young audience. The downside is how little the game shows before you commit: the title screen says nothing about which parts you get or how the building works, so the first minute is a surprise. The portrait layout is a plus on phones but leaves wide black bars on a computer screen, and the single play button is the only clear way forward from the title.

Kids and casual players who like making things will enjoy putting a robot together. Players who want a puzzle with rules spelled out first should pick a different game.

How to Play

On a computer, click the round blue play button at the bottom of the title screen to enter the game. On a phone, tap the same button; the game is portrait, so it fills an upright screen without turning the device. A gamepad icon in the top-left corner and a speaker icon in the top-right sit on the title screen. The listing says to use the mouse, so clicks are the main way to pick robot parts once inside.
