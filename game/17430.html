A cartoon landscape fills the screen with green trees, a blue lake, rolling hills and a pale sky, and across it sits a grid of green tiles, most of them marked with white question marks, with level one open in the bottom-left corner. A speaker button stands at the top right, and along the bottom sit a back arrow and a gamepad icon. The developer note outlines a physics game where Peppa Pig must be sent to the egg, and the level grid is the way in: pick a tile, solve the puzzle, open the next one. The portrait layout keeps the grid large and the landscape calm behind it, and the single open tile makes the first step obvious. What showed was the grid and the scenery, and the physics puzzle itself sits one tap past the open tile.

How to Play

On a PC, click a level tile, then click to send Peppa toward the egg. On a touchscreen, tap the level tile and tap to play, per the instructions: click or tap to play.
