How to Play

Desktop players drive with the arrow keys or WASD, boost with the Shift and N keys, switch the camera with C, and use the space bar as a handbrake, per the control sheet. A second player mode is also listed, so the full control set belongs on a keyboard.

Tips

  • Use the handbrake to tighten a drift, rather than steering harder through a turn.
  • Save the nitro for a straight stretch, where the boost actually buys distance.
  • Watch the flames and lean of the bikes, because they show when a stunt is working.
