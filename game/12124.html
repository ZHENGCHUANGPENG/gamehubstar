Grand Zombie Swarm Online

Checking Grand Zombie Swarm shows the game at its most direct: the title over a ruined city street, with a crowd of zombies filling the road and broken buildings behind them. The game description explains you play a special forces soldier in an open-world zombie shooting game, using different weapons to survive each level, and the screenshot matches that job exactly. The controls are the full first-person set, WASD to move, mouse to shoot and aim, wheel to change weapon, R to reload, SPACE to jump, which tells you this is a computer game through and through. It is not a subtle game, and it does not try to be: the street is full of zombies, the weapons are in your hands, and survival is the whole point. For players who like zombie shooters, the title screen alone sets the scene.

How to Play

On a PC, move with WASD, shoot with the left mouse button, aim with the right mouse button, change weapon with the mouse wheel, reload with R, jump with SPACE, and pause with P, according to the instructions.

Tips

  • Keep moving while you shoot, because standing still lets the horde surround you.
  • Reload during a gap in the attack, not when zombies are already close.
  • Switch weapons with the mouse wheel when the current weapon runs low, instead of waiting for it to empty.
