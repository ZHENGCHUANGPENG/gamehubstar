Mad Day Special Online

A muscular man in a green shirt and blue cap grins at the camera, a purple octopus floating in a fishbowl in one hand, a pistol in the other, while two bug-eyed aliens in glass helmets hover beside a green PLAY button. Behind them, a battered city skyline stretches under a pale sky, with flying saucers drifting overhead. The comic intro that follows is quieter, three panels of an ordinary day, a man kissing his octopus good morning, then sitting on a sofa with a game controller, captioned warm and sunny and nothing unusual. The official note promises the turn, an alien invasion, a racing escape, and a rescue for Fluffy, while the opening screen sets up that joke perfectly.

How to Play

Desktop players jump with the space bar and fire rockets with the up arrow key, according to the listed controls. The combination of jumping and rocket keys puts this firmly on a keyboard.

Tips

  • Press the green PLAY button on the title screen, then sit through the comic intro panels.
  • Use the jump key to clear obstacles, since the notes make jumping a core move.
  • Save rockets for the alien threats, rather than firing at everything on the road.
