Save The Girl Epic Online

The setup is brutally simple: a blonde woman tied to a chair in a dim basement, shelves of boxes behind her, a single hanging lamp. At the bottom, two items — scissors and a lighter. Pick right and she walks out; pick wrong and the level resets. Save The Girl Epic is a pure decision puzzle, and the screenshots capture exactly why the genre hooks people. Level 1 into 2 shows the formula working. The basement is rendered with care — pipes along the wall, a vent, a locked door — and the girl's worried expression sells the stakes. The two-item choice is the whole game: there's no timer, no dexterity test, just you reading the scene and committing. Scissors for the ropes seems obvious, but the lighter's presence makes you second-guess — what if cutting isn't the answer here? For puzzle fans, this is the "one more try" loop at its leanest. The official description promises varied dangers across levels plus decoration and clothing shops for style, but the core is that delicious moment of hesitation before you tap. Wrong picks sting just enough to make the right one satisfying.

How to Play

Tap to select one of the two offered items. Touch or mouse click — no keyboard needed.

Tips

  • Study the scene before choosing; the room details hint at the right tool. Scissors cut bindings but won't help against every threat. The lighter can be clever or catastrophic — think about what's flammable nearby. Wrong choices fail the level, but retrying is free and fast.
