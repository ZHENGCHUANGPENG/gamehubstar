The title screen sets the scene in one picture: a big rocket launcher across the top, the words IMPOSTOR RUSH ROCKET LAUNCHER in blue and red, a blue PLAY button in the middle, and two impostor characters, one with a backpack standing on the ground with a red mark above his head, and one in dark blue standing on a tower with a gun. The listing says a little impostor is trying to escape while soldiers on the towers watch for anyone trying to get away, and the screen shows exactly that standoff. Pressing PLAY opens a city level with tall buildings under a sunset sky, a score counter reading zero at the top, and a coin counter beside it.

The shooting follows the listing: on a computer the right mouse click aims and fires, and on a phone you touch the screen to aim and release to shoot. That touch rule is what makes the game work on a phone, since the whole level is portrait and the tower stands within thumb’s reach. The title screen carries music and speaker buttons at the top, and a settings gear appears once the level starts. There are no other buttons to learn, so the first shot comes seconds after the first tap.

The best part is how fast the game gets to the point: title, PLAY, and you are aiming at a soldier on a tower. The sunset city looks bright and the two impostors read clearly against it, and the zeroed counters give the first hits something to build on. The weak spot is the narrowness of the first level: one soldier on one tower is the whole opening, and the game does not explain what the coins buy or how the escape continues. The portrait frame also squeezes the city into a tall strip on a computer screen.

Casual shooter fans and players who like quick aim-and-fire rounds will enjoy this. Players who want a long campaign or many weapons should look elsewhere.

How to Play

On a computer, use the right mouse click to aim and shoot, as the listing instructs. On a phone, touch the screen to aim and release to shoot; the game is portrait, so the tower and the shooter both fit on an upright screen. Press PLAY on the title screen to start, and use the music and speaker icons at the top to change the sound. A settings gear appears at the top of the level once play begins.
