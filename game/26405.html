Rainbow Friends Survival Online

A colorful blocky castle fills the screen, blue walls with rainbow stripes, a pink mushroom at the side and a rainbow path running to the door. That is Rainbow Friends Survival, a 3D shooter where Level 1 opens with the words Shoot All Incoming Rainbow Friends and a CONTINUE button in the middle. Per the listing, shooting all incoming rainbow friends using different weapons, surviving each level to unlock new guns, and the screen backs that up: the castle is the arena, the rainbow stripes are the decoration, and the level banner is the mission. The posted controls are WASD to move, the left mouse to shoot, the right mouse to aim, the mouse wheel to change weapon, R to reload, Space to jump and P to pause, which gives the shooting a clear layout on a desktop. Bright and blocky, the castle holds the whole frame. The loop is short: press CONTINUE, aim, shoot the friends.
