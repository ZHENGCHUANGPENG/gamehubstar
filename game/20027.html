A tall furry monster walks down a city street, seen from behind, with a skull counter reading 0 of 2 in the top corner. That is Hagi Waga Attack, a horror game where the player is the monster. The street is drawn in flat colours: a variety store with a blue awning, a fire hydrant, green dumpsters, a parking meter and apartment blocks with fire escapes. The Level 1 label sits at the top right and an HP bar runs along the bottom left, so the hunt has a target and a health gauge from the first step. The listing tells the backstory: the beast once hid in the Poppy Playtime toy factory as a kind hugging toy, then ate the factory workers and went out into the city to terrorize the inhabitants, and each level asks the monster to capture and destroy a set number of locals. The title screen carries the same monster with Russian menu buttons, Play and Controls, which fits the game it opens. Walk the street, find the people, and fill the counter.

How to Play

Click the Play button to start the hunt, per the on-screen menu. The screen holds the monster from behind on a city street with a skull counter reading 0 of 2 at the upper corner, a Level 1 label and an HP bar at the bottom left.

Tips

  • The skull counter shows the catch target for the level
  • Watch the HP bar at the bottom left
  • The streets are the hunting ground, use them
  • Level 1 sets the pace, the city is the arena
