Bubble Shooter Pet Match 3 Online

Bubble Shooter Pet Match 3 is a cute, casual bubble shooter with a pet theme running through it. Instead of plain colored bubbles, you're matching bubbles with little animal faces — and a cheerful squirrel character at the bottom acts as your shooter, which gives the whole thing a friendly, playful feel. The mechanics are the classic bubble shooter formula: aim from the bottom, match three or more of the same color to pop them, and clear the board before the stack pushes down too far. The score bar at the top tracks your progress, and there's a pause button for when life interrupts. The night-sky backdrop with a big glowing moon makes it visually pleasant without being distracting. What makes it stand out from generic bubble shooters is the presentation. The pet faces on the bubbles, the squirrel shooter, and the soft colorful art style clearly target players who want something light and charming rather than intense. It's the kind of game that works well in short sessions — a few minutes of popping bubbles while waiting for something else. There's no complicated progression or upgrade system getting in the way. You load it up, start matching, and the difficulty ramps naturally as the bubble patterns get trickier. If you enjoy match-3 style games with a cute aesthetic, this one is an easy, no-commitment pick.

How to Play

Aim with the mouse and click to shoot bubbles, or tap and drag on mobile to aim and release. Match three or more of the same color to pop them. Swap your loaded bubble with the next one by pressing spacebar or tapping the swap button.

Tips

  • Bank shots off the walls to reach bubbles hiding behind clusters — the angle guide is your best friend. Drop large hanging groups by popping their anchor bubble instead of clearing them one by one. Save your color-swaps for when the board is messy, not when it's clean. Plan two shots ahead; the next-bubble preview is there for a reason.
