Crush These Ants Online

The title screen says it all in big letters: CRUSH these Ants, with the words sitting on a grey pavement slab next to green leaf buttons marked SMASH, ENDLESS, HOW and SCORE. That covers the whole game, and the screen matches the listing exactly: ants appear on the slab, and the player crushes them with a finger before they leave the screen. There is only one life, and the red ant is the trap, so the tapping has to be quick but not careless. The leaf buttons are large and easy to read, the pavement background keeps the dark ants visible, and the layout is wide, so the game sits in a landscape strip on a phone. It works as a simple reaction game with no learning curve: open it, tap the ants, and the pace does the rest. The HOW leaf explains the rules for anyone who wants them, and the SCORE leaf keeps the result visible after a run.

How to Play

With a desktop, click the ants with the mouse. On a phone, tap them with a finger, per the posted instructions: one click or tap to play. The leaf buttons on the title screen are tapped the same way on both devices.
