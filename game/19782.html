Red and Blue Stickman Huggy 2 drops you straight into the puzzle. Level 1 is already in motion in the captured image: a grey brick temple, two stickmen with crowns, one blue and one red, standing on a platform, with a WASD legend above the blue brother and an arrow-key legend above the red one. A heart and a counter sit in the top left, a zero score in the top centre, and pause, sound and home buttons in the top right. Three diamonds hang in the middle of the frame, a key waits further right, and blue water pools fill the gaps below the platforms. The studio description lays out the story: twin brothers who want to get out of the temple of darkness, everything under water, and they do not know how to swim, so you must take them out together and collect the diamonds to get the key. The screen alone teaches the game: two characters, two key sets, and a shared escape.

How to Play

On a computer, guide the first brother with WASD and the second with the arrow keys, and use the double jump where a single jump will not reach. Both brothers must make it through, since the description frames the escape as a job for two players together.

Tips

  • Move one brother at a time through a tricky section, instead of rushing both across together.
  • Collect the diamonds before going for the key, since the description ties the key to the diamonds.
  • Use the double jump only where the gap is clearly too wide for one jump, because a wasted double jump can drop you into the water.
