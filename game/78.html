How to Play

Fly the plane using desktop mouse control, or tap-and-guide touch on a phone, as the listed controls put it: mouse or tap to play. Start from the round play button under the title.

Tips

  • Steer with gentle curves so the plane never jerks into a rival path.
  • Sweep the open sky for energy points instead of hugging one corner of the map.
  • Give other planes a wide berth, since the description warns that hitting an enemy ends the run.
