Evil Granny Must Die Ch1 Online

Evil Granny Must Die Ch1 does not ease you in. The title screen is a small brick room: black tiles on the walls, a checkered floor, a round metal furnace with fire inside standing in the centre, and two pale figures facing the viewer, one in a dark dress on the left and one in a light dress holding something on the right. The title runs across the top in thin red letters, and below the furnace sit two buttons, START in green and Credits beside it, with Best Games and More Games tucked into the bottom corners. The source description casts the premise as waking up trapped in a bunker with escape as the only way out. The room on screen makes that premise feel immediate: walls close, fire low, and two figures already watching.

How to Play

On a computer, walk with WASD, look with the mouse, fire with the left mouse button, aim with the right mouse button, change weapons with the mouse wheel, throw grenades with G, reload with R, pick up items with F, run with Left Shift, and crouch with Left CTRL, in the game's own control list.

Tips

  • Start from the green START button under the furnace, and leave Credits for later.
  • Crouch in tight rooms, since the low view fits the bunker better than standing tall everywhere.
  • Aim before you fire in the dark, because the bunker gives you very little light to correct a miss.
