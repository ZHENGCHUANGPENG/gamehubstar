Lets Kill Evil Nun Online

Lets Kill Evil Nun sets its mood with a single room. A grimy tiled room fills the frame, with two tall dark windows, a black chair, a bare bed frame, a metal shelf with a hunched figure, and a rat on the checkered floor, while a nun in a long dark habit stands with her back half turned. The title runs across the top in jagged red letters, and START and CONTROLS buttons wait at the bottom. The studio description presents the premise: you came to investigate strange happenings at the abandoned asylum, rumour says the Evil Nun is behind them, so find crosses, prove her existence, and try getting out alive. That is a horror investigation with a shooter toolkit, not a flat arcade game: the room itself is part of the tension, and the instructions give the full set, walking, aiming, firing, grenades, reloading, picking up items, running, crouching and going prone. It is a slow, watchful game for players who like creepy rooms and careful movement.

How to Play

On a computer, walk with WASD, look with the mouse, fire with the left mouse button, aim with the right mouse button, change weapons with the mouse wheel, throw grenades with G, reload with R, pick up items with F, run with Shift, crouch with Ctrl, and go prone with X, following the instructions.

Tips

  • Move slowly through each room and check the corners first, since the asylum hides its clues in plain sight.
  • Keep a weapon reloaded before entering a new room, rather than reloading after trouble starts.
  • Listen for movement before running, because running makes noise and gives away your position.
