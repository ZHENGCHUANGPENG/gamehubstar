Pull Him Out Online Online

Pull Him Out Online is a pull-the-pin puzzle game starring a treasure hunter on a quest for gold. The core mechanic is instantly recognizable if you've seen this genre: the hunter is trapped in an underground chamber with pins separating him from treasure, hazards, and sometimes both. Your job is to pull the pins in the right order so the gold falls to the hunter — and not the lava, spiders, or boulders. Level 1, shown in the screenshots, lays out the basics clearly. The hunter stands at the bottom of a chamber, a horizontal pin holds back a pile of gold coins and gems above him, and a spider lurks in a side alcove. The pyramids in the background set the treasure-hunting theme. You can see the puzzle logic immediately: pull the right pin first and the treasure drops safely; pull the wrong one and something bad happens. What makes pull-the-pin games so addictive is that each level is a small logic puzzle with high stakes. There's no time pressure — you can study the layout as long as you want — but the solution isn't always obvious. Later levels add more pins, multiple chambers, and trickier hazard placement. The controls are simple tap-to-pull, making it perfect for both mobile and desktop. If you enjoy puzzle games where the satisfaction comes from that "aha!" moment when you spot the correct sequence, Pull Him Out delivers exactly that, one treasure chamber at a time.

How to Play

Click or tap a pin to pull it out, pins slide out in the order you touch them. On desktop the mouse works, on mobile a simple tap pulls each pin. There is no undo button, so think before you pull, though restarting the level is instant. Some pins are levers that rotate sections, and those are pulled the same way with a tap.

Tips

  • Always pull the pin separating treasure from the hunter last, because lava and monsters move the instant their barrier opens.
  • If a monster and lava share a chamber, drop the monster into the lava first by pulling the floor pin beneath them.
  • Watch for the pin that looks optional, in later levels the decoy pin floods the treasure chamber if pulled early.
  • Restart freely, since the levels are designed around trial and error and the reset is instant.
