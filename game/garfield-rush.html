Garfield Rush Online

Score 13. Coins 2. High Score 13. The menu remembers a run you never saw. Garfield Rush's title screen is a ledger first and a game second: the orange cat stands beside three blue panels keeping honest books. The loading thumbnail fills in the fantasy — Garfield mid-sprint on a city road, coins ahead — confirming the 3D endless runner the title promises. It's a small, tidy package: one shop button, one restart button, two counters up top, and a slot-machine icon hinting at bonus coins. What the test can't tell you is how the run feels — no gameplay was captured, so claims about dodging, jumping, or power-ups would be invention. But the structure is all there and legible: run, collect, beat 13. For a licensed-character runner, the presentation is clean rather than cluttered, and the persistent high score gives every session a target. Modest, honest arcade fare — verify the running feels good and it's a fine time-killer.

How to Play

Tap the restart button to start a run; the shop button (store icon) opens unlockables. Score and coin counters sit top-right.

Tips

  • - Score and coins are tracked separately — runs reward both distance and pickups.
  • - High Score persisted at 13, so beating your own mark is the session goal.
  • - Check the shop button before grinding — know what coins buy first.
  • - A slot-machine icon sits by the Coins row; it may offer bonus coins.
