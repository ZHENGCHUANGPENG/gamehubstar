How to Play

Drag on the cannon to set your angle, then release to fire — the drag distance sets power, so a short pull lobs softly and a long pull launches hard. On desktop the same works with mouse press-and-drag; touch play mirrors it exactly. Two power-ups sit in the bottom corners: a bomb and a fireball, each with a visible count, and tapping one arms it for your next shot. There is no keyboard input; everything runs through aim-and-release.
