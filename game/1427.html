Super Stickman Hook Online

Super Stickman Hook is a swinging game with one idea: hook, swing, fly. The level I played shows a red ball on a green platform under an orange sky, with another tilted green platform ahead, a pause button at the top left, a progress bar between the numbers 1 and 2 at the top, and a round hook target with a dashed ring at the top right. The listing says to tap to hook and make incredible jumps, avoiding every obstacle in the way and chaining acrobatic tricks in a row like a boss.

The controls are clicks on a computer. Click to hook onto the target and swing the stickman forward, then click again to let go and fly to the next platform. The title screen has a red stickman hanging from a hook line under the words STICKMAN HOOK, with a blue PLAY button marked 1/20 and a character box marked 1/6, plus a MORE GAMES badge in the corner. In testing, the PLAY button did not respond to taps on a phone, so the game is listed for computers only.

The strongest part is the feel: one click to hook, one click to release, and the swing carries you across the gap, which makes the game easy to understand in seconds. What holds it back is the narrow margin for error. The platforms are small, the hook target sits high on the screen, and a mistimed release drops the stickman, so the first levels already ask for good timing. The character box showed a different stickman look on each visit, which can be confusing at first.

One-button timing games and swinging physics are the whole appeal. Phone play is not on the table, so pick another game for that.
