Mr Stickman Online

Mr Stickman is a shooting game built around one clear job: aim at the enemy stickmen and shoot them. The title screen shows the name in big blue letters over a grassy field and a castle, with two round green buttons for the level grid and the play triangle. The level grid opens with level 1 in green while the rest stay padlocked, and arrows at the sides page through more levels. Level 1 puts two armed stickmen on a brick platform, one with yellow eyes and a rifle on the left and one with blue eyes on the right, with a row of bullet icons at the top left keeping track of the shots left. The description calls it a crazy shooting game with tons of challenging levels, and the first level already shows the shape of it: two gunmen, open ground, and a small number of bullets to do the job.

The controls are aim and shoot, nothing more. On a computer, move the mouse to aim and click to fire at the enemy stickman. On a phone, tap the target to aim and shoot, and the title and level grid both respond to taps. The green play triangle starts the game, the grid button opens the level map, and restart and level-grid buttons sit in the upper-right corner of each level. The bullet icons at the top left are always in view, so the shot count is part of every decision.

The strongest part is how readable it is. The two gunmen are big and dark against a bright field, the bullet count is impossible to miss, and the level grid makes progress obvious: one green square, the rest locked. The rough edge is the first level itself, which looks very dark on a computer and much brighter on a phone, so the same scene reads differently depending on the screen. The shooting is also thin at the start, with only two targets and no cover or movement to think about, so the early levels feel more like target practice than a fight.

Best for players who like quick shooting rounds and clear level goals. It suits short sessions on a phone or a computer, anyone who enjoys stickman shooters, and players who like unlocking levels one by one.

How to Play

Aim with the mouse and click to shoot on a computer; on a phone, tap the target to aim and shoot. The green play triangle on the title screen starts the game, and the grid button opens the level map, where level 1 is unlocked and the rest are padlocked. Restart and level-grid buttons sit at the top right of each level, and the bullet icons at the top left show how many shots are left.
