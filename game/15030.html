Hoop Stars Online

A vertical blue court fills the panel, with a red ball hanging in the air between two hoops and a bold yellow-and-orange title across the top. The court markings are simple and bright, and the whole panel reads like a pocket basketball court stood on end. The developer description promises a new kind of sport feeling, reverse basketball dribbling, plus trophy collecting and world challenges. The panel itself shows the court half of that promise clearly: ball, hoops, and open space for a shot. A red strip along the bottom of the panel anchors the court, like a baseline drawn in bold.

How to Play

【电脑专属，需键盘鼠标，手机无法操作】Aim shots on the blue court panel using a desktop mouse; the dribbling and dunking stay on a computer.

Tips

  • Aim each shot at the hoop on your side before worrying about fancy moves.
  • Watch the red ball in flight and learn its arc, since the arc tells you the next shot.
  • Keep the ball moving on the court instead of holding it still while rivals close in.
