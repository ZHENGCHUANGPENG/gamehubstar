Monsters' Wheels Special Online

The game opens as a three-panel comic. At the top, a driver in a red helmet and blue suit stands with arms crossed beside a red-and-blue monster truck numbered 77, asking in a speech bubble, ARE YOU READY TO CHALLENGE THE CHAMPION??? Below on the left, the same driver grips the wheel and answers himself: THEN, FREEZE YOUR BRAKES!!! On the right, three trucks crowd the starting line under a bank of signal lights, each one growling GRRR!!!, with the pink RIP truck at the top, a green truck in the middle and the 77 truck at the front. MONSTERS' WHEELS banners hang on both sides of the track, and a yellow arrow button waits at the lower right.

The driving scheme is the fullest of the day: the up arrow is gas, the down arrow brakes, the left and right arrows steer and balance, and Space or X fires the nitro, exactly as the instructions list them. The comic panels are the tutorial, since the champion challenge and the freeze-your-brakes line frame the race before the lights change. A yellow arrow button sits at the lower right of the panels. Learn the balance keys early, since the instructions give the side arrows a balance job as well as steering.

The comic opening is the strongest part of the five games today: the red helmet, the 77 truck and the GRRR!!! trucks give the race a character no plain menu can match, and the controls are the clearest, with every key named. The cost is the wait before it, since the loading bar climbs slowly and the comic panels have to be clicked through before the race, and the listing talks about ramming rivals, earning money and upgrading engines, none of which appears in the panels that were seen. The pink RIP truck also raises a question the opening never answers, since no rival names are shown beside the trucks.

Monster truck fans and keyboard racers who like a loud comic-book start will love this one. Leave it alone on a phone, and skip it if you want to jump straight to the track or dislike a slow load.
