The four-picture menu is the best part: the places are visible at once, the ramps and loops are easy to spot, and picking a run takes one click. What holds it back is the thin menu, since there is no car choice, no score board and no hint of what counts as a stunt on the screen, and the Unity loading bar before it climbs slowly enough to look stuck. The dark yard behind the pictures also hides most of its detail, so the menu reads flatter than the stunt driving it promises.

Keyboard driving fans who want quick stunt runs in a police car will like the one-click starts. Skip it for phone sessions, for players who want a car garage up front, or for anyone who will not wait through a slow load.
