How to Play

Drag your mouse or finger left and right to slide the cannon along the bottom; firing never stops and needs no trigger. Positioning is the entire game, which matters more than it sounds. Desktop has no keyboard to learn. Mobile plays identically with one-thumb dragging, even in short bursts.

Tips

  • Always target the lowest-numbered block nearest the bottom, not the biggest one up top — breaches come from the front row. Split fire between two columns when blocks bunch up, since shrapnel from destroyed blocks chips their neighbors. Grab extra-ball pickups before anything else; doubling your stream roughly doubles damage. When a huge block appears, park under it early instead of clearing small fry while it descends.
