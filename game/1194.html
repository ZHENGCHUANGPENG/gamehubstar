Temple Quest Online

A carved stone temple entrance fills the frame, TEMPLE QUEST written across the top in big yellow letters, a green play triangle glowing in the dark doorway, and four small round icons along the bottom, a flag, an info mark, a cart, and a speaker. Jungle leaves creep in from the sides, and the doorway itself is dark green, promising a run into the ruins. The developer's description adds the rest of the pitch: you stole an artifact and now you have to run for your life through an endless maze of temple ruins, collecting golden idols, ancient coins, and power-ups along the way.

The title screen carries the whole game on its own: carved stone, jungle leaves, and a glowing doorway that make the ruins feel like a place before the run even starts. The endless-run idea fits the theme too, since a temple you stole from should keep chasing you. The gap is between promise and proof: the menu shows only the entrance, so the outfits with special abilities, the power-up upgrades, and the score multiplier are all the developer's claims until you are inside. The game is also desktop-only, and the listing names no key map, so the first runs are spent learning controls the menu never explains.

Suited to desktop players who like endless runners and a strong theme, and who are happy to learn a run by playing it, not a good pick for phone players, since there is no touch play.

How to Play

Run through the temple ruins with the keyboard on a computer: the posted note describes keyboard running, and the site's own checks hold this one back from phones, so play it on a desktop. The listing names no key map, so treat the first runs as practice, learning how the run feels before chasing distance. Click the green play triangle in the middle of the temple entrance to start, then collect golden idols, ancient coins, and power-ups as you go, and steer around the obstacles the ruins throw at you.

Tips

  • Keep the first runs calm and learn the running feel before chasing distance.
  • Collect idols and coins on the way, since the description ties them to the score multiplier.
  • Treat the outfits and upgrades as goals for later runs, not things to chase at once.
