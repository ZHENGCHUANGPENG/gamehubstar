Alchemist Lab - Jewel Crush Online

Alchemist Lab - Jewel Crush takes the familiar match-3 board and gives it an alchemy theme: the jewels are the elements, and the bonuses are the interesting part. The official description explains the chain clearly, match three to clear, match four or five to make a bonus, match a bonus with a gem to activate it, and match bonuses together for a philosopher stone. That chain gives the game a goal beyond clearing tiles, because you are building toward bigger matches instead of just reacting to the board. The test run opened on a deep purple screen covered in a faint jewel pattern, which fits the lab theme even before the board itself appears. It is a calm, colourful puzzle, and the alchemy wording makes the usual match-3 moves feel like experiments rather than chores.

How to Play

Desktop players: swipe and match with the mouse the colourful gems, in the official instructions. On a phone, swipe with a finger in the same way, dragging a gem onto its neighbour to make a match.

Tips

  • Look for a four or five match before taking an easy three match, because the bonus is worth more than the quick clear.
  • Save a bonus until it sits next to a gem it can activate, instead of using it the moment it appears.
  • Scan the whole board after every move, since one match often opens a better one somewhere else.
