Cristiano Ronaldo Kick N Run Online

Cristiano Ronaldo Kick N Run is a 3D endless runner with a football star doing the running. The game opens on a cobblestone street with tram tracks running down the middle, old buildings on both sides, and a green tram waiting ahead. The runner wears a black and red kit and sprints away from the camera, and the first thing the game teaches is a swipe: big white letters on the street say "SWIPE LEFT!" as the tram looms. A poster on one building reads "VIVE LE FOOTBALL", and the coin counter at the top left starts at 100.

The controls are swipes and taps. Desktop players click to start and steer with the mouse; on a phone held sideways, swipe left and right to change lanes and dodge what is coming. The pause button sits at the top left beside the coin count, and the score and multiplier sit at the top right, so the run is always measured while you play. The description says the runner has to avoid obstacles, and the tram on the tracks is the first one you meet.

The setting carries this one. The cobblestone street, the tram tracks and the French posters give a familiar runner idea a real place to run through, and the swipe prompt teaches the controls without a separate tutorial screen. The narrow first impression is the drawback: the opening stretch is mostly straight street and one tram, the runner has little room to show off, and the score sits at zero until the run gets going.

This one suits endless-runner fans and football fans who want a quick lane-dodging run. Players after a deep football game with teams and tactics will not find it here.

How to Play

Tap or click to play, and swipe to steer. On a computer, click to start and use the mouse to guide the runner around the tram and the street furniture. On a phone held sideways, swipe left and right to change lanes; the first prompt on the street says "SWIPE LEFT!" so the opening move is taught on the run. The pause button sits at the top left, the coin count sits beside it, and the score and multiplier sit at the top right.
