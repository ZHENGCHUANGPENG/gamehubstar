Ancient Aztec Coloring Online

Ancient Aztec Coloring is a coloring book of eight Aztec line drawings, from a feathered serpent to a skull. The view is a wide grey panel with a zigzag pattern, headed by SELECT IMAGE in big white letters edged in green. Below it, eight framed line drawings in two rows of four: a feathered serpent, a stern Aztec face, a feather headdress, a skull with a round eye, a fox-like head and three more designs, all in clean black lines on cream paper, gold corners. A red book icon and a red sound icon sit in the corners. The listing bills it as eight pictures, twenty-three colors, and a way to save the finished image.

The sunny side, and the shady side: the eight pictures are large enough to tell apart at a glance, the skull and the feathered serpent are the boldest, and twenty-three colours give real choice. The wide view plays as a landscape strip, so the pictures stay big. The thin spots are the speed scoring, which rewards rushing over careful work, and the fact that the trial only saw the select grid, never a picture being coloured. Eight pictures is a small book, so the set may run out quickly.

Made for colouring fans and players who like bold Aztec line art with plenty of colours to choose from. Many pictures, slow careful colouring and a real test are not what it offers.

How to Play

With a desktop, click a picture to open it, then click a colour and an area to fill. On mobile, tap the picture and tap to colour, as the posted instructions put it: use your mouse to play the game or tap on the screen. Pick one of the eight pictures from the SELECT IMAGE grid, and fill the big shapes first while saving the fine lines for last. The skull and the feathered serpent are the boldest pictures, and save the finished image when it is done.
