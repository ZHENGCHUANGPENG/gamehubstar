How to Play

On mobile a Unity notice appears first — tap OK and the game continues loading normally. From the title screen, hit the big green Play button to start a puzzle. Once the letter grid appears, drag your finger across a word in any direction to highlight it, then release to lock it in. The music-note and speaker buttons on the title toggle sound before you begin.

Tips

  • On mobile, tap OK on the Unity notice — the game loads normally right after, so don't let the warning put you off.
  • Hunt rare letters first: a Q, Z or X shrinks the whole grid down to a few candidates.
  • When stuck, re-check backwards and diagonal placements — the sneakiest words hide there.
  • Pick a theme you know well for your first puzzles; familiar words surface much faster.
