ZigZag Snow Ski Online

ZigZag Snow Ski is a downhill arcade game with one run and one job: get the skier down the slope in one piece. The title screen shows the name over snowy mountains, cable cars and chalets, with a cartoon skier and a big blue play button. Once the run starts, the skier stands under a start arch that reads ZigZag Snow Ski, and the slope drops away below with yellow stars scattered between black rocks, logs and pine trees. A star counter sits at the top left and a music button in the upper right, and the whole slope runs as a narrow portrait column with snowy trees on both sides. The game is built by Unity WebGL, and the footer on the player names it ZigZag Snow Ski.

The controls are a mouse and a start button. Click the big blue play button to begin, then steer the skier down the slope and around the obstacles. The stars sit in the open stretches of snow between the rocks and logs, so collecting them means choosing a line early. The star counter at the top left ticks up with every star, and the music button at the top right switches the sound. This is a computer game, with the slope shown in a tall portrait frame.

What stands out most is how clean the slope reads. The stars are bright yellow against pale snow, the rocks and logs are dark and easy to spot, and the pine trees on the sides keep the run feeling like a mountain. The flaw is the start of the run, which drops the skier straight into the obstacles with no warm-up stretch, so the first few seconds are the hardest part. The slope stays a single straight run, so the variety comes from the obstacle layout rather than new ground.

Worth a try if you like quick arcade runs and score chasing on a computer. It suits short sessions between other things, anyone who enjoys dodge-and-collect games, and players who like a bright winter scene without complicated controls.

How to Play

On a computer, click the big blue play button on the title screen to start the run down the slope. Steer the skier around the black rocks, logs and pine trees on the way down, and ski through the yellow stars to add to the counter at the top left. The music button sits in the upper-right corner of the slope, and the run ends when the skier hits an obstacle.
