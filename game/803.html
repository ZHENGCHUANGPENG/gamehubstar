Zip Me Up Halloween Online

Zip Me Up Halloween opens with a picture that needs no explanation. A giant yellow moon fills the middle of the screen, black bats cross it, two ghosts hover beside a gravestone marked R.I.P., and a white cat sits in the grass at the edge. Two grey stone buttons sit at the bottom: a play triangle and a More Games button. The developer description is short and plain: click the lighter and find out what Halloween surprise you get. That simplicity is the whole appeal here, because there is no long tutorial to read before the first tap. The menu alone tells you this is a light, seasonal click game for short breaks, with a cartoon Halloween look rather than anything scary. It fits a quick session between other things: open it, tap, and see what pops out, then tap again. The bright moon against the dark sky keeps the play button easy to spot, which matters in a game that is meant to be picked up and played at once.

How to Play

On a computer, click using the mouse, as the instructions say mouse or tap to play. On a phone, tap the screen in the same way, starting with the large play button on the menu screen.

Tips

  • Tap the lighter on the menu screen first, since that is where the Halloween surprise starts.
  • Look at the whole moonlit menu before tapping, because the bats, ghosts and cat set the Halloween mood of the game.
  • Keep your taps on the centre of the screen, where the play button and the main action sit.
