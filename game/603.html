Fatboy Dream Online

A pale blue sky fills the screen with a rainbow arcing across it, white clouds drifting past and donuts floating in the air, and FATBOY DREAM is written in big bold letters at the top. Below the title stands a chubby boy in a red cap and a white shirt, smiling at the player, with info, fullscreen and speaker buttons in a row above him and a big orange play button near the bottom. The game description presents a boy who jumps from donut to donut, bounces on the walls and eats the flying donuts, and the boy, the donuts and the rainbow line up with the listing. The portrait layout keeps the boy large and the play button within thumb reach, and the bright colors make the whole screen feel light. A second look showed the same screen smaller, with a START button in place of the play button, the game adjusting its own layout.

How to Play

Using a keyboard and mouse, click to play and click to make the boy jump. On mobile, tap the display to jump, according to the instructions: tap to jump.
