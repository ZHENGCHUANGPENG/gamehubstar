Bandits Bane Online

Desert gold fills the frame, a wooden signpost pointing to western locations, a big revolver crossed over a board reading THE BANDIT HUNTER in red and white, and a cowboy in a blue hat and long brown coat holding two pistols beside a barrel. A wooden play triangle waits at the bottom centre, with info and speaker icons in the corner and a fullscreen button at the top. The phone in portrait mode only asks for landscape, which is itself useful information: this is a wide western, built for a wide screen. The game description adds the job, a robbed bank, bandits to shoot, hostages to rescue, and the first screen looks exactly like the poster for that hunt.

How to Play

Desktop players aim by moving the mouse and shoot by clicking it, the posted control sheet says. Aiming with a moving mouse is a desktop skill, so this one stays on a computer.

Tips

  • Aim with the mouse before clicking, since the notes make aiming a separate step from shooting.
  • Pick the most dangerous bandit first, as the description advises, rather than shooting at random.
  • Watch the wooden signs, because they mark the western locations the hunt travels through.
