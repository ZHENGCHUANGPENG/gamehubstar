Save the Kitten Online

Big orange letters spell CATASTROPHE across a blue sky, a white kitten leaps on the left, an orange cat reaches out in the middle, a black cat peers from a stone tower on the right, and a loading bar below reads FINDING THE TOMS TOWER. The desktop frame is bright and cartoonish, green leaves at the bottom, rocky ground on the left, the tower detailed with a red roof. The store name is Save the Kitten, but the game itself calls its opening CATASTROPHE, a difference worth stating plainly. The developer blurb adds the rescue story, kittens lured to a tower and carried to safety, and the opening screen backs the tower part with its cats and its stonework, even before any rescue is played.

How to Play

Desktop players use the arrow keys to control the trampoline cat, per the posted instruction sheet. The handset pass showed only an ad screen, so with no post-ad play confirmation, this title is held on computers and stays off phones.

Tips

  • Wait for the loading bar under CATASTROPHE to finish, since the opening view is a loading screen.
  • Read the cats and the tower first, because the scene sets the rescue premise.
  • Learn the arrow-key control on desktop first, because the sheet lists it as the core control.
