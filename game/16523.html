Squid Game Hidden Online

A giant doll with dark braids fills the title screen, a bare tree behind her and a pale sky above, while SQUID GAME HIDDEN lands in chunky pink letters across her face. A small magnifier icon hangs beside the title, a speaker button sits top left, a star button top right, and two yellow buttons, PLAY GAME and MORE GAMES, wait below. The store write-up puts the rules plainly: each picture hides objects to find, the clock runs, and wrong taps eat extra seconds. The doll and the magnifier do the thematic work before a single object is found, promising a quiet search rather than a chase. It is a landscape title screen, calm and readable, with nothing competing against the hunt itself.

How to Play

Desktop players click with the mouse to pick objects, and phone players tap the screen, according to the control sheet. The wide title screen plays in landscape, so turn the handset sideways.

Tips

  • Scan the whole picture once before tapping anything, since the blurb warns that wrong taps cost extra seconds.
  • Use the magnifier idea literally: sweep the scene in rows instead of staring at one corner.
  • When stuck, look at edges and shadows, where hidden objects usually sit.
