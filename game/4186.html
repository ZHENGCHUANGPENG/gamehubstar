Monster Clicker Online

Three monsters stare out of the title screen, one purple, one green and one orange, with a Play button waiting beside them. One tap opens the play screen, where a blue one-eyed monster fills the middle, a level readout shows Lv. 0, and the stage name reads Forest. The coin counter and a small swords icon sit in the top bar, and each tap adds to the total. The posted description promises endless gameplay and cool graphics, and the screen delivers the second half at once, with bright colours and a cheerful monster design that suits a phone held upright. The first half, the endless part, is a matter of the tapping loop itself: there is always another coin to earn and another level to reach. The trial showed the level counter, the forest stage and the coin total on the play screen, and that is the whole loop in view. A relaxed clicker with a friendly face, it keeps the interaction to one gesture and lets the numbers do the talking.

How to Play

Tap the monster to play, as the posted instruction says. Three monsters in purple, green and orange stand beside a Play button at the top of the screen. The same tap is a mouse click on a computer, and the play screen shows the monster, a level readout, the forest stage name and a coin counter.

Tips

  • Tap the monster in a steady rhythm
  • Watch the coin counter climb after each tap
  • The forest stage is the first area the game shows
  • Check the top bar for swords and coins between rounds
