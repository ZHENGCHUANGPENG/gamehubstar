Hero Rescue Puzzle Online

Hero Rescue Puzzle keeps its promise in the layout alone. The captured screen is a portrait panel: a gold header, then a tan board holding twenty square tiles in four columns and five rows, each tile numbered, the first one blue and the rest a flat grey. Below the grid sits a single blue button. Nothing on the screen shouts; the board itself is the invitation, twenty small doors in a row, one open at the start. The official description frames the game as a rescue puzzle, and the level board fits that framing: a long ladder of small problems to climb, one tile at a time, with the blue first tile marking where the climb begins.

How to Play

On a computer, pick a level tile with the mouse, as the instructions state. On a phone, tap the tiles the same way, starting from the blue first tile.

Tips

  • Start from the blue first tile in the top-left corner, since it is the only tile coloured differently from the rest.
  • Work through the grid in order instead of jumping to a later tile, because each level builds on the last.
  • Study the whole level before you act, since a rescue puzzle rewards a plan more than a fast first move.
