Epic Basketball Online

Epic Basketball gets its fun from physics you can feel. The drag-and-release shot is simple to explain and hard to master, because the ball has weight, the arc has a drop, and the hoop does not forgive a rushed release. The crazy characters give each shot a bit of theatre, and the world ranking gives a reason to keep shooting after a good run, but the core pleasure is quieter: a drag that feels right, a release that feels clean, and a ball that drops through the net. The bright loading photographed during the playtest, with its bold character icon, sets that playful tone before play even starts. Touch play is a natural fit, since dragging with a finger is how the shot was meant to feel. It is a pastime that rewards patience in small doses: slow down, aim properly, and the baskets start coming.

How to Play

Hold the left mouse button, pull back to set the arc, and let go to shoot on desktop; phones use the same hold, drag and release gesture with a finger.

Tips

  • Drag slowly and watch the aim line settle before you release, instead of flicking quickly.
  • Aim slightly above the hoop on longer shots, since the ball drops as it travels.
  • Use your first few shots to learn the bounce and weight of the ball, then start aiming for clean swishes.
