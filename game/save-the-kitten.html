Save the Kitten Online

Save the Kitten wraps a simple rescue mission in the kind of bright, storybook art that makes you smile before you have even moved. You play as a scrappy orange cat, and your job is exactly what the title promises: reach the little white kitten waiting by the rocks and bring it home. The level shown here sets the scene nicely — a stone tower on the left, a giant cat statue looming in the background, and a single white block sitting between you and your goal. The timer ticking down from the top of the screen adds a gentle urgency without ever feeling punishing, and the big yellow arrows make movement obvious on both touch screens and desktop. What makes it work is how readable everything is. The block you need to push is unmistakable, the kitten is clearly visible waiting for rescue, and the whole scene reads at a glance, which is exactly what a good puzzle-platformer needs. Pushing the block into position feels tactile and satisfying, and there is a small thrill in watching the timer while you line up the final shove. It is a short, sweet loop — see the kitten, clear a path, make the rescue — that is easy to pick up and hard to put down, especially for anyone who likes their puzzles wrapped in a cute package.

How to Play

Tap the big yellow arrows on the left and right edges of the screen (or use the arrow keys on desktop) to walk the cat. Push movable blocks by walking into them to build a path to the kitten.

Tips

  • Watch the countdown timer at the top — plan your route before it runs out.
  • Push the white block toward the kitten instead of wandering; every step costs time.
  • Use the pause button if you need a moment to study the layout.
  • Coins collected along the way add to your total, so grab them when the path is clear.
