Crazy Obstacle Blitz - Going Ball 3D Online

Crazy Obstacle Blitz - Going Ball 3D is a 3D ball-rolling game with one job: keep the ball moving forward and dodge the obstacles. The title screen is plain black, and across the middle in big white letters reads Obstacle Biltz, spelled with a z on the screen itself. Below the title sit three red buttons in a row: Start Game, More Games and Rate Us, in bold white letters. The Unity WebGL bar sits below with the words 3D game at the right end. The official description calls it a 3D ball game with simple controls: one click to play, and the ball must avoid obstacles while rolling forward.

What works, and what does not: the menu is as simple as it gets, with three large red buttons and nothing else in the way, so one click starts it and the job is clear before the first roll. The buttons are large and the menu reads at a glance on a desktop. The weak spot is the missing ball, since no ball appears anywhere on the title screen. The rolling itself is unseen, and the title carries a typo, Biltz for Blitz. One track and one job mean the game lives on speed alone.

Who will enjoy it: reaction-game fans and desktop players chasing a one-click ball roller with absolutely no learning curve. Tracks to choose, upgrades and phone play are all missing here.

How to Play

Desktop only: steer the ball with the keyboard controls, as the instructions list it, rolling forward, dodging the obstacles and keeping the ball on the track. Tap the red Start Game button to begin, and steer early around obstacles instead of waiting until they fill the screen. Keep the ball rolling forward, since stopping is worse than a risky swerve, and the More Games button opens other games if this one gets old.

Tips

  • Tap the red Start Game button to begin
  • Keep the ball rolling forward and steer early around obstacles
  • The More Games button opens other games
  • One click is all it takes to start, the listing says so
