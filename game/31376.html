Magic Solitaire Online

Magic Solitaire opens with a fairy who wants to teach you the game. A red-haired fairy with translucent wings and a teal dress fills the screen, asking "Greetings! Do you know how to play Solitaire?" with two green buttons below her, "Teach me" and "Skip tutorial". Behind her, playing cards are already laid out on a dark wooden table: a four, a five, a seven, a nine, a king and a jack of spades, with a deck and a spare jack waiting at the bottom. The listing describes a magical world of solitaire, with layouts on a tavern table by the fireside or in the shade of an oak tree by a rushing waterfall, and the fairy as your guide.

The controls are clicks or taps. Computer players click "Teach me" to hear the fairy's lesson, or "Skip tutorial" to go straight to the cards, then click the cards to play them. On a phone, tap the same buttons; the board fills a phone held vertically, with three stars at the top and four power-up icons along the bottom. A video ad can play before the map opens, so wait for the Skip Ad button instead of tapping the ad itself.

The strongest part is the presentation: the fairy, the castle backdrop and the wooden table make plain solitaire feel like a small story, and the tutorial means a new player is never lost. What holds it back is the friction before the game. A video ad can play before the map, the centre of the screen can go dark while an ad runs, and the first screen is a lesson rather than cards, so an experienced player will find the start slow. The board behind the fairy is already dealt, so the game is ready once the lesson is skipped.

This one suits solitaire fans who enjoy a fantasy setting and a guided opening. Anyone after hard boards from the first minute will be happier with a different game.
