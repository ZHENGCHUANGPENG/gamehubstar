The checkered gate sits at the end of a white track, a row of gems lined up in front of it, and a small runner in an orange shirt is sliding toward it on a bright yellow rail. That single frame is Roof Rails – Color Stack 3D at its most legible: a lane runner where the goal is to grow your rails by collecting matching color bars and carry them all the way to the finish. The screenshots show the runner mid-track with the rail visibly extended beneath his feet, the top progress bar creeping toward its checkered-flag icon, and then the payoff — a 'Level Complete' screen with a big yellow arrow button to continue. The visual language is deliberately plain: white track, bold yellow rails, a clean finish gate. Nothing distracts from the two questions that matter: are you in the right lane, and is your rail long enough? No control buttons appear during the run itself, which keeps the screen uncluttered but means the steering lives entirely in drags and swipes the screenshots can't show. An interstitial ad strip appeared between rounds, which is the honest price of the free loop. As a pure lane-running sketch it works — short levels, a clear finish, and the small satisfaction of watching the progress bar fill.

How to Play

The runner advances on its own along the rail — no steering buttons appear on screen during the run, so lane positioning happens through touch or mouse input with the game doing the forward work. The top progress bar tracks the distance to the checkered flag. Once the finish gate is crossed, the yellow arrow button on the Level Complete screen moves the run forward.

Tips

  • - Pick the lane with the matching color bars early — the runner never stops moving.
  • - Watch the top progress bar; the checkered-flag icon shows exactly how far the finish is.
  • - Aim the final stretch at the gem row in front of the gate — it sits right before the finish line.
  • - Hold your lane rather than swerving; late corrections cost more rail than they gain.
