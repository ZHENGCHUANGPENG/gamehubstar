Alphabet Kitchen Online

Alphabet Kitchen is a Sesame Street word game for young children, and the title screen says everything about it: the words Alphabet Kitchen sit on a big cookie, and Cookie Monster and Elmo stand behind it in chef hats with a green play button between them. Pressing play opens the kitchen itself, where the two chefs wait behind a wooden counter against a lime-green wall with two green shelves on the left. The listing says Chef Elmo helps children learn about vowels by blending letter combinations into words, baking three- and four-letter words into cookies.

The controls are taps and clicks. Computer players click the orange Play button on the Yiv loader, then the green play button on the title screen. Phone players tap the same buttons, and hold the phone sideways, since the kitchen is a landscape scene with dark bars above and below on an upright screen. An ad can appear before the kitchen opens; wait for the Skip Ad button and press it rather than tapping the ad.

The strongest part is the look: real Sesame Street characters in a bright kitchen make the game welcoming for small children, and the idea of baking words out of letter cookies fits the theme. What holds it back is the friction around the game. The Yiv Play button sometimes needs a second tap before the loader moves, an ad stands between the title and the kitchen, and the whole game is clearly built for little kids, so older players will find it too simple. The kitchen I reached was still setting up, with empty shelves and no letter cookies on the counter yet.

Parents with a preschool child learning letters and vowels will like this one. Players who want a word puzzle with a challenge should look elsewhere.

The game is built around one learning idea, stated in the listing: Chef Elmo helps children learn about vowels by blending letter combinations into words, baking three- and four-letter words into cookies. There is no fail state to worry about and no timer to beat; the session is a slow, bright kitchen visit, and the best way to play it is to let the child tap, say each letter out loud, and guess the word before the cookie is done.
