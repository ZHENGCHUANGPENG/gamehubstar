Correct Math Online

Correct Math does exactly what its title says, and the test screenshot proves it: the words Correct Math sit over a shower of floating numbers, with plus, divide and multiply signs scattered among them, and two big buttons, Play in red and More in green, underneath. The official description explains the loop, a question, three answers, a short time to think, and points for correct results, and the screen shows a Best Score of 0 waiting to be beaten. That is a clean, honest maths game with no extra dressing: the numbers are the game, the operators are visible, and the score is the reason to play again. It makes a good fit for short brain-training sessions, because each question is quick and the next one arrives straight after.

How to Play

On desktop, click on the correct answer from the three answers offered, per the control notes to tap on the screen to play. On the title screen, click Play to start and More for the extra options shown in the test screenshot.

Tips

  • Read the operator first, plus, minus, divide or multiply, before looking at the numbers.
  • Eliminate the obviously wrong answer first, so you only choose between two.
  • Do not pause to double-check every sum, because the short thinking time rewards fast, confident answers.
