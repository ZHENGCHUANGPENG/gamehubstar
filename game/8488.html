Among Us Color Defense Online

A dark purple banner carries the words Among Us Color Defense with four small potion-like icons below it in orange, green, blue and red, and two big dark round buttons sit underneath, one with a triangle play mark and one with a gamepad. Crewmates in green, blue, orange, pink and yellow crowd the edges around a big red crewmate at the center, and a 4Game Ground logo sits at the top. Tapping play opened the defense itself: a starry space scene with a score of zero at the top, a pause button at the right, colored icons down the left edge and crewmates along the lower edge. According to its description, a space base is under attack and the player must stop enemies with bombs of the matching color, and the four colors on the menu are the whole mechanic in one row. The wide view keeps the colors and the buttons apart so the right color is easy to reach.

How to Play

On a computer, click the bomb whose color matches the enemy. On a phone, tap the matching color bomb on the screen. The listing says to use the mouse or tap on the screen.
