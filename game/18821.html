Zombie Reform Online

Zombie Reform opens on a scene, not a menu. While the game loaded to 90%, the frame already presented the whole setup: a grassy field under a blue sky, pine trees, rocky hills, wooden crates and red-marked barrels, and an officer in a blue vest seen from behind, rifle in hand, facing zombies in police vests and one tall ragged figure. A sign on the central tree reads Zombie Reform, and a banner across the top names the maker. The developer description calls it a 3D zombie shooter survival game, and the picture matches that plainly: one armed survivor, several approaching dead, and open ground between them. The instructions give the full shooter toolkit, movement, firing, camera switching, a bazooka, reloading, crouching and weapon switching, which means the game expects mouse-and-keyboard play, not casual tapping. It is a combat game for players who want a third-person view and a clear enemy to stop.

How to Play

On a computer, move with WASD, shoot with the left mouse button, switch camera with the right mouse button, fire the bazooka with U, reload with R, crouch with C, and switch weapons with the number keys, following the instructions.

Tips

  • Keep moving between shots instead of standing in one spot, since the zombies close in from several directions.
  • Reload behind a crate or a barrel, not in the open, because a reload in the open leaves you exposed.
  • Save the bazooka for a group of zombies, since one heavy shot is worth more against several targets than against one.
