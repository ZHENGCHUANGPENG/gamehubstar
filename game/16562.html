Squid Game Challenge Honeycomb Online

Yellow hexagons pack the board like a honeycomb, and inside several of them sit small shapes waiting to be traced. Above the board stand figures in suits, one in green and others in pink, while a dark header bar and a blue button frame the play area. The developer blurb frames it as a test of drawing well-known shapes with a small stick, and the board shows exactly that test: no enemies, no timer shouting for attention, just shapes on hexagons and a steady hand. It is a quiet, tense kind of puzzle, where the challenge is precision rather than speed. A dark header bar above the board keeps the focus on the hexagons, not on surrounding chrome.

How to Play

Draw on a desktop with the mouse, following the shape outlines on the hex board. On a phone, draw the same shapes with a fingertip, which the listed instructions spell out as the mobile way to play.

Tips

  • Trace the outline slowly and stay inside the shape edge rather than rushing across it.
  • Start each shape at its clearest corner so the rest of the line has a steady anchor.
  • If a line goes wrong, lift, look at the shape again, and restart the stroke instead of scribbling over it.
