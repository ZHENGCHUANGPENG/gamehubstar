Soccer Physics Online

Soccer Physics Online is a 3D soccer game with blocky players and one idea at its core: challenge an online player or a friend in two player mode. The title screen spells it out in big letters — PHYSICS SOCCER ONLINE — over a green pitch, with a blond player in a red shirt standing near a goal at the left. A guest name field, a 2 PLAYERS icon and a green PLAY button sit below the title, so the whole setup is visible before a match starts. The description promises a fun 3D online soccer game, and the look backs that up: chunky figures, a bright pitch and a goal that is easy to spot.

The controls are a single input. On a computer, W jumps and kicks; on a phone, a tap does the same job, and the tap also rotates the player character, so the direction of the kick follows the taps. The guest name field takes a name before the match, the 2 PLAYERS icon sets up a game against a friend, and the green PLAY button starts it. A pre-roll ad runs before the title screen, so the first thing on screen is an ad rather than the pitch.

The strongest part is how little there is to learn. One key or one tap covers jumping and kicking, the blocky players are easy to follow on the green pitch, and the two-player option is right on the title screen instead of buried in a menu. The honest limit is what testing could actually see: only the title screen and the tap response were observed, so the flow of a real match — and whether goals come from timing or luck — is not something this review can confirm. The pre-roll ad before the title is also a wait every session starts with.

Good for soccer fans who want a quick two-player kickabout, and for anyone who likes physics games with a single control. Short sessions fit it best, on a handset or a desktop, especially with a friend sharing the screen.

How to Play

On a computer, press W to jump and kick. Tapping a phone screen does the same job, and each tap also rotates the player character, so the direction of the kick follows the taps. Type a guest name in the name field before pressing the green PLAY button, and use the 2 PLAYERS icon to set up a match against a friend. A pre-roll ad runs before the title screen appears, so the first thing on screen is an ad rather than the pitch.

Tips

  • Enter a guest name first, since the name field sits right above the green PLAY button
  • Tap once and watch how far the player rotates before tapping again, so the kick faces the goal
  • Use the 2 PLAYERS icon when a friend is beside you, since the game is built for two-player matches
  • Wait out the pre-roll ad before the title, because the PLAY button only appears after it
