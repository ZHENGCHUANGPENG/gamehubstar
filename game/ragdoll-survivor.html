Ragdoll Survivor Online

Ragdoll Survivor understands that floppy physics and stickman combat are a match made in heaven. The arena opens on a hazy sunset — watchtower silhouette, drifting smoke, a supply crate floating down on a tiny parachute — and then your red stickman squares up against the first enemy with a helpful PUNCH: press F floating overhead. Every limb is a live physics joint, so fights never look the same twice: punches send torsos spinning, grabs turn into wild throws, and a well-timed smash can fold an opponent like a lawn chair. The control scheme is generous for a browser brawler — move, jump, duck, punch, smash, block, dash, grab, and a super, all laid out in a tidy bar at the bottom of the screen so you never have to guess. Structure comes from the chapter-and-level framing with a KNOCK DOWN target (0/6 in the opening level) and a score counter that rewards aggressive play. The health bar up top keeps tension honest; the sunset may be pretty, but the enemies hit back. It is the kind of game where you lose track of time chaining dash-punch-grab combos just to see what ridiculous pose the physics spits out next. Simple to learn, endlessly watchable, and surprisingly deep once the later waves pile on.

How to Play

A/D to move, W to jump, S to duck, F to punch, G to smash, C to block, Shift to dash, E to grab, R for super — the full key list is shown at the bottom of the screen during play.

Tips

  • PUNCH (F) is your bread and butter — the tutorial prompt appears for a reason, so get comfortable with its range first.
  • Dash (Shift) through enemies to reposition; standing in the middle of a crowd is how health bars disappear.
  • Block (C) before a big swing lands — trading hits with ragdoll physics rarely ends well for you.
  • Grab (E) an enemy, then follow with smash (G); watch the KNOCK DOWN counter tick toward the level goal.
