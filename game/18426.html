PixelArt Online

PixelArt is a pixel-art coloring game on a pink screen, with a red heart for Level 1. Soft pink screen, Level 1 in black letters up top, a book icon and a gear at the left, a blue reset arrow on the right. Below the title, the target: a red pixel heart with a white shine. Under it, the grid, a heart of grey and white squares waiting to be filled. At the bottom, a small palette with a red square and a white square, a blue DONE button, a lightbulb hint and a double-arrow button, both marked AD. The developer note calls it a no-pressure way to draw cute images and build matching skill.

The plus side, and the minus side: the setup is calm and clear, with one heart, one grid and a palette of two colours, and the portrait layout works best with the phone upright, with big tappable cells and a DONE button that ends the picture. Less good is the thin palette, with only red and white on Level 1, and the hints, since the lightbulb is marked AD, so help costs an advert. The grid is also small, so later pictures may stay simple rather than detailed.

Who it is for: colouring fans, children and calm players who like a no-pressure picture to fill in. Many colours, big detailed grids and a real test are not on offer here.

How to Play

Using a mouse, click a colour in the palette, then click the grid cells to fill them. On a touchscreen, tap the colour, then tap the cells, as the posted note says: click for draw. Tap the red square in the palette first, and fill the grey cells that sit inside the heart shape, since the white cells are the background. Use the lightbulb hint if a cell is unclear, and tap DONE when the grid matches the heart.

Yes. PixelArt works on phones and tablets as well as on desktop. It is a portrait game, so it plays best held upright, using the touch controls.
