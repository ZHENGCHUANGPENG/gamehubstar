PixelArt Online

The screen is soft pink, and at the top reads Level 1 in black letters, with a book icon and a gear on the left and a blue reset arrow on the right. Below the title sits the target: a red pixel heart with a white shine. Under it is the grid itself, a heart shape made of grey and white squares, waiting to be filled. At the bottom sit a small palette with a red square and a white square, a blue DONE button, a lightbulb hint marked AD on the left, and a double-arrow button marked AD on the right. The words for the maker sit at the very bottom. The listing presents it as a game where the player draws a cute little image and builds color-matching skill without pressure, and the scene fits that: one heart, one grid, and a palette of two colors. The vertical layout suits a phone held upright, the cells are big enough to tap, and the DONE button is the one to press when finished.

How to Play

Using a mouse, click a color in the palette, then click the grid cells to fill them. With a phone, tap the color and tap the cells, as the posted note says: click for draw.

Tips

  • ['Tap the red square in the palette first', 'Fill the grey cells that sit inside the heart shape', 'Use the lightbulb hint if a cell is unclear', 'Tap DONE when the grid matches the heart']
