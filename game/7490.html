Scary Granny Online

Jagged white SCARY GRANNY letters cut across a dark corridor of grimy tiles and shadowed doorways, PLAY GAME and MORE GAMES sitting in the middle of the frame, with a plain control note below: use arrow keys or WASD to move, SPACE to shoot. The official blurb relates the premise in one breath: run, avoid or shoot the old woman, find the door, escape. The display confirms the horror side fully, with a corridor that looks damp, empty, and too quiet. It is a stark, uncluttered title screen, with the maze and the threat both implied by the empty hallway rather than shown outright.

How to Play

Desktop players move with the arrow keys or WASD and shoot with Space, exactly as the on-screen control note says.

Tips

  • Move in short bursts and listen for the chase, since the corridor gives little warning.
  • Keep the door in mind from the start, because the goal is escape, not a high score.
  • Shoot only when the shot is clean, since a miss in a tight corridor costs the moment you need.
