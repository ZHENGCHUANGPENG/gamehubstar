How to Play

The listing says to burrow with a finger, but the phone pass here shows a Unity warning that the engine is not supported on mobiles, so the verified play stays on desktop. On a computer, use the mouse to dig the path the balls follow, clicking the restart icon at the top left to try the level again. Keep the window tall, since the shaft fills a narrow frame.

Tips

  • Dig one clear channel first, since getting balls to the end is the goal.
  • Watch the coloured balls at the top, because they are the pieces that must reach the goal.
  • Restart the level when the path blocks, since the icon sits near the top for a reason.
  • Dig in small strokes rather than one long cut, because the balls follow the channel you carve.
