How to Play

Tap a bubble to pop it, and the board answers at once. The screen shows a four-by-four grid of bubbles in red, yellow, green and blue rows, a SCORE readout of zero, a Your progress bar below it and a level label of one of one with arrows on either side. A fingertip does the same tap on a phone, and a pointing-hand hint marks the first bubble to try.

Tips

  • Tap the bubbles in any order, there is no wrong first tap
  • Watch the progress bar fill as the board empties
  • The pointing hand shows the first bubble to pop
  • Pop a full row to clear the board fastest
