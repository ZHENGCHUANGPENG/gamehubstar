Shot Factor Online

Shot Factor takes a familiar shooting idea and gives it a number twist. Before the fight, the gun gets better through numbered squares — that is the entry the description promises — and Level 1 shows the idea working on both devices. The board is a grid of grey and white tiles with one green tile carrying a +1, a gun sits on the left firing at 1.0/s, a Laser Cannon card above it is marked AD Use, and a white hand hint points at the grid. A gem counter at the top reads zero, a sound button sits beside it, and a Collection trophy button waits below the board.

The controls are one input. Mouse or tap to play, says the instructions, and the Level 1 screen backs that up: tap the numbered squares to build the gun's power, then the gun shoots. The Laser Cannon card is the one gated extra — AD Use means an ad opens it — and the hand hint shows a new player where the first tap goes. The gem counter and the Collection button give the run a small economy to watch while the grid fills in.

The strongest part is that the loop was actually seen live: the grid, the numbered tile, the firing gun and the Level 1 label were all on screen, on a computer and on a phone, so the idea is confirmed rather than promised. The board is easy to read, with the green tile standing out against the grey and white. The honest limit is the depth: only Level 1 was seen, so later guns and higher numbers are not confirmed, and the Laser Cannon needs an ad before it can be used at all.

Good for casual shooter fans and number-puzzle players who like a short upgrade loop, and for anyone who wants a quick session on a phone. It suits one-level-at-a-time play, where each grid is a small puzzle before the shooting starts.

How to Play

Mouse or tap to play. Tap the numbered squares on the grid to build the gun's power — the green tile carries a +1 — and the gun on the left fires at 1.0/s once the numbers are set. The Laser Cannon card above the gun is an ad-gated extra marked AD Use, and the hand hint over the grid shows where to tap. The gem counter at the top tracks what the run earns.
