Monster Truck Extreme Racing Online

The menu opens on a garage scene with a bright green monster truck in the middle, big tyres, a raised body and a red detail on the side, with the game’s orange logo in the top corner and a row of menu buttons down the left. The listing describes desert races where finishing in the top three wins prizes, a garage where new trucks can be bought, and a huge free-drive map for stunt shows. The menu matches that picture: the truck is the star, and the garage is where the game wants you to spend time. One- and two-player options are part of the listing, so a second driver can join on the same keyboard.

The controls are keyboard only, which is why the game is listed for computers. Player 1 steers with the arrow keys, taps N for nitro, B to look back and R to restart a level. Player 2 uses W, A, S and D to drive, T for nitro and C to look back. Menu choices are clicks: races, the garage and the free-drive map are all reached from the menu buttons on the left. There is no touch steering on offer, so a phone cannot drive these trucks.

The strongest part is the truck itself: the green machine in the garage looks the part, and the promise of desert races, stunt maps and a garage full of trucks to unlock gives the game a clear loop. The two-player mode on one keyboard is a nice extra for anyone sharing a computer. What holds it back is that the menu is all the game shows before a race starts, and the first screen says nothing about how the trucks handle. The ad overlay that covers the menu at first also gets in the way until it is skipped.

Racing fans who like big trucks and keyboard driving will enjoy this. Phone players and anyone without a keyboard should skip it.

Tips

  • Finish in the top three to win prizes you can spend in the garage
  • Visit the garage between races to look at new trucks before the next race
  • Use nitro on the long desert straights, not in the middle of a turn
  • Try the free-drive map first to learn the truck before a real race
