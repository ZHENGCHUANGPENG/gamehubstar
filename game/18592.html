Fruits Pop Online

Fruits Pop is a bubble shooter with a rescue twist. The first stage opens on a night jungle scene, where a cluster of oranges, lemons and plums hangs in the middle of the screen with a small chicken trapped in a hexagon at its centre. A shooter sits at the bottom, loaded with an orange and a lemon. The game description says the goal is to free the little pets trapped inside the fruit, and the tutorial banner on the first stage says the same thing in plain words: "Clear Fruits and save the pets!" Shooting two or more identical fruits clears them from the board, so every shot is a small matching puzzle as well as an aiming test.

The controls are a slide and a release. Computer players move the mouse to aim the shooter and click to fire the fruit at the cluster. On a phone, slide a finger to aim and lift it to shoot; the title screen is built for a phone held upright, with a big blue play button and a sound button below the falling fruit. The pause button sits at the top right of the stage, and the score and high score sit at the top centre, so the numbers are always in view while you aim.

What works best here is how readable it is. The fruits are big and bright, the trapped pet is easy to spot, and the first stage teaches the goal with one banner instead of a wall of text. The slow start is the weak spot: the first cluster is small and forgiving, the score sits at zero for a while, and the night jungle background is darker than the cheerful title screen, so the mood changes between the menu and the game. The shooter also shows only the next fruit or two, so longer plans are hard to make.

This one suits casual bubble-shooter fans and anyone who likes a quick match-three break. Players after a fast, twitchy arcade game will find it gentle.
