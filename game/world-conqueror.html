World Conqueror Online

Everything on the menu has dice on it except the title. That detail matters, because the old listing described World Conqueror as an io-style territory game — and nothing on this teal menu supports that. What the screen actually shows: a PlayerName field, two chunky mode buttons (Adventure with dice, World War with a globe), a Skins button wearing dice, and a More Games link under an FMGames credit. The dice motif is the only mechanical hint the game gives, and it points at a dice-driven board game, not a shared-map io battler. The test never got past this screen, so modes, rules, and win conditions are all unverified. Category corrected accordingly: board, not io. What's fair to say is that the menu is clean, the two-mode structure is clear, and the presentation is cheerful. What's not fair is repeating the old io claims. Until a deeper test captures gameplay, treat this as a dice-themed conquest game with two modes — and verify before believing more.

How to Play

Type a player name, then tap Adventure or World War to start. The Skins button (dice icon) opens appearance options.

Tips

  • - Two modes are offered — try Adventure first to learn the ropes.
  • - Set your PlayerName before starting; the field sits center-screen.
  • - Dice icons on Adventure and Skins hint the game revolves around dice rolls.
  • - Check Skins if you want a different look before conquering.
