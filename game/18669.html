How to Play

Skateboard Lighter is a cartoon runner: a little green lighter with a red top rides a yellow skateboard down a white track under a pink sky. Tap or click the big yellow Start Game button to begin the run. On a computer, steer with the mouse; on a phone, swipe to steer, and hold the phone upright, because the track runs tall and this is a portrait game. The track is the white strip cutting diagonally across the screen, so keep the rider on it: the smiling yellow crescent moon and the smiling blue star are sky decoration, not the road. Red and white popcorn boxes sit in a row further up the track, waiting to be collected, so line up your steering early and ride through them instead of swerving at the last moment. The developer calls it a game of breaking through obstacles, and the start screen shows a clear track with only popcorn in view, so keep your eyes up the track and be ready to steer around whatever appears as the run picks up speed. Small steering corrections work better than big ones, because the track is narrow and the rider answers every move at once. A short ad may play before the run starts, and you can skip it when the countdown ends. One track and one rider mean the variety comes from speed alone, so treat the first run as a sighting run: learn where the popcorn lines up, then chase a cleaner, faster ride next time.
