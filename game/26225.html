How to Play

On desktop, steer with A and D or the left and right arrow keys, as the instruction note records. The title and tags mark this as a racing and drifting game, so play it with a keyboard and mouse.

Tips

  • Use the blue side arrows to look through the options before pressing PLAY, since the menu offers more than one choice.
  • Steer with A and D at first, because the note gives those keys as the steering set.
  • Aim for controlled slides around turns, since the note says points come from drift distance and style.
