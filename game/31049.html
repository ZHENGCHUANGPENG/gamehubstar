How to Play

Move with WASD or the arrow keys, shoot with the left mouse button and aim with the right, exactly as the posted keys list them. Press R to reload, F to interact, Ctrl to run and Space to jump. Open CONTROLS from the menu before the first run, since the menu puts it beside PLAY for a reason.

Tips

  • Use the police car and street corners as cover, since the listing warns enemy troops shoot on sight.
  • Reload with R after each wave, because an empty gun mid-fight ends the run.
  • Collect the coins enemies drop, since the listing ties new characters to what you earn.
  • Check the SHOP button before the first run, because the listing says characters carry unique abilities.
