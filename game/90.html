Angry Checkers Online

Angry Checkers does not hide what it is. The captured screen is a plain brown board with eight red ringed pieces lined up across the top row and eight blue ringed pieces across the bottom, a black header above with SCORE in the middle, and the words Go! on the blue side and Wait.. on the red side. Across the centre sits the honest message of the moment: Waiting for another player. Below the board are four dark buttons, SHADOW, BOARD, CHECKER and MENU, each naming something you can change or open. The official description lists three ways to play, against another player online, against the machine, or against a friend on the same device. The screen caught the online version between players, which is exactly when a board game looks its simplest: pieces set, score at zero, and nothing to do but wait for the other side.

How to Play

On a computer, interact with the board using mouse clicks and the buttons below it, as the instructions state. On a phone, use touch in the same way, tapping pieces and buttons on the board view.

Tips

  • Read the score header first: both sides start at zero, so the first move sets the tone.
  • Remember which colour is yours from the header, since red starts at the top and blue at the bottom of the captured board.
  • If the middle of the board says it is waiting for another player, that is matchmaking, not a frozen screen.
  • Use the four buttons below the board for view and menu options before you start moving pieces.
