Knock Balls Online

Knock Balls opens on a bright green title screen - LEVEL 1, TAP TO PLAY, best score zeroed out - with a loaded cannon waiting at the bottom. The premise is pure demolition: stacks of red cans arranged as pyramids and columns on a pedestal, a limited supply of striped cannonballs, and physics doing the rest. Each level frames its target while the cannon's remaining ammo is marked beside the barrel, and a slim bar at the top tracks progress from level 1 to level 2 as structures fall. Shots arc through the air - one screenshot catches a ball mid-flight - and the satisfaction comes from reading the structure. Hit the load-bearing seam at the base and the whole pyramid pancakes instead of chipping away one can at a time. What keeps it honest is the shell budget. With only a handful of balls per structure, wasteful shots are punished immediately, and every failure teaches something the next attempt uses: the tower fell left because the shot landed left. There are no characters, no power-ups, no slingshot theatrics - just aim, fire, and gravity settling the argument. It rewards a demolitionist's mindset: think backwards from the final collapse to the first impact point, and one shot can do the work of five.

How to Play

Tap to start each level, then aim the cannon at the stacked targets and fire. The cannon stays fixed at the bottom of the screen; every shot arcs toward the structure, and the remaining ball count sits beside the barrel.

Tips

  • Break the base first: pyramids and columns collapse from the bottom, never the crown.
  • Aim where two cans touch - dislodging one often drops its neighbors for free.
  • Watch each shot in flight and adjust; you only advance while you still have balls left.
  • Clear the entire structure to move on - a half-knocked tower does not count.
