How to Play

Swipe or tap to switch lanes and jump over the obstacles on the track, following the endless runner format. The phone screen shows the runner mid-sprint between two trains, a score of eighteen, a times-one multiplier, a coin count of zero and a pause button at the top. The same run is a mouse or keyboard game on a desktop, and a MORE GAMES bar sits along the bottom of the screen.

Tips

  • Stay in the middle lane until a train forces a switch
  • Watch the track ahead, not the runner
  • Grab coins only when the lane is clear
  • The pause button is at the top when a run needs a break
