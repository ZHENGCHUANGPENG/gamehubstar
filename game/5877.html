Pumpkin Monster Online

Pumpkin Monster is a Halloween anti-stress game. A foggy forest with bare trees and a dark leafy floor fills the view, and in the middle stands the monster: a grinning jack-o-lantern head with sharp teeth on a green body with clawed hands. Across it, in big orange and yellow letters with a black outline, reads PUMPKIN MONSTER, and below the title sits a round blue play button with an orange triangle. The words JulGames.com sit at the bottom in orange and white. The listing describes an anti-stress game built on kicking a pumpkin and buying weapons to use against the monster.

The fun, and the limit: it is instantly readable, with one monster, one button and a spooky forest behind them, and the monster is big and easy to hit, so the first kick lands within seconds. There is no score chasing and no fail state, which is the whole point. The limit is repetition, because one monster and one action is the whole game, and the weapons the listing promises never appeared on the title screen, so the variety is claimed, not shown. After a few minutes the joke may wear thin.

Good for Halloween fans who want a silly, no-pressure toy to tap for a minute or two. Levels, goals and a game that fights back are not on the menu.

How to Play

Click the round play button with the mouse, then click the monster to kick it. Phone players tap the play button, then tap the monster, in the words of the posted instructions: tap to play. Hold the phone sideways, since the forest runs wide, and tap the pumpkin wherever it stands, because the monster is the whole target. The game is for letting off steam, so there is no rush, and look for new weapons to try against the monster once the kicking starts.

Tips

  • Tap the pumpkin monster to kick it
