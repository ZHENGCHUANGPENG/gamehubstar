Highway Racers Online

At first glance the screen is a level-select grid laid over a road at dusk, with level 1 open at the top left and the rest locked, and a play button waiting at the side. That is Highway Racers, a driving game about racing through traffic, collecting cash and power-ups, and finishing the track, and the listing says exactly that in one short line. The screen stretches wide, the road stretches behind the grid, and the locked levels give the game its shape: finish one to open the next. Steering uses the keyboard, WASD or arrow keys, so the game stays desktop-only. It feels like a simple traffic racer with a clear loop, dodge the cars, take the cash, and open the next level. The dusk light and the road markings keep the speed visible, and the locked levels ahead give every finished track a reason to run again.
