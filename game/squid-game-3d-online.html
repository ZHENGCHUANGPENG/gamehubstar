How to Play

Everything runs through one large circular button: press while it glows green to run, and freeze the instant it turns red. The tested round showed no other inputs - timing that button is the entire skill. Desktop players use the same on-screen button via mouse.

Tips

  • Freeze the moment the button turns red. That color swap is the only signal that matters - the crowd around you will not save you.
  • Advance in short bursts during green windows instead of one long sprint. Short steps are far easier to halt when the light flips.
  • Watch the 60-second timer, not just the doll. Players who dawdle burn their green windows and get forced into a panicked final dash.
  • Keep your line straight toward the tree line. Sideways drifting wastes distance you can never earn back on the clock.
