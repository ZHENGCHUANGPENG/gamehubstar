BitLife - Life Simulator Online

A narrow red panel fills the centre of the frame, a white tadpole-like mark floating above a bold yellow BITLIFE wordmark, with a small candywrite credit tucked at the bottom. There is no character, no street, and no score on this screen, just colour, a logo, and a promise. The official blurb fills in the rest: a text life simulator that mashes up adult life, where you can aim for a model citizen, marry, raise children, and study, or horrify your parents with crime, prison riots, and smuggled bags. The title screen is honest about its genre, a story told through choices, not through action, and that is exactly what the red panel advertises.

How to Play

Desktop players play with the left mouse button, per the control sheet, clicking through the life choices the simulator presents. The whole interaction is mouse-driven, so a keyboard and mouse are the way in.

Tips

  • Read each choice fully before clicking, since life sims reward the careful reader.
  • Think a few steps ahead, because one choice shapes the options that follow.
  • Replay with a different attitude, since the description promises very different lives from different choices.
