Truck Parking 2 - Truck Driver Online

Truck parking is driving with the glamour removed and the skill left in, and this game knows it. The title photographed during the playtest is confident about the job: Truck Parking in big letters, with Play, Shop, Rate and Settings below, and a truck ready to move. The challenge is not speed but precision, because a truck is long, slow to turn and unforgiving of a bad angle. That makes every successful park feel earned in a way a fast lap does not: you planned the approach, corrected it twice, and slid a big vehicle into a tight space without touching anything. The landscape presentation suits the job, giving you room to see the truck and the spot together. It is a calm, methodical driving game for players who like doing a hard, ordinary job well.

How to Play

【电脑专属，需键盘鼠标，手机无法操作】Keyboard steering for a truck: small corrections, slow speed and careful parking, desktop only.

Tips

  • Approach the parking spot slowly and straight, so the truck's angle is right before the final move.
  • Use small steering corrections instead of big turns, since a truck responds late to every input.
  • If the angle looks wrong, pull forward and reset the approach rather than forcing the truck in sideways.
