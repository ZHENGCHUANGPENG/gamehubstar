How to Play

Player one moves with WASD, shoots with F and hides with S, while player two moves with the arrow keys, fires the laser gun with J, uses the catcher capsule with K and throws a trap with L, following the listed controls. Tips

  • Player one shoots with F, player two with J, keep the fire split
  • Hide with S when the aliens close in
  • Throw a trap with L where the aliens are heading
  • Catch with the capsule key K, it is the move that scores
