Draw The Car Path Online

Blue and yellow title letters dominate a flat grey parking lot, a gold play button sitting dead centre, while small cars in orange, dark blue, green, light blue and yellow wait around the edges like pieces on a board. The developer description calls it a combination of parking and puzzle, and the title screen shows exactly that combination: no road, no traffic, just cars and the empty lot between them and their spots. The puzzle is the line you have not drawn yet, and the clean top-down view keeps every car and gap visible at once. It is a landscape board, quiet and orderly, inviting one careful stroke at a time.

How to Play

Desktop players draw paths with the mouse, and phone players swipe a finger along the same route, as the in-game instructions explain. The wide lot plays in landscape, so hold the handset sideways.

Tips

  • Plan the longest route first, since one long path can block every car behind it.
  • Keep paths from crossing where possible, because a crossing usually means a jam.
  • Send the car with the tightest parking spot first and let the easy ones wait.
