Would you Rather? Online

Would You Rather? is a party quiz built on a single idea: two options, one choice. The title screen shows a red THIS card and a blue THAT card with a PLAY button, and each question fills the screen with a photo in the middle, one option on the red side and one on the blue side, and a CHOOSE button at the bottom. The first question I saw showed a photo of hands holding cash with the words Be Rich, and the job was simply to pick a side. There is no board, no timer and no complicated menu, just a question and two ways to answer it.

The controls are the same everywhere. On a computer, click PLAY, read the two options, then click the red or blue side or the CHOOSE button. On a phone, tap the same spots; the question screen fits an upright screen, and the title screen works in both directions. A short ad grid can appear after PLAY, so wait for its countdown or close button instead of tapping the ad tiles.

The charm is the format itself: one question, two answers, and an instant reason to argue about the choice, which makes it a natural game for a group of friends. The weak spots are visible too. An ad wall sits between PLAY and the first question, the question text can overlap the photo and look cramped, and the screens I saw show no score or progress, so a long session has no target to chase.

Friends and families who like party games will get the most from it. Anyone looking for a solo quiz with scores and levels should pick a different game.

How to Play

On a computer, click PLAY on the title screen, then click the red or blue side of the question, or the CHOOSE button at the bottom, to pick an option. On a phone, tap PLAY and tap the side you would rather choose; the question screen fits an upright screen, and the title screen works in both directions. A short ad grid can appear after PLAY, so wait for its countdown or close button instead of tapping the ad tiles.
