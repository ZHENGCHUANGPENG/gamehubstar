How to Play

Controls are touch-first. A big hand icon and the words 'touch to play' open Stage 1 — tap once to start the boat's run. The stage bar at the top fills as you advance toward the finish; no other buttons appeared during the playtest, so the whole loop stays one-tap simple. A settings gear sits in the corner of the start screen if you want to adjust anything first.

Tips

  • - Wait for the 'touch to play' hand icon before tapping — the boat only starts once Stage 1 is on screen, so tapping through the loading screens does nothing.

  • - The gear icon on the start screen opens settings — worth a quick check before your first tap.
