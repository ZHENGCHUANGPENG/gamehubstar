How to Play

Press the left key for a small jump and the right key for a big jump, as the posted key list puts it. The menu shows the red hero with a finger to its lips and a green Play button on a black background. The menu and Play button appear the same way on a phone, and tapping Play starts the run.

Tips

  • Small jumps for close asteroids, big jumps for wide gaps
  • Skip the hot asteroids the listing warns about
  • Keep moving, the evil Red is right behind
  • Watch for burning comets on the way
