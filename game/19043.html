123 Squid Game Challenge Jigsaw Online

123 Squid Game Challenge Jigsaw is a jigsaw collection built on scenes from Squid Game, and it opens on the grid itself: twelve picture tiles in four rows on a pale wooden background, under an orange banner of four pink-hooded guards whose masks carry a square, a triangle, a circle and an umbrella. The first tile is open, marked with a green check, showing the doll in the yellow dress between two guards, and the tile beside it shows a dalgona cookie with a play mark. The other ten tiles sit behind white padlocks, with a pause button at the top right beside a small gamepad icon.

The pluses, and the minuses: the grid is honest about what you get, with twelve named scenes, one open tile, and padlocks that open as earlier puzzles are finished. The pictures are bright, the tiles are big enough to read, and the upright layout fits the grid on a phone. The minus is the start, because only one of twelve tiles is open, so the first minutes are a single puzzle, and the trial never saw pieces being dragged, so the jigsaw itself is still unproven. A fan of the show will recognise every scene.

Best for Squid Game fans and quiet jigsaw players who like working through a picture set one tile at a time. Skip it if you want every puzzle open from the start or fast arcade action.

How to Play

With a mouse, click a tile to open its puzzle, then click and drag the pieces into place. On a phone, a finger does the same work: tap the tile and drag the pieces, the way the instructions list it: tap or click. Start with the first tile, the one with the green check, and finish it to unlock the next padlock. Work the edges of the puzzle first so the picture takes shape, and tap the pause button at the top right whenever you want to stop.

Tips

  • Start with the first tile, the green check marks the open one
  • Locked tiles open as earlier puzzles are finished
  • The dalgona cookie tile sits in the top row
  • Tap the pause button toward the top right to stop
