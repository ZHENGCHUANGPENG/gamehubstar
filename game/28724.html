Rescue from Rainbow Monster Online Online

A brown bear sits tied up on a rope, a blue crowned monster and a green monster wait nearby, and a portal door stands behind them. That is Rescue from Rainbow Monster Online, a casual puzzle game built on the cut-the-rope idea. One tap on the orange PLAY button opens a SELECT LEVEL grid with 35 levels, the first one open with a checkmark and the rest locked behind padlocks, plus a MORE RAINBOW GAMES button and an UPGRADE button below. The listing says a small impostor is tied up and confined to the room, and cutting the rope rescues him and collects unique skins, and the screen fits that picture: the rope, the tied-up character and the monsters are all drawn in bright cartoon colors. One tap and slide does it on a phone, a mouse drag on a desktop. Playing it is simply this: look at the rope, cut it, free the character.

How to Play

Use the mouse to cut the rope and free the character on a desktop, per the posted note. On a phone tap and slide to cut the rope. The screens show the title art with the tied-up brown bear and the monsters, then a SELECT LEVEL grid with level 1 open and the rest locked.

Tips

  • Cut the rope in the right order, the bear drops where you cut
  • Level 1 is open first, the rest unlock as you finish
  • Watch where the monsters stand before you cut
  • A slide across the rope is the whole move
