Grab.io Online

The menu says everything in one line: draw, enclose, conquer the disc. Below it the instructions spell out the loop, steer with the mouse or drag on a phone, leave your land, trace a loop and return to your own colour to capture the area inside, and grab stars by enclosing them. The top bar keeps the stakes visible, Land at zero percent, Best at zero percent, zero stars and three lives, and the warning that touching any trail ends the run at once makes every loop a small gamble. The posted description calls it a fast land-grab arcade game on a round arena packed with hungry bots, and the menu backs that up with the star counter and the lives readout. It is the paper.io formula with a round board, and the loop is easy to understand in seconds. A quick arena session needs one skill only, drawing a loop and coming home alive.

How to Play

Steer with the mouse on a computer, and on a phone drag anywhere to set the direction, as the menu itself puts it. The menu reads Draw, enclose, conquer the disc, with a Play button and a Skins and Fills button below it. The top bar shows Land at zero percent, Best at zero percent, zero stars and three lives, and the instructions warn that touching any trail ends the run at once.

Tips

  • Draw small loops near your land first, they are the safest claims
  • Grab stars by enclosing them, they add bonus points
  • Loop back into your own colour to capture the area inside
  • Never cross a trail, yours or an enemy, one touch ends the run
