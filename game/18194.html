Noob Huggy Online

Noob Huggy is a blocky platformer where a blue Huggy has to cross a pixel world, dodge the slimes, grab the key and reach the door. The level is a bright pixel scene under a light blue sky: a green pixel tree on brown dirt, a strip of green grass, and a blue blocky Huggy with a red mouth and yellow feet on the left. LEVEL 1 and a zero score sit at the top, beside a speaker icon and a pause button. To the right of Huggy sit a small green slime, a gold key and a row of gold coins, with brown arrow buttons at the bottom for moving and jumping.

What is good here, and what is not: the goal is clear from the first screen, with the slime, the key, the coins and the door all visible at once, and the touch buttons are large enough for thumbs. The pixel look is bright and reads at a glance. The downsides are scope, because the slime is the only enemy seen and only LEVEL 1 was open, so later levels are unproven, and the jump needs timing, since a slime ends the attempt. A fan of blocky platformers will feel at home at once.

Made for platformer fans, Huggy fans and spare-minute players after a short level to clear in a single sitting. Many enemies, long levels and deep movesets all live somewhere else.

How to Play

On a desktop, move with WASD or the arrow keys and jump to dodge the slimes. On a phone, use the on-screen left and right arrows at the bottom left and the jump arrow at the bottom right, as the screen shows, and the posted instructions add that double jump is active. Grab the gold key before heading for the door, and jump over the green slime instead of walking into it. Collect the gold coins along the grass, and use the pause button at the upper right to stop the level.

Tips

  • Grab the gold key before heading for the door
  • Jump over the green slime instead of walking into it
  • Collect the gold coins along the grass
  • The pause button at the upper right stops the level
