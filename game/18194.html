Noob Huggy Online

The level is a bright pixel world under a light blue sky: a green pixel tree on brown dirt, a strip of green grass, and a blue blocky Huggy with a red mouth and yellow feet standing on the left. LEVEL 1 and a zero score sit at the top, a speaker icon at the top left, and a pause button in the top right. To the right of Huggy sit a small green slime, a gold key, and a row of gold coins. At the bottom left are two brown buttons with white arrows for moving left and right, and at the bottom right is a brown button with a white up arrow for jumping. The listing calls it a Huggy who must escape the slimes, get the key, and open the door to the next level, and the trial saw exactly that: slime, key, coins, and clear touch buttons. The layout is wide, so the game sits in a landscape strip on a phone, and the controls are large enough for thumbs.

How to Play

On a desktop, move with WASD or the arrow keys and jump to dodge the slimes. On a phone, use the on-screen left and right arrows at the bottom left and the jump arrow at the bottom right, as the screen shows, and the posted instructions add that double jump is active.

Tips

  • ['Grab the gold key before heading for the door', 'Jump over the green slime instead of walking into it', 'Collect the gold coins along the grass', 'The pause button at the upper right stops the level']
