Storm Dash Online

The storm never appears on screen during the menus — but its economy does. Storm Dash introduces itself as an endless runner with a simple creed: 'Twenty-five worlds. One storm. Don't stop running.' Around that promise sits a surprisingly complete progression shell. A daily reward ladder pays 50 coins on Day 1 and climbs to 1000 by Day 7, with the card bluntly asking you to come back tomorrow to keep the streak. The upgrades tab offers Magnet (extending its pull from 6 to 8 seconds at level 1, priced at 150 coins) and Storm Breaker (an unstoppable rush growing from 3.0 to 3.8 seconds), each with five visible levels. Missions feed a 225-star track — the observed one asks players to bounce on 3 springs — and a rank multiplier ticks the coin earnings upward. That is a lot of structure for an arcade runner, and it is all verified in the playtest's own interface text. The honest limitation is platform: the game asks for landscape orientation, and in portrait only the menus were reachable, so mobile players will need to rotate or switch to desktop. Within that frame, Storm Dash reads as a runner that wants to be a habit — the running may be endless, but the daily card is the real boss fight.

How to Play

Tap PLAY on the main menu to start an endless run, and CLAIM on the daily reward card to collect your streak coins. The menu asks players to rotate the phone sideways for the full view — in portrait, only the menus were reachable during the playtest. A gear icon opens settings, and tabs for UPGRADES, HEROES, and MISSIONS sit on the main screen between runs.

Tips

  • - Claim the daily reward every day — Day 1 pays 50 coins and the streak climbs toward 1000 by Day 7.
  • - The first Magnet upgrade costs 150 coins and stretches its pull from 6 to 8 seconds — the cheapest real power spike.
  • - Chase missions like 'bounce on 3 springs'; they feed the 225-star progression track.
  • - Rotate the phone sideways as the menu asks — portrait mode only reached the menus in testing.
