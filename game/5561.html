A green striped pitch fills the instructions screen, Movement and Jump arrows on the left beside matching keyboard keys, a Space bar labelled Kick below, and a Power Ups panel on the right showing eight icons: a bottle for Glued, ice for Freeze, a face for Grow and another for Shrink, a spring shoe for Jump Higher, a boot for Speed Up, and big and small balls. A green Back to game button waits at the bottom. The developer description promises soccer scored with feet, head and superpowers, and the visuals verify the superpower aspect on its own, with a power-up for nearly every situation. It is a cheerful arcade take on the sport, with the rules taught before the first whistle rather than during it.

How to Play

Desktop players move and jump with the arrow keys and kick with the Space bar, exactly as the on-screen instructions show.

Tips

  • Learn one power-up at a time instead of chasing them all, since each changes the ball differently.
  • Jump Higher and Speed Up suit an attacking run, while Freeze and Glued suit stopping one.
  • Kick with Space only when the ball sits in front of you, because a wild kick hands the ball away.
