Evil Granny: City Terror Online

Evil Granny: City Terror sets its mood before a single shot. The menu is a night street: grey apartment towers on both sides, a long rusted fence across the middle, a small fire burning in a barrel on the left, and an empty road running away into the dark. Over that scene sit three black-and-gold buttons, START GAME, CONTROLS and CREDITS, with a volume slider and a speaker icon below them and a red More Games button in the corner. The developer's write-up presents the premise as being trapped in a city hunted by a granny and her puppets, with escape as the goal. The menu sells the trapped half of that story well: fences, firelight, tall buildings, and no open road out.

How to Play

On a computer, walk with WASD, look with the mouse, fire with the left mouse button, aim with the right mouse button, change weapons with the mouse wheel, throw grenades with G, reload with R, pick up items with F, and run with Left Shift, according to the listed controls.

Tips

  • Open CONTROLS from the menu before you start, so the key list is fresh in your head.
  • Aim with the right mouse button before firing, instead of spraying from the hip in a dark street.
  • Reload early, while the street is quiet, rather than waiting until a fight forces the issue.
