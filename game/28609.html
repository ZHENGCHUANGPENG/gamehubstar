Legendary Soccer Online

Legendary Soccer is a turn-based soccer game where each side fields three players and the whole match runs on drags. The title screen shows two cartoon star faces over a night stadium, a red-shirted striker on the left and a light-blue-shirted one on the right, with yellow 1 Player and 2 Player buttons below. Picking 1 Player opens a green pitch with goals at both ends, three red player discs on the left, three light-blue discs on the right, the ball sitting at the centre spot, and a 0:0 score at the top. The description calls it a game for soccer lovers of all ages, and the pitch backs that up: big discs, a clear ball, and nothing to learn before the first move.

The controls are one gesture repeated. Grab one of the three players, drag toward the destination, and release; the player walks there and the turn passes to the other side. On a computer the drag is a mouse pull, and on a phone it is a finger pull on the same disc. The instructions say to hold a player and pull towards the ball to shoot legendary goals, and both sides take turns making moves, so every drag is a small decision about which disc to spend. The 1 Player mode plays against the computer, while 2 Player puts a friend on the other side of the same screen.

What works best is how readable the pitch is. The discs are large, the ball is always visible at the centre, and the 0:0 score never leaves the top of the screen, so the state of the match is obvious at a glance. The weak point is the pace: because each side moves only one disc per turn, a match moves in small steps and the waiting between your own moves can feel slow, especially against the computer. The title screen also explains nothing beyond the two buttons, so the drag itself has to be discovered from the instructions rather than shown.

Best for soccer fans who like short tactical matches and for two players sharing one screen. It suits quick games on a phone or a computer, anyone who enjoys turn-based play instead of real-time running, and players who want a soccer game they can pick up without learning buttons.

How to Play

On a computer, grab one of the three player discs with the mouse, drag it toward where it should go, and release to move it there. On a phone, drag a disc with a finger the same way. Both sides take turns making moves, so after your drag the other side moves one of its discs. The 1 Player button starts a match against the computer, the 2 Player button starts a match against a friend, and the home button at the top right leaves the pitch.
