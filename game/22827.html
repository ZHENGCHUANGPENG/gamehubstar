ParkourCraft Online

This is the most complete parkour package in the batch. Ten maps with a real difficulty curve, a chest-and-emerald reward loop, and speedrun sharing give it structure that most voxel jump games lack. The screenshot's lava world is vivid and readable, the platforms pop against the orange, which is the thing that matters when jumps are pixel-precise. The description's pitch, show off your parkour skills, is earned by the design: the maps are the content, not the movement tech. Emerald collection adds a completionist layer for players who clear maps and want a reason to return. The cute pixel-art note in the description undersells it; the look is functional first, and functional is what parkour needs. A strong pick for the genre faithful.

How to Play

【电脑专属，需键盘鼠标，手机无法操作】WASD moves, the mouse looks, space jumps, and left-click interacts, with the golden chest as the interact target at each level's end. The rules are simple: reach the chest, don't fall in lava. Ten maps means ten escalating routing puzzles. Speedrun timers reward the committed.

Tips

  • Sprint-jump the long lava gaps; walking jumps fall short.
  • Open every chest; the emerald count is your real score.
  • Replay maps for time once you know the route; the first clear is just reconnaissance.
