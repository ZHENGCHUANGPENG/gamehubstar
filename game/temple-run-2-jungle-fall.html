Temple Run 2: Jungle Fall Online

Temple Run 2: Jungle Fall is the classic endless runner that defined the genre on mobile, now playable in your browser. You play a treasure hunter sprinting through a jungle temple with demonic apes in hot pursuit. The premise is simple — run as far as you can — but the execution is what made this a phenomenon. The controls are intuitive: A/D or arrow keys to steer left and right, W or up to jump, S or down to slide under obstacles. As your speed increases, the game throws more at you — gaps to leap, low barriers to slide under, sharp turns, and venus flytraps waiting to snap. One mistake and the apes catch you, ending the run. What keeps it compelling is the progression layered on top. Coins collected during runs feed into upgrades, the multiplier increases your score rate, and the daily challenge (run 3,000 meters for escalating rewards across five days) gives a reason to come back. The stats screen tracks your highest score, longest run, and lifetime totals, turning every attempt into a small competition against yourself. The jungle temple setting looks great — crumbling stone paths, orange autumn foliage, and that constant sense of forward momentum. If you've never played Temple Run, this is the definitive version to start with. If you have, you already know the drill: how far can you get before the temple claims you?

How to Play

On desktop, press the left and right arrow keys or A and D to turn, up arrow or W to jump, and down arrow or S to slide under barriers. On touch screens, swipe left or right to turn, swipe up to jump, and swipe down to slide. Tilt controls are not used here, so every move is a deliberate swipe or keypress, which keeps the controls readable at high speed.

Tips

  • Save your tilt-free turns for the forked paths after the 500-meter mark, where the track splits and the wrong choice drops you into a flytrap pit.
  • Grab the magnet power-up only when coin trails get dense, usually past the second waterfall, so it pays for itself.
  • When the apes close in after a stumble, stop chasing coins for ten seconds and run clean lines to shake them off.
  • The slide-jump combo clears the double log barriers faster than jumping twice, which most players discover too late.
