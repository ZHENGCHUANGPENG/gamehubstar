How to Play

On desktop, jump with a left mouse click, as the posted instructions note. On a phone, touch the screen to jump instead, and time each tap to the walls ahead, since the note frames the game as jumping at the right moment.

Tips

  • Tap once per wall at first, since the note stresses jumping at the right time rather than tapping constantly.
  • Watch the red character against the green ground, because its position tells you when the next wall is coming.
  • Treat bonuses and coins as a bonus on the way up, not the reason to risk a jump.
