Cookie Clicker Online

Cookie Clicker is the clicker game about baking cookies, here in version 2.052. The screen is simple on purpose: one big chocolate-chip cookie on the left, a counter above it reading 0 cookies with a per-second rate of 0, and a Store panel on the right. A line at the top sets the joke: "You feel like making cookies. But nobody wants to eat your cookies." The bakery gets a fresh random name on every visit, so one load calls it one thing and the next load calls it another. A language box can appear on the first visit, offering thirteen languages from English and French to Japanese, Chinese and Korean.

The controls are one action repeated. Desktop players click the big cookie to bake cookies; on a phone held sideways, tap the same cookie. Each click adds to the counter, and the per-second rate beside it shows what the bakery earns on its own. The Store on the right lists its first items as question marks, priced at 15 and 100 cookies, so the first goal is clear: click enough to afford the cheapest one, then keep going.

Its honesty is the best part. There is no tutorial to sit through, the cookie is huge and impossible to miss, and the counter, the rate and the Store are all on one screen. The bare opening is the weak spot: the counter starts at zero, the rate starts at zero, the Store items are hidden behind question marks, and the first minute is only clicking, so a player who wants action at once will have to wait for the numbers to grow.

This one suits idle-game fans and anyone who likes watching numbers climb. Players after fast reflexes or a story will find the cookie quiet.

How to Play

Mouse click or tap to play. On a computer, click the big cookie on the left to bake cookies, and watch the counter and the per-second rate climb at the top. On a phone held sideways, tap the same cookie; the Store panel stays on the right. A language box may appear on the first visit, with thirteen languages to pick from, including English, French, German, Japanese, Chinese and Korean. The Store lists its first items as question marks, priced at 15 and 100 cookies.
