Squid Soccer Game Online

Squid Soccer Game wears its theme on its sleeve. The menu is a golden-brown pitch pattern with the title stacked in white and hot pink, a pink-suited guard with a triangle mask holding a ball on the right, and three small guards lined up along the bottom. Down the left edge run the three shapes of the contest: triangle, square and circle. Between the title and the guard sit two buttons, PLAY GAME in green and WON MEDALS in blue, with a BEST: 0 strip above them. The official description frames the job as a free-kick challenge: you are the goalkeeper, shots keep coming, and your task is to stop them. The menu backs that up with nothing else to learn, which is the point: pick the green button and start saving.

How to Play

On a computer, use the mouse to play, clicking PLAY GAME to start and moving to meet each shot. On a phone, play with touch in the same way, tapping the screen to dive toward the ball.

Tips

  • Start from the PLAY GAME button in the middle of the menu, not from the medals button below it.
  • Watch the ball, not the shooter, because the shot direction is what decides where you need to dive.
  • Cover the middle first and only stretch wide when the shot clearly heads for a corner.
