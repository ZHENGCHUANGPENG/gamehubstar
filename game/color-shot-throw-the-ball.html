How to Play

The whole game is one tap: press anywhere on the screen to throw the ball toward the ring, and the throw only lands when the colors line up. Timing is the entire skill, because a mistimed tap wastes the shot. Two large buttons anchor the bottom of the screen — a home icon to leave the session and a circular arrow to restart the run. The Unity WebGL build showed a mobile-compatibility warning during testing, so a desktop browser will give the steadier frame rate this timing game demands.

Tips

  • - Watch the big center numeral before tapping — it marks your throwing window, not decoration.
  • - Keep your finger hovering near the screen center so the tap lands the instant the colors match.
  • - Use the restart arrow freely; short runs are cheap and the muscle memory builds fast.
  • - The mobile build showed a compatibility warning, so prefer a desktop browser for precise timing.
