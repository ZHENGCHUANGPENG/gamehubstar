Pig Bros Adventure Online

The title screen is a charming night poster built around three pigs. A huge pink moon fills the upper right of a purple sky, with a dragon silhouette flying across it and soft clouds drifting below. Three round cartoon pigs stand on a rocky ledge: a pale pig at the left, a big brown pig in the middle with its back turned, and a spotted pink pig at the right. The Chinese title runs across the top in bold yellow and orange characters, and four gold round buttons sit in the bottom-right corner, the top one carrying a gamepad symbol and the others showing one, two and three little figures. A music note button hangs at the upper right and to43.com is written at the bottom left. The listing sends the pig brothers out to explore the countryside and collect the gems they find there.

The control scheme is a shared keyboard for up to three players, as the instructions list it. Player one moves with A and D and jumps with W, player two takes the arrow keys, and player three moves with J and L and jumps with I. Click the gold gamepad button to start, then pick a player-count button for the run. That layout suits a couch session, since each player owns one corner of the keyboard and no one needs the mouse once the game begins.

What works best here is the character art: three distinct pigs, a pink moon and a dragon silhouette give the title a storybook mood in one glance, and the four gold buttons make the multiplayer promise visible before play. The drawback is how little the title proves about the adventure, since no countryside, no gem and no platform appears on it, so the exploration and the collecting rest on the listing. The phone load is also a real limit, stalling at a low percentage and ending on a black screen, which is why the game stays on computers despite the touch-friendly look.

Co-op platform fans, siblings and friends who share one keyboard will like the three-pig start and the night-sky art. Phones are out of the question, and so is any run by a solo player who wants a level map up front, or needs the gem collecting visible before pressing play.
