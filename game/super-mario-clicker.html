Super Mario Clicker Online

Super Mario Clicker drops you onto one busy screen and never wastes a moment: Mario grins in the center, the star counter sits at the top, and an upgrades panel holds every way to grow your income. The loop is exactly what the description promises. Tap Mario and your star count climbs; spend stars on items like the Clicker bot that adds automatic clicks, the Mushroom that raises per-second earnings, or the Fire Flower that multiplies star power, and your idle income keeps rising on its own. Locked silhouettes of familiar friends like Yoshi, Peach, and Bowser stand in the background as the long-term carrot, each one another milestone to chase. Where point and click escape games reward slow, careful exploration, this is built for short bursts and steady accumulation, with a visible game-progress bar and stars-per-second readout showing whether your setup is improving. The x1 multiplier slider on the side hints at bulk-buying upgrades, and the save icon means you can return to your progress later. The art is bright and cartoonish, all chunky buttons and cheery colors, and the controls never ask for more than a finger. For a free clicker you can play in a browser tab, Super Mario Clicker delivers the satisfying rhythm-based tapping that the genre lives on: tap, upgrade, watch the numbers climb, a zero-friction alternative when point and click escape games feel too demanding.

How to Play

You play entirely with taps, clicks, or touches, as the in-game tutorial spells out: touch the Mario to increase your money. Buy upgrades by tapping their rows in the upgrades panel, which lists each item with its star cost. Arrow buttons beside the panel scroll through more upgrades, a slider on the right adjusts the purchase multiplier, and the floppy-disk icon at the top left saves your progress.

Tips

  • Follow the on-screen tutorial first: tap Mario directly to build up stars before spending anything
  • Buy the cheapest upgrades first, since even small per-second income keeps ticking while you watch
  • Scroll the upgrades list to see the full row of purchasable helpers before deciding what to buy next
  • Check the GAME PROGRESS bar and stars-per-second readout to tell whether your income engine is actually growing
