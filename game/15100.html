Zumba Ocean Online

Zumba Ocean is a bubble-shooting game set under the sea. The title screen opens on the words ZUMBA OCEAN in big yellow and white bubble letters above a cheerful yellow pirate fish in a brown hat and an eyepatch, holding a pink gem over an open treasure chest full of gold coins. Blue rocks, pink corals and green seaweed frame the scene. The listing says the game plays like the Zuma series: match three or more same-coloured gems to clear them and destroy all the jewels in the chain before it reaches the end, with three stars to win on each level.

The controls are clicks or taps. Computer players press the big orange play button, then click to aim and shoot gems at the chain. On a phone, tap the same spots; the title screen is built for a phone in portrait, with a red sound button on the left of the play button and a red info button on the right. The first level asks you to destroy all the jewels in the chain, reach 500 points and make more than three combos, so the target is clear before the first shot.

The charm is the look: the pirate fish, the treasure chest and the bright blue water make a familiar match-three idea feel cheerful, and the level targets are shown before play so you know what to aim for. The weak spots are the usual ones for the genre. The chain keeps moving while you aim, the title screen is the whole first impression, and the game shows no board until the play button is pressed, so the first seconds are guesswork about the layout.

Bright, simple bubble shooters are the audience here. A slow puzzle with no moving chain is a different game entirely.
