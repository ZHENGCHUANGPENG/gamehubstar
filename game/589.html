Top Shootout: The Saloon Online

The loading screen sets the scene before a shot is fired: a hand-drawn western saloon in wood tones, with wanted posters on the walls and the title over the door. The developer's description gives the job in one breath: evil bandits have kidnapped hostages and are keeping them in the saloon, and only you can save them. So the loop is simple and urgent: reload your gun, shoot the bandits, and take down their bosses. There is no walking, no cover to hide behind, and no map to learn, just a room full of targets and a decision with every click.

The hostage rule is what makes this one work: because a hostage stands beside the bandits, every shot is a choice rather than a race to click fastest, and that turns a plain gallery shooter into something that asks for patience. The bosses give each wave a shape, and the saloon artwork has more character than the genre's usual clip-art. What holds it back is the ceiling: a veteran of light-gun games will find no new challenge here, the loop is shoot and reload and nothing else, and the game stays on a computer, with no phone play.

Best for players who want a quick-draw western time-killer in short desktop bursts, a few waves between other things, not for players after movement, a story, or a long campaign.

How to Play

Play it on a computer with the mouse. The listed instructions give a single action, click to shoot and recharge, and there is no movement to learn, so the whole skill is aiming and choosing targets. Aim at the bandits first and save shots for their bosses, who take more hits than the regular gunmen. Never click a hostage: the saloon holds kidnapped hostages alongside the bandits, and a careless shot costs more than a miss. Start from the play button on the western loading screen, then work through each wave at your own pace, reloading during the lulls when no bandit is drawing.

Tips

  • Save shots for the bosses, who soak up hits that could clear the regular bandits.
  • Reload during the lulls, not while a bandit is mid-draw.
  • Check every target twice, because a hostage beside a bandit punishes a careless click.
