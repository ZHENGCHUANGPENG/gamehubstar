Plants vs Zombies Online

Forget the classic lane layout you know — this version rebuilds the plant-versus-zombie formula as a winding-road tower defense on a colorful cartoon map. The trial session opens on a map screen where a zombie horde gathers on a hill, defense tiles glow along the blacktop, and a tutorial bubble tells you a giant wave is coming and to buy the first two Peashooter plants. That is the whole game in miniature: economy first, placement second, survival third. Two Peashooters are already on tiles in the screenshots, one upgraded to Lv.2, which hints at the real decision layer — where to invest limited coins as waves escalate. The bottom bar is surprisingly busy for a simple game: a coin purse, a plant roster, timed x2 damage and x2 gold multipliers, and a shop button that starts locked. The objective counter ('Destroy 1, 0/1') and three area tabs suggest a campaign structure beyond a single endless wave. Visually it leans bright and goofy, with the enemy hill boss looming pink and ridiculous over the whole scene. What the session never shows is an actual wave hitting the defenses, so the moment-to-moment combat is unverified — but the setup screen is legible, the tutorial is clear, and the upgrade path is visible. For tower defense fans who like their strategy served cute, this one has the right bones.

How to Play

Everything runs on taps — the captured session never shows a keyboard or gesture hint. Tap the plant button at the bottom of the screen to buy (the tutorial highlights buying the first two Peashooters), then place them on the glowing defense tiles along the road. The bottom bar holds the economy: coin count, damage multiplier, gold multiplier, and a shop button. A hand cursor in the screenshots guides the first purchase, and the map screen lets you switch between the three area tabs at the top.

Tips

  • - The tutorial's advice is sound: buying the first two Peashooter plants before the wave arrives gives you early coverage on the road bends.
  • - One Peashooter in the shot is already Lv.2 — upgrading a plant on a key tile beats spreading weak plants across the whole road.
  • - The bottom bar shows x2 damage and x2 gold multipliers with timers, so plan wave defense around when those boosts are active.
  • - Watch the coin counter before buying: the session shows 10 coins, so spend early on plants rather than saving for the locked shop button.
