How to Play

This is a tap-and-drag game: tap bugs to smack them, or keep your finger down and drag — the LOCKER screen's 'STOMP AND KEEP DRAGGING' hint and the yellow drag line under the shoe confirm continuous stomping works. The shoe follows your finger as the cursor. Between rounds, the LOCKER lets you pick footwear, and CLOSE returns you to the action.

Tips

  • Keep your finger down and sweep — drag-stomping clears clusters far faster than individual taps.
  • Clear dense bug patches first; chasing lone stragglers at the edges wastes the timer.
  • Watch the top-bar timer and spend the last seconds mopping up easy targets.
  • Check the LOCKER between rounds — better shoes change your stomp width, so match footwear to your style.
