UNO Card Game Online

UNO Card Game is the classic card game, and it starts with a bit of dressing up. The opening screen is a purple profile page with six cartoon superhero avatars to choose from, a field to enter a name, and a green CONTINUE button. Once the profile is set, the lobby shows the big UNO WITH BUDDIES logo, the chosen avatar and name in the corner, and two green buttons: VS COMPUTER and ONLINE MULTIPLAYER. The listing calls it simple to learn with some strategic depth, and the lobby matches that idea: pick a mode and the match begins.

The controls are clicks and taps. Computer players click an avatar, click the name field and type a name, click CONTINUE, then click VS COMPUTER for a solo match or ONLINE MULTIPLAYER to play with others. Phone players tap the same spots and hold the phone sideways, since the profile and lobby are built for a wide screen and the profile is cropped on an upright phone. The game is a slow Unity load, so the screen can stay black for half a minute on a phone before the profile appears.

The best part is the setup itself: choosing an avatar and a name takes seconds and makes the lobby feel personal, and having both a computer mode and an online mode on the same screen is handy. What holds it back is the wait and the ads. The black loading screen is long on a phone, an ad grid appears between the profile and the lobby on a computer, and the profile asks for a name before CONTINUE will work, which is not obvious at first. The match itself only starts after the mode choice, so the first minute is setup rather than cards.

UNO fans and casual card players who want a quick match against the computer or online will enjoy it. Players who want to be dealt in immediately should expect a minute of setup first.
