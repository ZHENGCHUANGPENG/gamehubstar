FNF FPS Online

FNF FPS takes Friday Night Funkin into a 3D street shooter, and the menu is a city street at dusk. A MEGA MALL sign stands to the left, a HARDWARE sign at the center, and a green truck waits behind them. Across the top, in pink and white graffiti letters reads Friday Night Funkin FPS, with three buttons in green frames below: START, Controls and More Games. The street is crowded: the red-haired girlfriend on a speaker stack, the blue-haired boyfriend with a microphone, a skeleton, a pumpkin-headed character in a green cap, and a lemon-headed one in black. The listing calls it a 3D shooter: kill the bad guys, save the girlfriend.

The strong points, and the thin spots: the cast is the draw, with the whole FNF gang standing in one street scene, the graffiti title is loud and colourful, and START is the brightest button, so starting is obvious. The Controls button is there for anyone who wants the keys first. The catch is the menu itself: no shooting ever appeared in the trial, and this is a fan-made crossover, not an official FNF game, which shows in the mix of characters. One mall street is the backdrop, so the shooting must carry the variety.

Best for FNF fans and desktop shooter players after a quick street fight with familiar faces. Avoid it if the rhythm game, official content or phone play is what you want.

How to Play

Keyboard and mouse only: move with WASD, shoot with the left mouse button, aim with the right button, reload with R, run with Shift, and jump with Space, according to the posted instructions. Tap START to open the first street fight, and open Controls first to see every key before the shooting starts. Reload with R before the street gets crowded, and run with Shift to reach cover much faster.
