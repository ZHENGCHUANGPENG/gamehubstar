Among Space Escape Online

Among Space Escape is a space-themed action runner that borrows the crewmate look everyone recognises and drops its hero somewhere far more dangerous: deep space, with a red pursuer on his tail. The setup is a pure runner: leap from asteroid to asteroid, steer clear of the hot ones, dodge burning comets, and never let the evil Red catch you. The title screen sells it with cartoon charm, a grinning green one-eyed planet, a ringed orange giant, Earth glowing below, and one big green play button dead centre. Speed climbs as the run goes on, so readable hazards and a chaser that never stops are the whole pressure system.

The strength is the simplicity: a game you can explain in five seconds and play for twenty minutes, wrapped in friendly cartoon visuals that make every hazard easy to read. The escalating speed and the pursuer keep the tension up without any learning curve. The gap is the test session itself, which never got past the title screen, so the later asteroids and the comet patterns come from the developer's description rather than from play. Two inputs also means little variety once the novelty of the jumps wears off.

Best for quick-session players and anyone who wants a five-second runner for a school break or a short gap between tasks. Less suited to upgrade hunters and level grinders; the run barely changes from one attempt to the next.

How to Play

Tap the big green play button in the centre of the title screen and you are in. The entire game runs on two inputs: Left triggers a small jump and Right triggers a big jump. Timing is the whole skill, so learn which jump each gap needs before you commit. Save the big jump for wide gaps and use small hops on tight asteroid chains, where one overshoot ends the run. Glance at the red pursuer's position before a risky leap, and learn what the hot asteroids look like early.

Tips

  • Save the big jump for wide gaps; small hops keep you safe on tight asteroid chains.
  • Glance at the red pursuer's position before committing to a risky leap.
  • Learn what the hot asteroids look like early — recognizing them instantly saves runs.
