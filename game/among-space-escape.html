Borrowing the crewmate look everyone recognizes, Among Space Escape drops its hero into a much more dangerous neighborhood: deep space, with a red pursuer on his tail. How to Play

The title screen centers on one large green play button — tap it and you are in. Per the developer, the entire game runs on two inputs: Left triggers a small jump, Right triggers a big jump. Timing which jump to use, and when, is the whole skill. There are no complex combos to memorize; two buttons carry the full game.

Tips

  • - Save the big jump for wide gaps; small hops keep you safe on tight asteroid chains.
  • - Glance at the red pursuer's position before committing to a risky leap.
  • - Learn what the hot asteroids look like early — recognizing them instantly saves runs.
