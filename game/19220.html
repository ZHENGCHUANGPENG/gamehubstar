Red and Blue Stickman Rope Online

Dark brick fills level one, with a heart counter and a zero score at the top and pause, sound and home buttons in the corner. On the left stand two stickmen, one blue and one red, beneath floating key diagrams that assign WASD to one and arrows to the other. Between them and the spiked door on the right sit a golden key, three glowing blue diamonds and a patch of floor spikes. The developer description calls them two cute brothers who must fetch a key and reach the door together, and the screen shows that exact errand: two figures, one key, one door, and hazards in between. The on-screen arrow pads at the bottom hint at an alternative input, but the keyboard diagrams above the brothers are the real instruction.

How to Play

【电脑专属，需键盘鼠标，手机无法操作】Move the blue brother with WASD and the red brother with the arrow keys, exactly as the diagrams on screen show, and note that a double jump is listed as available; two keyboard sets are the whole point here.

Tips

  • Move the blue brother with WASD and the red brother with the arrows, keeping each hand on its own set.
  • Grab the glowing key before heading for the spiked door, since the door is the exit, not the prize.
  • Step around the floor spikes and collect the diamonds on a safe line, rather than jumping blindly for them.
