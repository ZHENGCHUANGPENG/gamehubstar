DungeonCraft Online

A grid of blue tiles fills the screen, sixteen of them, and only the first one is open. That is DungeonCraft, a dungeon crawler that starts by showing the whole map as a set of locked doors. The open tile carries a green gem, the rest carry padlocks, and the blocky stone background behind the grid sets the Minecraft style at once. Per the listing, the game is a dungeon where the player faces green creeper faces to collect blue eggs and keys, shoots them all and finds the door to escape, with many challenging levels to explore. That grid is the full extent of the trial, and it shows the structure clearly: one open level, fifteen locked ones, and a reason to clear them in order. The presentation is simple, the tiles are easy to read, and the locked ones make the progress visible. Open the first tile, clear it, and unlock the next.

How to Play

Arrow keys move and the mouse shoots, the posted keys list. What the trial caught was a four-by-four grid of blue level tiles over a blocky stone background, the first tile marked with a green gem and the rest locked.

Tips

  • Start on the one open tile, the green gem marks it
  • The locked tiles open as levels are cleared
  • Arrows move, the mouse shoots, keep both ready
  • Find the door in each level to escape
