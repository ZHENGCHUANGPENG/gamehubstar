Super Mario Mission Impossible Online

The loading strip is a small platform scene in itself: a blue sky with white clouds, checkered green grass, brown question-mark blocks at the left, a sunflower beside a grey pipe, a wooden crate at the right and a tuft of grass at the edge. SUPER MARIO MISSION IMPOSSIBLE sits in the middle in red, yellow and blue letters with two guns crossed above it, LOADING 100% printed beneath, and a gold PLAY button below that, while a green BEN10ALIENS.NET banner runs along the bottom. The listing changes the familiar hero's job, handing Mario an original gun and sending him against invading robots, with other weapons to pick up along the way, so the platform look hides a shooting game. No robot appears on the loading strip, and no shot is fired in the captured frame, so the fighting rests entirely on the posted description while the strip proves the setting and the entry. What the strip does show is a clean, readable start with one obvious button, and the sideways frame suits the wide grass strip. The twist is the point for anyone who grew up on the platform games: the same blocks and pipes, now with a gun in hand.

How to Play

On a computer, press SPACE to shoot and the up arrow to jump, exactly as the posted keys list them. On a phone, turn it sideways and tap the gold PLAY button to enter; the control note lists keyboard keys only, so the shooting and jumping inputs belong to the desktop pass. Wait for the LOADING line to reach 100% before tapping anything, since the strip is the entry screen.

Tips

  • Shoot from a standing position first, since SPACE is the only attack key the note lists.
  • Jump with the up arrow when a robot or gap blocks the path, rather than walking into it.
  • Watch for other weapons along the route, because the note says collecting them helps clear robots faster.
