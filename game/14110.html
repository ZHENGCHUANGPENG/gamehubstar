Real Car Pro Racing Online

The run-through of Real Car Pro Racing shows a proper city race, not a menu: a yellow sports car fills the foreground, blue, green, purple and red rivals sit on the road ahead, and city buildings and trees line the street. The official description calls it a 3D car riding game with great graphics and vehicle physics, and the screenshot backs that up, with the cars looking solid and the road reading clearly. The racing goal is also clear from the description, finish in the top three to reach the next level, with races getting harder as you go. Nitro is the tool for difficult moments, and the controls give you a camera key and a handbrake key as well, so there is more to learn than just steering. It is a straightforward arcade racer if you enjoy city streets, rival cars and a clear finish target.

How to Play

For computer play, move with the arrow keys or WASD, use N for nitro, C for camera, and SPACE for the handbrake, as the control list says. Avoid obstacles on the roads, and use nitro when you are in a difficult situation in a race.

Tips

  • Save nitro for a straight stretch or a difficult moment, instead of using it the moment the race starts.
  • Watch the rival cars ahead, not just your own car, because the top-three finish is what moves you on.
  • Steer around road obstacles early, since a late swerve costs more speed than an early one.
