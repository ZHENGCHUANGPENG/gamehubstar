Сrossroads Online

Some puzzles hide their rules behind layers of decoration, but this one states its job plainly: fill every empty space with lines and finish the level. The twist that keeps it from being a simple drawing exercise is the value condition, since lines must be moved with a finger or mouse until each line value reaches zero. That single constraint turns placement into a small logic problem, where a line that looks right can still be wrong if its value does not add up. Levels grow harder and more interesting as you progress, according to the description, so the early boards teach the mechanic while later ones demand more careful planning. The portrait layout suits the format, giving a tall board that fits naturally on a phone screen, and touch testing confirmed the game responds to finger input. There is no rush described in the rules, which makes this a good fit for players who prefer to stare at a board, test a route mentally, and only then commit to a move. The satisfaction comes from that moment of completion, when the last gap closes and the values line up. For anyone who enjoys puzzles that reward aim and forethought over reflexes, this is a compact, focused entry in the genre.

How to Play

On a computer, use the mouse to move the lines into the empty spaces until each line value is zero. On mobile, drag the lines with your finger in the same way, which testing confirmed works by touch. Play in portrait orientation on a phone, where the tall board layout fits the screen naturally.

Tips

  • Plan the whole route before moving a line, rather than filling gaps one at a time.
  • Keep the zero-value goal in mind with every move, not just at the end.
  • If a board feels blocked, undo your thinking and look for a different line order.
  • Use the early, easier levels to learn how line values change as lines move.
