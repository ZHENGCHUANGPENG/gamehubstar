Mortal Brothers Survival Friends Online

The title screen is a dark dungeon of grey brick, with an orange dragon emblem at the centre and the words MORTAL BROTHERS SURVIVAL FRIENDS beneath it. A fire fighter in orange stands on the left and an ice fighter in purple on the right, with torii gates and gold coins scattered around the platforms. Pressing the big green play button opens a level map of three orange rows holding fifteen circles, of which only the first is open and the other fourteen carry padlocks. The listing frames the pair as two old enemies who must cooperate to escape the dungeon.

The controls come from the instructions and they are all keyboard. W A S D or the arrow keys move the active character, the space bar swaps between the fire and ice fighters, and the E key moves the platforms. The listing adds the rules that the screen does not show: collect only the flames that match your character, because the wrong fire ends the run at once, and reach the portals together to leave each level alive. A failed level restarts freely, as many times as needed.

The clearest strength is the two-character idea: one pair of hands, two fighters, and puzzles that need both. The level map is easy to read, with one open circle and fourteen locked ones giving a visible goal. What holds it back is how much of the opening is locked: fourteen levels wait behind padlocks from the first minute, and the keyboard-only scheme keeps the game off phones entirely, where a tap on play leaves a white screen. The flame and portal rules also live only in the listing, not on the title screen.

Cooperative puzzle fans and players who like keyboard platformers will enjoy the dungeon escape. Leave it alone on phones, and leave it alone if a game must show its rules on the first screen instead of the listing.
