FNF: Trick or Die Online

A grinning yellow monster with pink teeth stands in a dark room, and behind it a boarded door and a fuse box wait on the wall. That is FNF: Trick or Die, a 3D horror game set in the house of Lemon Demon. The screen shows the controls overlay in the middle: WASD to move, F to interact, hide or use an item, G to drop an item, T to unhide, Escape to pause and C to crouch. The listing describes it as entering the house to rescue Girlfriend from Friday Night Funkin before it is too late, and the screen fits that picture: the dark room, the monster, the boarded door, and a smear of blood on the floor. The art is dark and 3D, and the monster is the only bright thing in the room. A session runs like this: move, hide, use the fuse box, get out.

The overlay is the manual: the full control list, WASD, F, G, T, Escape, and C, is printed on the screen before the first step, so the game teaches itself. The grinning yellow monster is the only bright thing in a dark room, and the boarded door and the fuse box give the first room a clear job. The reservation is the scope: only the controls overlay screen was seen, so the rescue of Girlfriend and the rest of the house are still unplayed. There is no mobile version of this title, so the horror stays on the desktop.

For Friday Night Funkin fans after a 3D horror detour, and for horror players who like a house to explore with a clear rescue mission. Desktop only, and the house beyond the controls screen is still unseen.

How to Play

WASD to move, F to interact, hide or use an item, G to drop an item, T to unhide, Escape to pause and C to crouch, per the on-screen controls overlay. Hide with F when the monster is near, use F on the fuse box and the boarded door, and crouch with C to move quietly past the danger.

Tips

  • Move with WASD, the house is dark and the rooms connect
  • Hide with F when the monster is near
  • Use F on the fuse box and the boarded door
  • Crouch with C to move quietly past the danger
