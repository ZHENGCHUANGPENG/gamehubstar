Gravity football Online

Gravity football is honest about its size: it is a point-and-click physics puzzle, and it does not pretend to be more. The loading screen is plain, the instruction is one line, and the game is the chain of causes that follows a single click. That modesty is appealing, because each scene becomes a small experiment: click a box, watch gravity move things around, and learn what the scene wanted. The football theme gives the puzzle a clear goal to aim for, while the physics gives it surprise, since a box you remove can send things rolling in a direction you did not expect. Touch play is the natural way in, because tapping a box is exactly what the puzzle asks. It is a quiet, short-session game for players who like thinking in causes and effects rather than in reflexes.

How to Play

Point at a box and click it with the mouse on desktop; tap that box on a phone. One action, both devices, no other buttons.

Tips

  • Click the box that changes the most of the scene first, not the one closest to the ball.
  • Watch what falls after each click before clicking again, since gravity does the second half of the work.
  • If a scene looks stuck, try clicking a different box and let the chain reaction play out.
