City Car Stunt 4 Online

Opening it shows a garage with an orange sports car parked in the middle and a menu down the left side: RACING, FREE DRIVING, GARAGE, and MORE STUNTS, with the City Car Stunt 4 logo at the top. That sums up the game, the fourth episode of a stunt-driving series, and the listing describes the same map: collect diamonds, unlock new cars on the huge free-driving map, catch the bonus boxes, and use the blue teleport points to reach the hills faster. The scene runs wide, the car is bright orange against the grey garage floor, and the menu lays out the four modes at once. Control is the keyboard, with two-player support on the same keys, so it remains a desktop game. It is a stunt driver with a garage to show off in and a big map to explore.

How to Play

On a desktop Player 1 moves with the arrow keys and uses N for nitro, Player 2 moves with WASD and uses T for nitro, per the posted instructions.

Tips
