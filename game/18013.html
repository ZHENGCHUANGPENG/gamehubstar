Real Truck Fire Drive Sim Online

A fire-station garage opens the game, with a round platform in the middle and red level cards across the screen. That is Real Truck Fire Drive Sim, a driving simulator that starts by asking which level to take. LEVEL 1 is lit up and LEVEL 2, LEVEL 3 and LEVEL 4 wait beside it, with a BACK button at the top corner and a poster of tools on the wall. The listing describes the game around the fire engine itself: the truck has to be ready for emergencies, positioned under the water tank and filled without going over the limit, because a badly loaded truck is hard to control on the highway. The garage scene is the last screen the trial saw. Its shape is clear: a simulator that starts in the station and heads for the road. Pick a level, take the truck, and mind the brake.

How to Play

WASD drives and SPACE brakes, according to the posted keys. On the screen, the garage with a BACK button and red LEVEL 1, LEVEL 2, LEVEL 3 and LEVEL 4 cards, the first one lit up.

Tips

  • Start on Level 1, the garage cards are the level list
  • WASD steers the truck, SPACE is the brake
  • The truck is the tool for the mission, drive it like one
  • Watch the road, the highway is where control matters
