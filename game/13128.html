Penalty EURO 2021 Online

Penalty shootouts compress football into its tensest moment, and this game leans into that. What greets you first is a stadium scene set up for a kick, with the tournament framing front and centre, and the loop is exactly what the description promises: choose a team, defend your goal, and score when your turn comes. There is no long match to sit through, just the kick itself, which makes it easy to play one shootout and stop, or several in a row. The tap controls suit the format, since a penalty is one decision, not a sequence of them. Choosing a favourite team first gives the shootout a personal stake that a generic kicker never would, and defending your own goal between attempts keeps the tension from going one-way. The tournament framing helps too: every kick feels like it belongs to a bigger occasion, even when you are only playing one shootout. That sense of occasion is what turns a simple mechanic into a game you return to.

How to Play

PC players control it with the mouse. On touch screens, play with taps, aiming and kicking through the same penalty actions. The penalty format keeps every action short: pick your spot, take the kick, and defend the next one.

Tips

  • Vary where you aim instead of shooting at the same spot every time.
  • Watch the goalkeeper's position before choosing your kick, since the setup rewards placement over power.
  • Treat defending as seriously as scoring, because the shootout goes both ways.
  • Play in landscape on your phone so the goal and the stadium stay fully in view.
