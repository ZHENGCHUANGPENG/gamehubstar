Zoo Mahjongg Deluxe Online

The phone screen caught a loader, not a board: a white strip framed in brown, LOFGAMES.COM printed across the top, a spotted cartoon dog with its tongue out in the middle, and a black START button underneath. That strip is the whole visible evidence, and it sits wide, which is why it reads as a landscape game. The developer note supplies the rest of the frame, a tile game where identical tiles leave in pairs until nothing is left, and the animal tag fits the dog on the loader rather than anything seen in play. Nothing on the screen shows a tile, a pair, or a finished board, so the honest picture is a clean entry screen for a matching game whose board only appears after START. For a player who likes quiet pair-matching, the appeal is the simplicity of the premise: two matching tiles, one tap each, and a board that shrinks as pairs leave.

How to Play

On desktop, use the mouse to pick tiles, as the posted control note says. On a phone, the same note lists touch-screen play, and the loader strip sits wide across the screen, so hold the phone sideways and tap the black START button to enter.

Tips

  • Tap START only after the loader strip has fully appeared, since the dog graphic and button mark the ready state.
  • Look for two identical tiles before tapping anything, because the note frames the whole game as pair matching.
  • Clear pairs from the edges of the board first when you can, so later tiles are less likely to stay blocked.
