Noob vs Pro - Armageddon Online

Checking Noob vs Pro - Armageddon is one of the clearest title screens of the day: NOOB VS PRO in big green letters, ARMAGEDDON in yellow underneath, PLAY and CONTINUE buttons in the middle, TNT crates at the bottom, and a sword-wielding blocky character climbing a ladder on a stone wall. The official description frames the game as a trial-filled adventure with traps in caves and deserts, zombies and skeletons to hit for coins, and a cheater to kill and a pro to find. The controls list is long, arrows, Z, X, SPACE, V, B, N and M, which tells you this is a computer game with several weapons and tools, not a one-button game. The pixel art, with its moon, stone walls and TNT, gives the whole thing a playful blocky look. It is a solid fit if you enjoy trial-based adventures with lots of weapons to switch between.

How to Play

Computer play: use the arrow keys to move, Z to go up, X to fire, SPACE for TNT, V for sword, B for the assault rifle, N for the bazooka, and M for maps, as the control list says.

Tips

  • Watch for traps in caves and deserts before moving, because the trials hide them in unexpected places.
  • Use the bazooka for grouped enemies and the rifle for single targets, instead of one weapon for everything.
  • Keep TNT for a blocked path or a thick group, since it is more useful there than on one enemy.
