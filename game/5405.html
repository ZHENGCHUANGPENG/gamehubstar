Cyber City Driver Online

The menu is a neon showroom. On the left, CYBER CITY DRIVER runs down the side in big outlined letters, CYBER CITY in white and DRIVER in pink below it, with three dark buttons underneath: RACING, FREE DRIVING and MORE STUNTS. On the right sits the car itself, a teal sports coupe with red trim and glowing red details, parked on a black floor marked with a pink grid, under a dark sky. A speaker icon sits at the top right, and a small credit at the bottom reads Made By RHM Interactive. The listing sets the game in a future city with a two-player mode, and the menu matches that promise exactly.

The driving is a two-player keyboard set. Player 1 drives with the arrow keys, N fires the nitro and B looks back, while player 2 uses WASD, T for nitro and C to look back, and R restarts the level. The two buttons on the menu set the shape of the session: RACING for a run against the clock and FREE DRIVING for a trip around the future city, with ramps in the listing that lead up to the roofs of buildings. Pick a mode, learn the nitro key, and the car does the rest.

The menu is the best part: the car looks fast standing still, the neon grid sells the future city, and the two modes are named on plain buttons. The trade-off is the keyboard, since a driving game with nitro and look-back keys has no touch scheme and belongs on a computer only, and the menu is the whole evidence seen, with no race on screen before a mode is picked. The MORE STUNTS button is a third option on the menu, but what it opens was not seen.

Driving fans who like a neon city and keyboard racers who want a two-player chase will enjoy this one. It is not built for a phone, and it will not suit players who want instant arcade racing with no keys to learn or the race itself on screen before picking a mode.
