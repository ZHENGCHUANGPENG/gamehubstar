Basketball AllStars Online

Basketball AllStars is built for two-player basketball with a silly edge, and the official description is upfront about it: choose a team, enter a tournament, dunk, shoot three-pointers, and block or slap your opponent to get the ball back. The supershot bar is the special ingredient, because a fully loaded bar gives you a special dunk that never fails, no matter where you stand. The captured screenshot shows the game loading screen with a big basketball theme and a 33 percent loading readout, which fits a game that wants to get you onto the court quickly. The controls are simple on paper, arrows for movement, X for steal and shoot, Z for the supershot, but the mix of blocking, pumping and stealing gives each match more than just shooting. It is a loud, competitive basketball game suited to those who enjoy head-to-head matches.

How to Play

Computer play: use the arrows to move and jump, arrow down to block and pump, X to steal and shoot, and Z for the supershot, per the instructions.

Tips

  • Watch the supershot bar at the top, and save the special dunk for a moment when it cannot miss.
  • Use block and pump to stop an opponent drive instead of chasing the ball every time.
  • Steal with X when the opponent is close, because a steal turns defence into a scoring chance.
