Tricky Puzzle - Brain Test Online

Tricky Puzzle - Brain Test is a brain-test puzzle game built around a level map. The top bar is pink, with a yellow menu button, five red hearts in the centre, and 500 gold coins on the right next to a gold coin icon. Below it, a Daily bonus popup covers the middle of the screen: a black crow holding a gold coin, the words Join us daily and earn free coins, and six day cards with rising coin rewards, plus a pink Earn button. Behind the popup sits the level map, a green hill with a yellow bus on a road and numbered nodes 1 and 2.

The bright side, and the dull side: the variety is the selling point, with the listing promising puzzles from saving aliens to counting bees and petting a dog, and the map, the bonus and the hearts give it a shape to return to. The daily bonus is generous. The snag is the popup covering the map on entry, so the first tap is a bonus claim, not a puzzle, and the trial never saw a puzzle being solved, so the out-of-the-box thinking is promised, not proven. Five hearts mean failed tries cost something.

A fit for puzzle fans who like a new odd little challenge every level, and for daily players who collect bonuses. Players who hate hearts and timers, or want one long puzzle instead of many short ones, should look elsewhere.

How to Play

Click a level node on the map with the mouse, then click or drag the objects in the puzzle. On mobile, tap the node and tap or drag with a finger, and some puzzles ask to shake or tilt the phone, per the posted instructions. Claim the daily bonus first, since the Earn button sits in the popup, then start at node 1 on the map and work forward. Read each puzzle before tapping anything, because the trick is usually in the wording, and shake or tilt the phone when a puzzle asks for it.

Tips

  • Start at node 1 on the map and work forward
