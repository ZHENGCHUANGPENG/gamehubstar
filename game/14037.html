DinoZ Online

The menu is a plain magenta screen with a single word in the middle: PLAY, written in big chunky letters in yellow, blue, green and orange. There are no mode buttons, no settings and no character choice on view, just the word and a skippable ad at the corner. The listing gives the premise behind that plain door: intelligent dinosaurs kept in a lab for research escape after an accident, take the scientists hostage, and head for the forest, setting traps along the way. The job is to save the hostages, alone or with a friend in two-player mode.

The controls are a full two-player keyboard set, listed in the instructions. Player 1 moves with WASD, hits with F, throws a bomb with H, jumps with W and switches weapon with Q, while player 2 uses the arrow keys, L to hit, J for a bomb, the up arrow to jump and U to switch weapon. Both players can wall jump with W or the up arrow, which matters in a game about climbing out of a lab and through a trapped forest. Learn the wall jump first, since it is the move the levels are built around.

The two-player mode is the best part of the setup, since a shared keyboard rescue with different keys per player is rare, and the premise is clear enough to start without a tutorial. The weakness is the menu, which is only the PLAY word on a flat colour, with no hint of the dinosaurs, the lab or the forest before the game starts, and the game is keyboard only, so it belongs on a computer and will not work on a phone. The ad sitting over the menu has to be skipped before PLAY can be pressed.

Co-op platformer fans and dinosaur game players should give it a try, especially with a friend sharing the keyboard. A phone will not run it, and solo players who want a guided first level or a detailed menu first should look elsewhere.
