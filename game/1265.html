Winter Clash 3D Online

Snow falls over a dark winter scene, a warm light glows at the top of the frame, a title banner crosses the middle, and a round play button waits below it. Blues and purples dominate, the snow giving the whole display a seasonal feel. The official note tells a Christmas story: evil elves plan to take over Santa's secret place and summon the evil Baba Yaga, also known as the Pagan Idol, into the world, and the player takes the role of a mighty Santa Claus who must capture the idol from a deserted lighthouse island and burn it in a Christmas fire. It is a team shooter, with multiplayer in the tags.

The premise is the hook: a holiday shooter that casts the player as Santa fighting off elves and a pagan idol is stranger and more memorable than another grey military map, and the full keyboard set, movement, jump, crouch, aim and a score table, shows a real FPS under the theme. The gap is between the story and what testing could see: only the snowy title screen showed, so the lighthouse island, the idol and the team fights are the developer's claims until a match loads, an ad bar runs along the bottom on phones, and the Christmas theme feels out of season most of the year.

Suited to desktop FPS players who like team shooters with a seasonal twist and are happy to learn a keyboard set, not a pick for phone players, since no touch play is listed, or for anyone wanting a match visible from the menu alone.

How to Play

Move with WASD, jump with Space, crouch with C, aim with the right mouse button, and check the score table with Tab, exactly as the posted control sheet lists. There is no touch scheme in the listing, and the phone pass caught only the title display behind an ad bar, so this one stays on a computer. Tap the round play button in the middle of the snowy display once it has loaded, learn the movement keys first, and keep Tab in mind, because the score table is the fastest way to check your team.

Tips

  • Learn the WASD movement set first, since the note lists it as the core control.
  • Crouch with C when you need a steadier shot instead of firing on the run.
  • Check the score table with Tab between fights.
