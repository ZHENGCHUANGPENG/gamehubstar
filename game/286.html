Penguin.io Online

A lone penguin stands on a jagged slab of pale ice, blue water lapping at every edge, under a frosty header that reads PENGUIN LEFT beside a round avatar slot. The desktop view is wide and airy, with the floe filling the lower half and the water stretching to the horizon, while the phone view zooms into the same world and finds the bird already paddling, yellow feet kicking, a faint dashed line trailing behind it. The official blurb calls this survival with a joke attached: penguins at the North Pole are always busy, and this game of staying alive on a floe is the deadliest of their pastimes. Nothing on screen explains a score or a timer, so the honest read is a clean, readable arena where position is everything and the ice itself is the opponent.

How to Play

Desktop players steer with the mouse, as the posted control notes say. On a phone, taps move the bird, and the touch pass caught it shifting from the floe into the water after taps, so finger steering works the same way.

Tips

  • Keep the bird near the middle of the floe at first, since the edge is where trouble starts.
  • Move as soon as the water closes in, rather than waiting for the floe to shrink under you.
  • Watch the top bar, because it tracks how the contest is going while you steer.
