Plants vs Zombies Online

The tower-defense genre was arguably built on this game, and the browser version here is faithful: the screenshot shows a real lawn, a real wave in progress, and the plant selection bar that is the whole strategic interface. The description's promise of hundreds of plants and zombies is the genre's depth; the fun is in the combinatorial puzzle of which defenders counter which horde. Sun economy is the hidden game, and players who plant producers early will outlast players who rush damage. The presentation is colorful and readable, which matters when the lawn gets crowded. For anyone who has never played a tower defense, this is the ideal starting point; for veterans, it is a comfortable, competent version of a classic.

How to Play

Drag plants from the bar onto the lawn grid; sun resources fund each placement. Zombies advance in waves, and the wave tracker shows what is coming. There is no direct combat control; your strategy is the placement. The lawn is the battlefield.

Tips

  • Plant sun producers early; economy wins the mid-game.
  • Match plant types to zombie types as waves escalate.
  • Keep a cheap blocker in reserve for surprise rushes.
