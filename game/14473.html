How to Play

Drag plants from the bar onto the lawn grid; sun resources fund each placement. Zombies advance in waves, and the wave tracker shows what is coming. There is no direct combat control; your strategy is the placement. The lawn is the battlefield. Plant sun producers early, since economy wins the mid-game, match plant types to zombie types as waves escalate, and keep a cheap blocker in reserve for surprise rushes.
