MiniBattles - 2 3 4 5 6 Player Games Online

MiniBattles is a party minigame collection for two to six players where friends battle in quick rounds, and the trial caught a plane dogfight over a farm field. Two planes cross over the field, one red and one blue, with bombs bursting between them and a health bar sitting in the top corner. That dogfight is one round of MiniBattles, built for two to six players on one screen; the listing names Soccer, Cars, War Tanks, Sumo, Archers, Vikings and Spaceships inside. Each round is an all-against-all battle with a single winner, and a counter keeps track of who has won the most battles, so a session becomes a small tournament.

The good, and the bad: the one-button control keeps every round readable even when six people are playing, the farm below is full of cows, chickens and power lines, and the win counter turns a few rounds into a tournament. The art is bright and simple, and a round ends before anyone gets bored. The downsides are the shared keyboard, six players crowding one set of keys, and the fact that only the dogfight was seen, so the other minigames are named but unproven. A phone tap is a poor substitute for six-player keyboard play.

Who should play: friends sharing one keyboard, party hosts and groups after a quick tournament with no setup. Skip it for solo play, phone-only play or any deep single-player modes.

How to Play

On a desktop, each player gets one button: C for player 1, N for player 2, P for player 3, Q for player 4, and the arrow keys for players 5 and 6, per the posted keys. On a phone the same tap starts the round. Pick a minigame everyone knows first, since the dogfight is a good opener, and remember that one button is the whole control, so timing beats mashing. Watch the health bar at the top left, because it decides the round.
