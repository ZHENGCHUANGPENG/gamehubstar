MiniBattles - 2 3 4 5 6 Player Games Online

Two planes cross over a farm field, one red and one blue, with bombs bursting between them and a health bar sitting in the top corner. That dogfight is one round of MiniBattles, a collection built for two to six players on one screen, and the listing names Soccer, Cars, War Tanks, Sumo, Archers, Vikings and Spaceships among the games inside. Each round is an all-against-all battle with a single winner, and a counter keeps track of who has won the most battles, so a session turns into a small tournament without any setup. The farm below the planes is full of cows, chickens and power lines, and the clouds drift past while the planes trade shots. The control is one button per player, which keeps every round readable even when six people are playing. The art is bright and simple, and the dogfight moves fast enough that a round ends before anyone gets bored. A party round ends fast, and the counter decides who hosts the next one.

How to Play

One button per player on a desktop: C for player 1, N for player 2, P for player 3, Q for player 4, and the arrow keys for players 5 and 6, per the posted keys. On a phone the same tap starts the round. The screen shows two planes dogfighting over a farm field with cows and power lines below, a health bar at the very top and bombs bursting between them.

Tips

  • Pick a minigame everyone knows first, the dogfight is a good opener
  • One button is the whole control, so timing beats mashing
  • Watch the health bar at the top left, it decides the round
  • The win counter tracks who has the most battles won
