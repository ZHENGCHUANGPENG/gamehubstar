Bubble Shooter Fruits Wheel Online

The wheel is what makes this one different from a plain bubble shooter. Instead of a static board, your target keeps rotating, so a shot that looks perfect one second can be a miss the next. That movement turns aiming into a small timing puzzle: you are not just picking a colour, you are picking a moment. The fruit theme keeps the board readable at a glance, with each fruit colour standing out against the wheel, and the match-three rule means the goal never needs explaining. The start screen is the same friendly landscape panel shown by the test capture, with a dog mascot and a START button, so the game gets out of your way quickly. Playing on touch feels natural, because the whole game is one aimed shot after another. The rotating wheel adds just enough pressure to keep a simple formula interesting, without adding buttons or systems to learn.

How to Play

Point with the mouse on desktop and shoot fruit at the turning wheel; on a phone, tap to aim and fire, waiting for the colour you need to rotate into line.

Tips

  • Watch the wheel turn for a moment before shooting, so your fruit lands on the colour you want.
  • Aim for the largest matching group on the wheel rather than the closest single fruit.
  • When no good match is showing, use a shot to set up the next turn instead of forcing a weak match.
