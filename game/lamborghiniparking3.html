How to Play

Player one steers with W, A, S, D and brakes with the spacebar, per the developer. A second control scheme uses the arrow keys with keypad braking. The title screen presents a simple START entry, and the game notes phone support for touch play. Precise, small inputs matter more than speed here.

Tips

  • - Approach the bay slowly and straighten up early — correcting at the last second causes scrapes.
  • - Use the brake constantly; coasting into the spot is safer than powering in.
  • - Line up from a distance rather than turning sharply next to the bay.
  • - Watch all four corners of the car, not just the front — rear clips fail runs silently.
