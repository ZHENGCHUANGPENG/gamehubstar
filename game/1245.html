How to Play

【电脑专属，需键盘鼠标，手机无法操作】Shoot by jumping with the ball and aiming at the hoop with desktop mouse control, before the timer runs down; phones showed only a blank screen for this one in testing.

Tips

  • Watch the green timer bar as you shoot, since the clock is the real opponent here.
  • Aim at the hoop first and let the jump follow the aim, not the other way round.
  • Take the clean shot in front of you instead of waiting for a perfect one that never comes.
