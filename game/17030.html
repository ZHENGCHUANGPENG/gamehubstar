Stickman Parkours Online

Stickman Parkours is a running and jumping game, and its title screen says so in one look: a big blue STICKMAN PARKOUR logo with a star in the middle, a blue START button below it, grey pentagons scattered around and a HYHYGAMES mark at the top. The description promises a brave stickman, challenging levels filled with devious traps, acrobatic moves and gems to earn. Tapping START opens a level select with ten tiles in two rows — level 1 is blue and open, the rest carry padlocks — and a HOME button waits below the grid.

The controls are two schemes for the same moves. On a computer, the arrow keys move and jump, or A and D move while S jumps; pressing D several times climbs. On a phone, the path in is a tap on START and a tap on level 1, and the level tiles answer taps the same way a mouse click does. The setup is quick: title, START, level select, and then the run. Nothing else sits on the title screen, so there is no menu to get lost in.

The clearest strength is the honest level select. Ten tiles, one open and nine locked, tell the player exactly how much road lies ahead before the first jump. The title art is also direct: the logo, the star and the START button leave no doubt about what the game is. The limit is the evidence: the run itself was not observed, so the traps, the achievements and the gems are the developer's description rather than something testing saw. The game is also keyboard-first on a computer, with the phone path limited to taps.

Good for parkour and runner fans who like short attempts at one level at a time, and for anyone after a simple arcade game on a phone or a computer. It suits quick sessions where the goal is to open the next tile.

How to Play

On a computer, use the arrow keys to move and jump, or use A and D to move and S to jump; press D several times to climb. On a phone, tap the blue START button on the title screen, then tap level 1 on the level select — the level tiles answer taps the same way a mouse click does. The title screen carries no other buttons, so START is the only way in.
