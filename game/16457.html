Mini Shooters Online

A wooden sign reading MINI SHOOTERS stands in a desert camp, with blue tents and sand behind it. That is Mini Shooters, a 2D shooting game where the listing frames the game as equipping multiple weapons and fighting in deathmatch play. One tap on the play triangle opens a level grid with 30 levels, the first one open and the rest locked behind padlocks, plus a MULTIPLAYER button and a back button below. The art is flat and warm, the desert and the tents fill the frame, and the grid is laid out in three neat rows. WASD or the arrow keys move the character, the mouse aims, a left click shoots and Space picks up a weapon, which gives the shooting a clear setup on a desktop. The menus respond to taps on a phone. A session runs like this: open the level, pick a weapon, aim, shoot.

How to Play

WASD or the arrow keys to move, the mouse to aim, left mouse click to shoot and Space to pick up a weapon, per the posted controls. On a phone the same menus respond to taps. The captured screens show the desert title art with the wooden sign and blue tents, then a level grid with 30 levels, level 1 open, the rest locked and a MULTIPLAYER button.

Tips

  • Level 1 is open first, the rest unlock as you finish
  • The MULTIPLAYER button sits below the level grid
  • Aim with the mouse on a desktop, the shots follow the cursor
  • Pick up weapons with Space when one appears
