Tiny Archer Online

Tiny Archer keeps everything small except the title. The menu shows a dark green range with the name in big white and red letters, a target with a red centre at the top right, a small archer holding a bow to one side, and a round target standing on a post to the right. In the middle sits a large blue play button, with small buttons below and a SKINS button in the lower corner. The write-up sets a simple challenge: targets keep coming, your arrows are limited, and every miss costs you one, so the question is how far you can get before the quiver runs out. That is a game about patience dressed as a game about archery, and the menu shows why: one archer, one target, and a single button between you and the next shot.

How to Play

【电脑专属】On a computer, play with the mouse, releasing the arrow at the right moment to hit the target directly in the middle, per the control notes. This listing is computer-only.

Tips

  • Wait for the right moment instead of loosing the arrow early, since timing decides whether you hit the middle.
  • Aim for the centre of the target every time, because the middle is what moves you forward.
  • Guard your arrows: the notes warn that misses cost arrows, so a careless shot costs more than a point.
