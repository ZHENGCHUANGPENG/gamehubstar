Monsters Attack Impostor Squad Online

A black-and-white checkered floor runs to a wall of grey lockers, and across the middle sits a gold PLAY button under MONSTERS ATTACK in red and AMONG US SQUAD in white, with a star badge on the header. On the left stand three small soldier figures in helmets, one holding a rifle, while on the right loom three monster figures, a tall blue one with a wide grin, an orange robot-like one, and a pale slender one in a suit. A taptapking.com credit sits in the bottom corner with a v1.3 mark. The developer note tells a bigger story about monsters taking over soldiers, but the screen itself sells a simpler promise: two teams, one button, and a floor built for a run-and-gun round.

How to Play

Desktop players move with the arrow keys, change hero with C, shoot with Z, and use a special power with X, per the posted control sheet. On a phone, the note says to tap the on-screen game buttons instead, and the landscape pass caught the title screen with its PLAY button ready to tap.

Tips

  • Tap the gold PLAY button once the checkered title screen has loaded, since the header is the whole menu.
  • Study the two sides of the screen first, soldiers on the left and monsters on the right, before the action starts.
  • Keep the movement keys in mind on desktop, because the note lists arrows, C, Z, and X as the full set.
