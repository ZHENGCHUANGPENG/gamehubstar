Car Chase Online

Car Chase is a top-down 2D driving game where a small red car weaves across dirt and grass, collecting gold coins while the police close in. The view is brown dirt and green grass from above, with the red car in the middle of the field and gold coins with dollar signs scattered across the dirt. A police icon and a zero sit at the top right, and a big 2 sits at the upper left as the score. A large black tire rests at the bottom of the field, with a Unity WebGL bar below. The official description calls it a simple 2D game of steering, collecting and dodging police cars.

What is good, and what is weak: the whole game reads in one glance, with the car easy to follow, the coins standing out against the dirt, It plays as a quick survival drive: keep collecting while the police close in, with open dirt to turn wide and stay ahead. The gap is that no police car ever appeared on the screen, only the police icon, so the chase itself is unproven. The grass patches slow the turns, which punishes sloppy steering, and one field means every run looks the same.

Best for driving fans and quick-session players chasing a simple collect-and-dodge run on the desktop. Not one for players who want visible police chases, varied tracks, or any phone play.

How to Play

Keyboard only: steer the car with the keyboard, as the instructions describe a joystick game, collecting the gold coins and keeping away from the police cars. Steer around the green grass patches, since they slow the turns, and grab the coins in a line instead of chasing single ones. Keep away from the police cars at the top of the screen, and use the open dirt to turn wide and stay ahead of them.

Tips

  • Steer around the green grass patches, they slow the turns
  • Grab the coins in a line instead of chasing single ones
  • Keep away from the police cars at the top of the screen
  • Use the open dirt to turn wide and stay ahead
