Making Beverages Online

Making Beverages puts the player behind the counter of a milk tea shop. The idea is the one the description states: run the shop, make a variety of milk teas, add more ingredients to a cup, and let customers enjoy the unique taste. Level 1 opens on a BUBBLE TEA ORDER, with a red bubble-tea cup shown as the target, an empty cup waiting in the centre, and a hand hint resting over a red dispenser. Ingredient buttons line the left side, a green check button sits on the right, and the coin counter at the top reads zero.

The controls are taps. On a computer, click the ingredient buttons on the left and the red dispenser in the centre; a phone tap works the same way, and the centre tap answers with a response. The hand hint shows the first move by pointing at the red dispenser, and the green check button confirms the cup when it is ready. Three keys sit at the top right of the screen, and the coin counter tracks earnings from the start of the level. The target cup stays visible throughout, so the order is always in view.

The clearest strength is how readable level 1 is. The red target cup, the empty cup and the hand hint explain the task without words, and the green check button gives the level an obvious finish. The shop theme is cheerful, and the coin counter gives every finished cup a visible reward. The weak point is legibility: the names on the ingredient buttons were not readable on screen, so what each button adds has to be learned by tapping rather than read. Only level 1 was seen, so later orders and any customers beyond the first cup are not part of this review.

Good for simulation fans who like shop games and for casual players who enjoy matching a target one cup at a time. It fits relaxed sessions on a phone or a computer, especially for anyone who likes a calm making game without a timer in view.
