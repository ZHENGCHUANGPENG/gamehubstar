Tangle Master 3D Online

Tangle Master 3D is a wire-untangling puzzle, and the listing frames it as moving plugs to untie tangled wires, with simple early levels and harder ones later. What opens is a plain mauve panel with the words TANGLE Master 3D in big yellow capitals edged in white. Below the title sit two level thumbnails: one shows three girls with colourful braids on a green field, and the other shows a bride in white with a roll of toilet paper flying toward her. A large blue PLAY button with white letters sits under them, bestgames.com sits at the bottom left, with a More Games button at the bottom right.

The good side, and the bad side: the premise is easy to grasp, one tangled mess, one plug at a time, and the tall screen fits a phone held upright, with thumbnails large enough to read and a PLAY button that is impossible to miss. The downside is the title screen itself: no actual tangle ever appeared in the trial, and the two thumbnails promise wildly different themes, which says little about how the puzzles play. The braid picture and the bride picture look like different games entirely, so the variety may be skin-deep.

Who it suits: casual puzzle players who like untangling things at their own pace, and phone players after a quick brain teaser. Proven puzzle systems and consistent themes are not what this one offers.

How to Play

Desktop players click a plug and drag it with the mouse until its wire runs free. Touch players drag the plug with a finger, per the posted instructions: drag to play. Tap PLAY to open the first tangle, and look at both ends of each wire before moving a plug, since a wire that looks stuck may only need its far end freed. Free the easiest plug first and the rest loosens up, and work one tangle at a time instead of pulling everything at once.
