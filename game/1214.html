Stickman Gun Shooter 3D Online

Stickman Gun Shooter 3D is a third-person survival shooter set in a grey 3D city, and the goal is the one the description states plainly: shoot the enemy stickmen before they shoot you, and survive as long as you can. The level screen shows a black stickman holding a rifle in front of tall grey buildings, with Level 1 in green, Level 2 and Level 3 greyed out, a Survival label with five stars, and a blue notice that mobile controls are off. Level 1 opens on a wide tiled plaza between the buildings, a red health bar sitting at the top left, and a red arrow marker floating ahead to point the way. The footer names the game Stickman Gun Shooter 3D and the Unity WebGL badge sits below the scene.

The controls are keyboard and mouse only. WASD or the arrow keys move the stickman through the city, CTRL fires the weapon, Space jumps, and G throws a grenade. The description warns that the game contains blood and shooting, lists weapons from shotguns and machine guns to an MP5, M16, AK-47 and Uzi, and promises ragdoll deaths with physics-based animations. The start screen is clear that mobile controls are off, so the keyboard is the only way in. The red health bar stays in view at the top, so damage is always visible.

The city itself reads well. The grey buildings and pale tiles make the black stickman easy to follow, the red arrow gives a direction when the plaza feels empty, and the health bar keeps the stakes in sight. The drawback is the opening: Level 1 starts in a quiet plaza with no enemy in view, so the first seconds are a walk toward the arrow before any shooting starts. The level screen also shows only the starting rifle, so the longer weapon list from the description does not appear until later levels.

Good for players who like 3D stickman shooters and survival rounds on a computer. It works for short arena sessions, for anyone comfortable with WASD movement and mouse aiming, and for players who enjoy a clear survive-as-long-as-you-can goal.
