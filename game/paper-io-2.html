Paper.IO 2 Online

Paper.IO 2 is a competitive multiplayer arena game where you claim territory and try to outlast rival players. Everyone starts with a small colored zone and expands it by venturing out and looping back safely — any ground you circle becomes yours. The catch is that you're only safe inside your own turf. The moment you step outside, your exposed trail becomes a target, and a single touch from an enemy ends your run. The strategy is all about risk management. Small, careful loops near your border grow your area steadily with minimal danger, while long expeditions into open space can pay off big if nobody intercepts your tail. Watching other players' trails and cutting them off at the right moment is just as important as expanding your own zone. Matches are quick and the leaderboard-style scoring (territory percentage, kills, survival time) keeps every round tense. Controls are simple — mouse or WASD/arrow keys to steer — so the skill ceiling comes from positioning and timing rather than complex inputs. If you enjoy .io games built around outsmarting real opponents rather than grinding upgrades, this one delivers exactly that loop: expand, defend your tail, and punish anyone careless enough to leave theirs exposed.

How to Play

Steer with the mouse, WASD, or arrow keys — your block moves continuously in the direction you choose. On touch screens, drag to change direction. Your trail becomes permanent territory only when you loop back to your own land. There is no attack button; killing is done by crossing an enemy's exposed tail.

Tips

  • Make small loops near your border early — big ambitious loops are how most runs end in the first minute.
  • Watch for players returning to their turf and cut across their tail at the last second for easy kills.
  • When a bigger player targets you, dive back into your own territory and wait — they can't touch you there.
  • Team up with friends in the same server and corner rivals together; coordinated trapping is brutally effective.
