Super Mario Bros Movie Online

A red-capped runner in blue overalls takes centre stage on a bright hillside, with a green pipe, a brown mushroom-like foe, grey spikes and a trail of gold coins filling the run ahead. Above him, a how-to-play panel lays out the whole deal: jump to survive, steer clear of enemies and spikes, and pick up coins and stars to unlock fresh characters. A coin counter reads 120 and three lives sit in the corner, while a big blue play button waits on the right. The developer blurb calls it a side-scrolling 2D platform adventure, and the screen backs the platform half completely: one runner, one jump, and a string of hazards that punish a late hop. The hillside backdrop, with soft clouds and distant peaks, keeps the eye on the runner rather than the scenery.

How to Play

Desktop players click to make the runner jump, exactly as the how-to-play panel describes. Phone players tap the screen for the same jump, and the opening prompt even shows a handset turning sideways, so this one wants a wide view.

Tips

  • Jump a beat before a spike row so the landing clears the whole hazard, not just its first edge.
  • Watch the blocky enemy ahead of the runner and time the jump from its position, not from your own feet.
  • Chase the coin and star lines when they sit on a safe arc, since the how-to panel ties them to new characters.
