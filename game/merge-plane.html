Merge Plane Online

Idleness is the whole point here, and Merge Plane wears it proudly. There is no pilot to steer and no runway to manage minute by minute — just a green airfield, an oval track, and four dark hangar slots waiting to be filled. The buy button prices your first plane at 50 coins, the earnings readout sits at 0/second, and the entire opening act is one long stare at an empty sky. That honesty is the game's double edge. Fans of idle tower defense games online will recognize the ritual instantly: hoard the first coins, fill the grid, then let compound growth do the heavy lifting while the Exchange panel tempts you to convert coins into propeller tokens at 10,000 to 1. During testing the balance never left zero — the buy button stayed a tease and the slots stayed empty — which says less about the game than about a playtester who never got the economy rolling. The interface itself is crisp and responsive: Speed x2, SHOP, and the little red trash bin all answer taps, and the track graphic gives the field more character than the genre usually bothers with. It will not thrill anyone who needs a joystick. But if idle tower defense games online are your comfort food, this airfield offers the same merge-and-watch dopamine with planes instead of turrets.

How to Play

Everything is left-click or tap — no keys involved. Tap the blue buy button to purchase a plane for 50 coins once you can afford it, and use the four dark slots as your hangar grid. The Speed x2 button accelerates earnings, the SHOP button opens upgrades, and the red trash bin at the bottom clears out planes you no longer want. The Exchange panel swaps coins for propeller tokens at a posted rate of 10,000 to 1.

Tips

  • Your coins start at zero and the buy button wants 50 — do not waste early taps on the Exchange panel, which does nothing without a balance.
  • Fill the four hangar slots before touching anything else; an empty airfield earns 0/second, and the counter in testing never moved from there.
  • The SHOP button promises upgrades, but spend there only after your fleet is actually earning.
  • Speed x2 multiplies whatever your fleet makes, so trigger it once planes are in the airfield, not on an empty field.
