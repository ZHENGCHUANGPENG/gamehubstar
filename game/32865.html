Sprunki baby PHASE 3 Online

The screen opens on a soft green hill under a pale blue sky with white clouds, and three grey baby Sprunki characters stand in a row on the grass. Each one has big round eyes, a small sad mouth, and a white diaper with yellow trim, and they look like a younger, gentler version of the usual Sprunki cast. Below them sit two rows of small accessory icons, a pink flower, a brown hat, a pink bow, and more, waiting to be dragged onto the babies. A gear icon sits in the top left and a faint circular arrow at the upper right. The listing describes it as a baby version of the Sprunki Retake mod with a softer, family-friendly feel, and what showed agrees with that: no stage, no notes, just the babies and the dress-up tray. The portrait layout suits a phone held upright, the babies are big enough to tap easily, and the whole scene reads as a calm dress-up toy rather than a rhythm game.

How to Play

With a desktop, click a baby and drag an accessory onto it with the mouse. With a phone, tap the baby and drag with a finger, as the instructions put it: click and drag.
