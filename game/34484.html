How to Play

Tap the screen or press the spacebar to flap and stay in the air, as the posted controls list them. The menu shows the pink bird, red pipes, a snow mountain backdrop and a HIGH SCORE readout. On a phone a fingertip tap does the same job, and the score screen shows the run total with a RESTART button.

Tips

  • Tap in short steady beats instead of long presses
  • Keep the bird near the middle of the gap
  • Watch the next pipe, not the bird
  • One point is a start, the high score tracker remembers every run
