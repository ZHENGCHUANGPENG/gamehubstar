Bunny Punch Online

A worried bunny and a tall stack of crates set the scene at once. The title screen shows the grey bunny standing on a wooden platform in a forest clearing, a PLAY button beside it and a BEST score of zero. One tap opens the game, and the screen turns into a single column of crates, wooden ones and riveted metal ones, with the bunny waiting at the bottom right and a green timer bar draining at the top. Each tap makes the bunny punch to that side, and the crate on top of the stack breaks away. Per the posted description, the boxes must be cleared without touching the spikes before the time runs out, and the timer bar on screen makes that deadline visible at all times. There is no clutter and no second mechanic to learn, just the column, the bar and the bunny. The forest backdrop and the chunky crate art give the scene a friendly look, and the up-arrow button in the corner hints at a way back up the stack. A quick session is just this: punch, clear, race the bar, try again.

How to Play

Tap the left or right side of the screen to punch that way, following the posted control to click the side you want the bunny to punch. The game screen shows the bunny at the bottom right, a stack of wooden and metal crates in the middle, a green timer bar at the top and a score readout of zero. The same tap is a mouse click on a desktop, and an up-arrow button sits in the top right corner.

Tips

  • Tap the side the next crate sits on, not the bunny
  • Keep a steady rhythm, the timer bar drains while you think
  • Watch the crate stack, it is the only thing that matters
  • The score starts at zero, every cleared crate is a point
