Princess Elsa Hidden Hearts Online

Start with the obvious charm: this is a genuinely pretty little hidden-object game. The Level 1 scene is a hand-drawn winter castle with a smiling ice princess, a goofy snowman, and sparkles drifting through the frame — soft purples and blues instead of the flat clipart look a lot of these games settle for. The structure is dead simple: ten hearts hidden in the illustration, a countdown under the minute, and a counter tracking how many you have found. That simplicity is both the selling point and the limit. There is no hint system visible, no zoom, no penalty readout for wrong taps in the captured session, so the whole game is your eyes versus the artwork on a clock. For kids or anyone who wants a calm, five-minute search game, that is probably exactly right. For puzzle veterans, Level 1 will feel easy. The one real annoyance in the trial was an interstitial ad cutting in over the level, which is worth knowing if you hand the phone to a child. Still, the presentation carries it: bright, cheerful, and immediately understandable. Find the hearts, beat the clock, move on — nothing more, nothing less.

How to Play

No control tutorial appears in the captured session, and the input is entirely taps — touch a heart where you spot it in the illustration. The top bar shows your found count and the current level, while a timer in the corner counts down the search. A cross button exits the level and a 'more games' button sits at the bottom. Nothing else in the screenshots implies any other input.

Tips

  • - The counter shows '0 of 10 found', so you are looking for exactly ten hearts — check edges and corners of the artwork first, where small items are usually tucked.
  • - The timer runs under a minute in Level 1, so scan systematically (for example, left to right) instead of tapping randomly.
  • - Hearts blend into the purple winter palette in the screenshot — look for the small heart silhouette against both light snow and dark shadows.
  • - An ad interrupted the trial session; if one pops up mid-search, wait it out rather than closing the game, since the level was still running.
