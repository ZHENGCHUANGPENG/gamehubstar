Sugar Cookie Battle Online

The screen opens on a round honeycomb cookie in a metal tin, a dashed triangle drawn across its golden surface, with a needle tool leaning against the shape, its red ball handle at the lower right. A Start button sits below the tin on a blue background scattered with faint triangle, circle and square outlines. Tapping Start opened a shape select row: five round icons, the triangle open and the rest locked behind small padlocks. The developer write-up presents a dalgona candy challenge where the shape must be carved out without breaking the cookie, and the cookie, the needle and the Start button all match that idea. The upright layout keeps the cookie large and the needle easy to follow, and the shape select makes the next step obvious. What showed was the cookie, the tool and the menu, and the tracing itself is the game that follows.

How to Play

With a desktop, click Start, then trace the dashed shape with the mouse. With a phone, tap Start and trace the shape by fingertip. Its instructions call for the mouse.

Tips

  • ['The triangle is the open shape, start there', 'Trace slowly along the dashed line instead of rushing', 'Locked shapes open as earlier ones are finished', 'The needle tool is what does the carving']
