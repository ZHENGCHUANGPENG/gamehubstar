The zombie himself is enormous, a green-skinned figure in a torn green shirt and grey trousers, barefoot, with red wounds and a wide open mouth, filling the centre of the frame under CRUSH THE ZOMBIES in yellow letters. The posted description sets up a zombie uprising to destroy at all costs, with different types of zombies and difficulty levels, nice graphics and sounds, bombs and extra lives for the player, and a stored top score. No zombie is crushed in the opening frame, so the action is told only by the posted description. The road, the cars and the giant zombie are the visible facts.

How to Play

Use the mouse or tap the screen, as the posted note puts it, pressing the big blue PLAY button to start. On a computer, click with the mouse; on a phone, press with a finger, keeping the handset upright since the road fills a tall frame. The gamepad icon at the lower left opens the control choice, and the speaker icon at the lower right mutes the sound.

Tips

  • Press PLAY only when ready, since the road opens the moment the button is tapped.
  • Watch the cars as well as the zombie, because the road carries traffic in both directions.
  • Save the bombs for a crowded stretch, since the listing lists them as the scarce help.
  • Check the score lines at the top after each run, because the game stores the best total.
