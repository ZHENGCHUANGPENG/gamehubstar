Brain Test: IQ Challenge Online

Level 1 asks you to find the longest one, and the screen offers four candidates that all look equally long: an orange coiled belt, a green snake, a little truck, and a suspension bridge. That is Brain Test: IQ Challenge in a single frame — a cartoon picture, a deceptively plain question, and an answer hiding in how the drawing cheats your eye. Level 3 follows the same playbook with a different disguise: five near-identical orange cars and the prompt 'find the slowest car,' with a white glove cursor floating over the scene like a mischievous hint. The presentation is bright and hand-drawn, the level text set in chunky black type against a textured paper background. A settings gear, a restart arrow, and a hint bank showing 35 keys frame every puzzle, so the scaffolding is honest about what the game wants: tap, drag, experiment, and don't trust the literal reading. Solving a level triggers a confetti burst and a 'Congratulation — Good Works!' card with a Next Level button, which keeps the loop breezy rather than punishing. No timers pressured the observed levels, and nothing in the screenshots suggests a fail state harsher than a retry. It is the rare puzzle game where getting the answer wrong is the actual entertainment, because the trick — once seen — is impossible to unsee.

How to Play

Tap the objects on each level directly, or click and drag with a mouse on desktop — every level is touch-driven and asks you to poke at the artwork rather than press buttons. A gear icon in the top-left opens settings, a curved arrow restarts the level, and the key icon with 35 in the top-right holds the hint bank. After a solved level, the blue Next Level button moves the run forward.

Tips

  • - Read the level text literally but think sideways — 'find the longest' is a perception trap, not a measuring task.
  • - Tap, drag, and pull objects around; many answers hide inside the artwork itself.
  • - The key icon shows 35 hints banked — spending one is cheaper than a long stuck session.
  • - The restart arrow resets the level instantly, so experiment freely before asking for help.
