No predator or seedling appears on the opening screen. The adventure is told only in the posted description, while the fish, the arrows and the level line are plainly visible.

How to Play

On a computer, use the mouse to move the character, as the posted instructions put it. On a phone or tablet, touch the screen and slide to move the character, in the same note. Tap the red play button to enter, and use the red arrows to switch the fish before starting. Keep the phone upright, since the blue screen fills a tall frame.

Tips

  • Tap the red play button only when ready, since the level opens the moment it is pressed.
  • Use the arrows to look at the fish choices first, because the centre fish is only the starting one.
  • Collect the seedlings the listing mentions, since they sit along the safe path.
  • Watch for predators while swimming, because the listing warns they share the sea.
