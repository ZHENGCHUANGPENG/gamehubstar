KillMaster Secret Agent Online

The title screen is a snowy street between tall buildings, with a secret agent in a suit and sunglasses holding a rifle, a crossed-rifles emblem above him, and the words KILLMASTER SECRET AGENT on a banner. A big round play button sits below, with a small character icon beside it, and the KUKOGAMES logo at the bottom. That covers the game: a secret agent runs through snowy streets to take out masked men, and the listing adds that barrels can explode and take nearby enemies with them. The upright layout suits the street, the agent is drawn large in the center, and the play button is impossible to miss. The result is a straightforward shooter where the screen shows the target and the finger does the shooting, with no complicated setup in the way. The music and sound icons sit at the top right, and the small character icon beside the play button hints at other agents to try.

How to Play

On a computer, click to shoot. With a phone, tap the screen to shoot, as the instructions put it: use the mouse right click to shoot, and touch the screen on mobile devices to shoot.
