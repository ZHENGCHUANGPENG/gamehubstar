Emerland Solitaire Online

Emerland Solitaire is a solitaire game wrapped in a fantasy kingdom. The kingdom map shows a wizard, a path of numbered levels, a Star Chest, a coin counter and a hearts counter, and tapping level one opens a tutorial board where the wizard speaks first: he has heard the player wants to learn the magic of Solitaire, and before he starts teaching, the basics come first. The board below him shows two rows of cards in runs, three through six and eight through jack, with a deck and a seven of spades waiting at the bottom. The listing calls the game easy to learn, and the wizard's lesson backs that up.

The controls are clicks or taps. Computer players click a numbered level on the kingdom map, then click the cards on the board to play them. On a phone, tap the same spots; the board fits an upright screen as well as a wide one, and the wizard's speech sits at the bottom of the board while the cards stay visible above. A short ad grid can appear between the map and the board, so wait for its countdown instead of tapping the ad tiles.

The strongest part is the presentation: the wizard, the kingdom map and the card runs make a plain solitaire game feel like a small adventure, and the tutorial means a new player is never lost. The downside is the pace and the interruptions. The first board is a teaching level, so an experienced solitaire player will find the start slow, the board dims while the wizard talks, and an ad sits between the map and the game. The map also shows a timer and a Star Chest whose purpose is not explained on the screens I saw.

Solitaire players who like a fantasy setting and a gentle start will enjoy it. Players who want to jump straight into hard boards should look elsewhere.
