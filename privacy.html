PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

This page explains what data GameHubStar collects, why we collect it, and what rights you have.

Last updated: November 15, 2026.
01

Overview

GameHubStar collects as little data as possible. The site has no accounts, no sign-ups, and no logins, so we never ask for your name, email address, or password to play a game. The data described on this page comes from standard web analytics, cookies, and the third-party services that deliver the games and the advertising that keeps the site free.

02

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how the site is used — for example, which pages are visited, which games are popular, and what kinds of devices and browsers our visitors use. This information is aggregated and anonymous: it does not identify you personally, and we do not combine it with any other data to identify individual visitors. We use these statistics only to improve the site, for instance by featuring games people actually play and fixing pages that are never visited. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing Google's opt-out browser add-on or by using your browser's tracking protection settings.

03

Cookies

GameHubStar and its service providers use cookies — small files stored in your browser — for a few limited purposes: remembering your site preferences such as the day or night theme, and measuring anonymous usage through analytics. We do not use cookies to build advertising profiles of our own. You can control cookies through your browser settings, including blocking them entirely; the games themselves will still work, although your theme preference will not be remembered between visits.

04

Third-party content and advertising

The games on GameHubStar are delivered through GameMonetize, and advertising on the site is served by third-party advertising providers, including GameMonetize. When you play a game or view an advertisement, those third parties may set their own cookies and collect information such as your device type, browser, approximate location, and how you interact with their content, in accordance with their own privacy policies. GameHubStar does not control those practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of GameMonetize on their website if you want the details. We never sell your personal information to anyone, and we do not share analytics data with advertisers in a form that identifies you.

05

Children's privacy

GameHubStar is a general-audience website and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email address on our Contact page and we will remove it promptly.

06

Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you, and to object to or restrict its processing. If you are in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) gives you the right to know what personal information is collected and to request its deletion, and to opt out of any sale of personal information — GameHubStar does not sell personal information. To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address on our Contact page and tell us what you would like us to do; we will respond within a reasonable time. If we change this policy in a material way, we will post the updated version on this page and revise the date above.
