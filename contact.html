GET IN TOUCH

Contact GameHubStar

Questions, broken games, business inquiries, or copyright complaints — email us and we will get back to you.

01

Email us

The fastest way to reach GameHubStar is email: zhenglinyong123@gmail.com. We read every message. Please write in English or Chinese, and include enough detail for us to act on your message — a game title, a link, or a screenshot helps far more than a one-line note.

02

Reporting a broken game

If a game does not load, freezes, or plays incorrectly, please tell us the game title and its page link, the device and browser you are using, and what exactly happened. That information lets us reproduce the problem quickly. Some issues come from the game itself rather than from our site; in those cases we will either fix the listing, mark the game for desktop only, or remove it from the catalog. Reports from players are one of the main ways we keep the catalog healthy, so please do not hesitate to write in.

03

Business inquiries

For partnerships, advertising, or game submissions, email us with a short description of what you have in mind. Game developers are welcome to suggest their own HTML5 games for the catalog — please include a playable link and a brief description of the game, its controls, and the devices it supports. We only list games we have actually played and verified, so every submission goes through the same playtesting as the rest of the catalog. We do not accept paid placements or sponsored rankings.

04

Copyright complaints

If you believe content on GameHubStar infringes your copyright, please email us with a description of the copyrighted work, the exact URL of the page you are complaining about, and proof that you own the rights or are authorized to act for the owner. We review every complaint promptly and remove content that is found to infringe. You can also read our Terms of Service for the full policy on intellectual property.

05

Response time

GameHubStar is a one-person project, so replies can take a few business days, especially on weekends. We answer messages in the order they arrive and prioritize broken-game reports and copyright complaints. Thank you for your patience — and for playing.
