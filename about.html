About GameHubStar

GameHubStar is a free browser game site. We collect hundreds of HTML5 games you can play instantly — no download, no signup, no install.

01

What is GameHubStar

GameHubStar is a curated directory of free browser games. Our catalog covers action, adventure, arcade, board, card, clicker, driving, .io, multiplayer, puzzle, shooting, simulation, sports, strategy, trivia, and word games — hundreds of titles and growing every day. Every game runs directly in your web browser on desktop and on phones, with no download, no installation, and no account required. You open a game page, press play, and you are playing within seconds.

We built the site to be fast and simple: a clean catalog, honest descriptions, and games that actually work. There are no fake download buttons and no forced registrations standing between you and the game.

02

Who runs it

GameHubStar is run by an independent developer, not a company. The site is a one-person project, built and maintained in spare time out of a genuine love of browser games. That independence is deliberate: there are no investors to please and no sponsors deciding which games get featured. Every decision about the catalog is made by the person who also tests the games, writes the reviews, and answers the mail.

03

How games are chosen

Every game on GameHubStar is actually played before it is listed. We open each title on desktop and on a phone, check that it loads correctly, that the controls respond, and that the game can be finished or enjoyed without errors. Games with broken embeds, unplayable controls, or pages that hijack navigation are rejected and never listed. Our reviews and tips are written from that real playtesting, not copied from store descriptions, so what you read on a game page is what you will actually experience when you press play.

There are no paid placements and no sponsored rankings. A game earns its place by being fun and working well. If a listed game stops working, we remove it or mark it for desktop only, and readers are always welcome to report problems through our Contact page.

04

Free to play

All games on GameHubStar are free to play. There are no accounts, no subscriptions, and no paywalls. Games are delivered through GameMonetize, a platform for HTML5 game developers, and the site is supported by the advertising shown around the games. That is the whole business model: free games for players, and a share of advertising revenue that keeps the catalog growing.

05

Get in touch

Questions, suggestions, a broken game to report, or a game of your own to submit — we would like to hear from you. Visit our Contact page for the email address and details on what to include. We read every message and reply as quickly as a one-person team can.
