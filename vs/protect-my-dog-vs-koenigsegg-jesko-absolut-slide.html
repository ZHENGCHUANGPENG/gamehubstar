Protect My Dog vs Koenigsegg Jesko Absolut Slide: Which is Better?

Two puzzle games, two very different kinds of thinking. Protect My Dog asks you to draw one clever line to save a dog from bees, while Koenigsegg Jesko Absolut Slide asks you to rebuild a photograph of a silver hypercar, tile by tile. Both are free puzzle games on GameHubStar, both work on desktop and on phones, and both were play-tested before they were listed — but they suit different moods, different players, and different lengths of session.

01

How Protect My Dog plays

Protect My Dog opens on a bright cartoon scene: a blue sky with white clouds, green hills and grey mountains, a yellow sun in the top left, and the title in bubble letters with a smiling dog face tucked inside the letter O. A bee sits by the title and a pencil on the right, and below them are a green PLAY button and a yellow LEVEL button, with a gear icon for settings.

The job is simple to understand and quick to start: bees are coming for the dog, and you draw a defensive line to keep them away. The whole game is a single gesture — on a computer you draw with the mouse, on a phone with a fingertip. The line has to be drawn before the bees arrive, not after, and a closed shape protects better than an open stroke, so each level is a small sketching puzzle: where should the line go, and how much of the dog should it cover? Levels are short, the idea never changes, and the fun is in finding a shape that works on the first try.

02

How Koenigsegg Jesko Absolut Slide plays

Koenigsegg Jesko Absolut Slide opens on a clean white screen with two choices and nothing else competing for attention. First pick a difficulty — 3x3, 4x4 or 5x5, with 4x4 highlighted when the screen first opens — then pick one of three real photographs of the silver Koenigsegg Jesko Absolut: the car from the front on a road, from the rear at speed, or in a dark studio. Tapping a photo opens it full-screen with a single large play button in the middle.

The photo is then cut into tiles, the tiles are shuffled, and the job is to slide them back into place. It is the classic sliding puzzle, done without fuss: a speaker icon mutes the sound, a fullscreen icon stretches the puzzle across the display, and the finished picture is the only guide to where each piece belongs. Starting on 3x3 teaches how the pieces slide before the bigger grids, and working the edges first, then filling in the middle of the car, is the way most players solve it.

03

Controls and devices

Both games play the same way on desktop and on phones, and both are landscape games, so rotate your phone sideways for the full view. Protect My Dog is built around one gesture — draw the line with a mouse or a fingertip — while the slide puzzle is built around clicks and taps: pick a difficulty tile, pick a photo, press the blue play button, then drag or tap tiles to slide them. Neither game needs a keyboard, and neither asks you to learn a control scheme before the first level.

04

Head to head

Pace. Protect My Dog is fast — each level is a single drawing that takes seconds. The slide puzzle is slow — a 5x5 grid is a quiet, methodical job that takes minutes.

Skill. Protect My Dog rewards imagination — any shape can work if it covers the dog. The slide puzzle rewards patience and planning — every tile has exactly one right place.

Replay value. Protect My Dog replays through its level list, with a new sketching problem each time. The slide puzzle replays through nine combinations — three photos times three grid sizes — so the same car can be solved three ways.

Best for. Protect My Dog suits short breaks, younger players, and anyone who wants a creative answer rather than a correct one. The slide puzzle suits car fans, calm evenings, and players who like a puzzle with a definite finished picture.

Ratings. Our listing rates Koenigsegg Jesko Absolut Slide 8.0 and Protect My Dog 7.5 — close, and both earned their scores in play-testing: the slide puzzle’s title screen, three photos and 4x4 grid were checked on desktop and on a phone held sideways, and Protect My Dog’s menu, PLAY and LEVEL buttons and drawing controls were checked the same way.

05

The verdict

There is no overall winner — the better game is the one that matches your mood. Pick Protect My Dog if you want a quick, playful puzzle you can finish in a spare minute, with a creative answer every level. Pick Koenigsegg Jesko Absolut Slide if you want a slower, calmer puzzle with a real hypercar waiting at the end, and a difficulty setting that grows with you. If you only play one and you like cars, take the slide puzzle — its 8.0 rating and nine photo-and-grid combinations give it the longer life. If you only have a minute, take the dog.
